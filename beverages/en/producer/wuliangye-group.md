# Sichuan Yibin Wuliangye Group Co., Ltd.

Sichuan Yibin Wuliangye Group Co., Ltd. is based in Yibin, Sichuan, and is a large corporate group whose main business is [baijiu](ref:baijiu). Public information shows that the group was restructured in August 1998 from the Yibin Wuliangye Distillery, and its listed arm is Yibin Wuliangye Co., Ltd. To understand the company, separate the group, the listed company, the brand and the [specific product](ref:wuliangye-drink).

## Origin and site

Yibin lies in southern Sichuan, where the Min and Jinsha rivers meet before joining the Yangtze. The humid valley climate and relatively mild swings suit open qu making and long pit fermentation. Local water, airborne microbes and grain supply form the environment for [strong-aroma baijiu](ref:strong-aroma). Within the wider map of [China](ref:china)'s baijiu regions, Yibin is an important strong-aroma node.

Both the registered address and the site are in Yibin, but "the company is in Yibin" does not mean every bottle is filled in one plant. Product lines, production bases and contract arrangements should be checked against the production address on the label. Regional advantage is not one factor but climate, water, grain, pits and long practice accumulated together.

## Business and layers

The group's main business is wine and spirit production and sales, with supporting packaging, logistics and glass operations. Support and main business reinforce each other and can lower packaging and transport costs. The exact business mix and revenue shares change by year, so this producer does not list figures; readers should use the company's periodic reports.

The layers work like this: the group holds industrial resources and the brand system, the listed company produces and sells and discloses, the brand carries the name and product lines, and the product carries strength, volume and price. The group is not limited to one aroma type or one position, so when discussing "Wuliangye", first make clear whether the subject is the company, the brand or a bottle.

## History

The common public account of the early history is: in the early 1950s, several traditional distilling workshops in Yibin were combined into a state-run distillery; in 1959 it was formally named the Yibin Wuliangye Distillery; in 1998 it was restructured into the group company, and the listed arm was listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in the same year. Sources differ slightly on the number of workshops and exact years; this producer follows the more common public account and avoids over-precise claims on uncertain details.

"Old pit" and "old method" in historical narrative need separating: continuous use of a pit has physical and process meaning, while "old method" is often a general phrase; for a specific product, the label and standard still govern. Company history is not product history, and brand age is not the age of the liquid in the bottle.

## Common misconceptions

First, conflating the group with the listed company. Their legal identities and disclosure duties differ.
Second, treating the registered place as the origin of every product. Use the production address on the label.
Third, equating company history with product age. Brand age and liquid age are different things.
Fourth, assuming diversification means the baijiu main business has weakened. Support industries and the main business can run in parallel.
Fifth, replacing public disclosure with promotional language. The two have different natures and different tests.

Wuliangye Group is a baijiu group whose listed platform is Wuliangye Yibin. Group, listed company, brand and product are not interchangeable. The group’s business is not limited to baijiu, and the listed company’s financial statements describe a different perimeter from the brand’s marketing. The multi-grain strong-aroma process is a production tradition, not a guarantee that every product uses the same recipe.

Sichuan Yibin Wuliangye Group Co., Ltd. is best approached through the range it makes, then the site and the owner, and then the bottler named on the label. One check alone will not settle it.
