# Jiannanchun

Jiannanchun is a strong-aroma baijiu brand from Mianzhu, Sichuan, held by Sichuan Jiannanchun (Group) Co., Ltd. Three things come up whenever the brand is discussed: the Tang-dynasty name "Jiannan Shaochun", the old pits of Tianyi Laohao, and the presence of Crystal Jiannanchun in the 400-yuan price band. They belong to historical narrative, archaeological evidence and product structure respectively, and none can substitute for the others. Keeping the three layers apart explains why the brand carries both the weight of a famous liquor and the repeated adjustments of a high-end challenger.

## Mianzhu and the Jiannan circuit

Mianzhu sits on the northwestern edge of the Chengdu plain, backed by the Longmen Mountains, with river and groundwater conditions suited to brewing. The Tang dynasty established the Jiannan circuit, which included Mianzhu, so "Jiannan" became a regional label; "chun" was often used in Tang liquor names and may relate to the rhythm of winter brewing and spring maturation. The name preserves that association, but today's trademark rights and producer are determined by registration.

## Tianyi Laohao: a site still in use

Tianyi Laohao is part of the Jiannanchun distillery site. Public material says the site begins in the Southern Qi period and that the old craft has been passed down for more than 1500 years. The most closely watched feature is a group of eleven pits built in the early Kangxi reign of the Qing dynasty, still in use today. In 2004 the Jiannanchun distillery site was selected as one of China's top ten archaeological discoveries of the year and was listed as a nationally protected cultural relic. For the brand this is not a "year advertisement" but a checkable record of pits, workshops and continuous production.

## 2008: craft enters national intangible heritage

In 2008 the traditional brewing craft of Jiannanchun was entered in the second national intangible cultural heritage list. The craft is based on the traditional Mianzhu Daqu method and includes more than 200 production steps; its core is solid-state fermentation of grain in mud pits, continuous mash recycling, mixed steaming and long storage. Reading "intangible heritage" as recognition of craft transmission is more accurate than treating it as a quality grade.

## The product ladder

Public product material lists Jinjiannan, Crystal Jiannanchun, the Zhencang grade and Dongfanghong 1949. Jinjiannan serves everyday banquets, Crystal Jiannanchun is a widely distributed core item, Zhencang sits higher, and Dongfanghong 1949 targets high-end gifting and collecting. Strength, volume and packaging differ clearly between series, so the bottle label should decide what is inside.

## Price bands and occasions

Jinjiannan sits roughly at 100-200 yuan for everyday banquets; Crystal Jiannanchun commonly at 400-500 yuan as the main business-gift item; Zhencang at 700-900 yuan; and Dongfanghong 1949 can exceed 1500 yuan. The structure shows that the brand's centre of gravity remains the upper-mid market rather than the ultra-premium. Prices move with channel and promotion, and one low price is not long-term positioning.

## Packaging and recognition

Crystal Jiannanchun's clear bottle, red-and-gold label and square gift box create strong visual recognition; Zhencang and Dongfanghong 1949 use heavier bottles, book-style boxes and period symbols to mark the step up. Similar names and counterfeit packaging exist, so checking producer, anti-counterfeiting marks, batch and execution standard is more reliable than bottle shape.

## Channels and market

Jiannanchun is strongest in Sichuan and expands into major drinking provinces such as Henan, Shandong, Jiangsu and Hebei, mainly through dealers, terminal stores and banquet group purchases. Competition in the upper-mid band is intense: Crystal Jiannanchun faces both national famous liquors and regional rivals at the same price. The brand's historical assets are strong, but its product structure depends heavily on one core item.

## Historical points

In September 1996 Sichuan Jiannanchun (Group) Co., Ltd. was registered. In 2003 it completed a restructuring in which state capital withdrew and management and employees took shares. In 2012 employee shareholders stopped work for 77 days over an equity change, a governance dispute in the brand's history. Read together, 1996, 2003 and 2012 show how ownership change affected the brand and its channels.

## Honours and controversy

Jiannanchun has received the National Famous Liquor title at national tasting competitions, is one of the six golden flowers of Sichuan liquor, and its traditional brewing craft is national intangible cultural heritage. Controversy has three strands. First, the 2003 restructuring and employee-share arrangements left a long dispute, and the 2012 stoppage is still discussed. Second, the brand relies heavily on Crystal Jiannanchun, while high-end products still need time to build stable recognition. Third, the upper-mid band is prone to price inversion and channel inventory pressure.

## Reading a bottle

First identify the series: Jinjiannan, Crystal Jiannanchun, Zhencang or Dongfanghong 1949. Then check strength and volume, with 52% vol and 500 mL common. Next read ingredients and the execution standard to confirm a multi-grain strong-aroma spirit. Finally check producer, batch and anti-counterfeiting marks. A bottle that says only "Jiannanchun" without a series is giving incomplete information.

## Common misconceptions

First, do not conflate the brand with the group. Second, the Tang "Jiannan Shaochun" record is not the modern founding year. Third, Crystal Jiannanchun is not the whole brand. Fourth, "vintage" wording is not the age of the liquid. Fifth, price is not a quality grade.
