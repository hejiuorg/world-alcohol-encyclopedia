# Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Group Co., Ltd.

Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Group Co., Ltd. is based in Xinghuacun, Fenyang, Shanxi, and is a wholly state-owned company. Public information shows that it was restructured in March 2002 from the former Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu (Group) Company. Its listed arm is Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Distillery Co., Ltd., stock code 600809. Group, listed company, brand and [specific product](ref:fenjiu) are four layers that must be checked separately.

This page records producer identity: name, registered place, founding time, organisational form and public entry point. The group handles state-owned asset operation and industrial management, while the joint-stock company handles production, sales and disclosure. The names are similar, but the legal entities and financial scope differ, so group statements cannot be applied directly to the listed company's reports.

In public material, "Fenjiu Group" usually means the parent group described here, while "Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Distillery Co., Ltd." is the listed entity. Figures on output, revenue and channels should follow the joint-stock company's periodic reports.

## Relationship with the listed company

Official information shows that Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Distillery Co., Ltd. was restructured in December 1993 and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in January 1994. The company describes itself publicly as "China's first baijiu stock"; that is a company statement and can be checked against exchange records.

The joint-stock company's main business is Fenjiu, Zhuyeqing and series products. The group also has industrial investment and support businesses whose structure changes by year. The two sets of figures must not be mixed, especially when comparing years and growth rates.

## Origin and environment

Fenyang lies in central Shanxi with a temperate continental monsoon climate: distinct seasons and limited rainfall. The groundwater and loess environment around Xinghuacun suit separated jar fermentation. Within the wider map of [China](ref:china)'s baijiu regions, Fenyang is an important light-aroma node, contrasting with Sichuan strong aroma and Guizhou sauce aroma.

The "light" in light aroma is both a process requirement and a flavour direction. Jar fermentation separates the mash from soil and reduces the influence of pit microbes, so the aroma is cleaner and fresher. Regional tradition and archaeological finds are often mentioned, but specific dates and interpretations of objects need archaeological reports; this page does not turn legend into dated fact.

## Raw materials and process position

Fenjiu uses sorghum as the raw material and barley and peas for [qu making](ref:qu-making). "Qingzheng erciqing" means the raw material and mash are steamed separately, with two fermentations and two distillations, emphasising the word "clean": clean steaming, clean distillation, jars and clean fermentation. This separation reduces sources of off-flavour and explains why light aroma is assessed differently from strong aroma.

The qu raw materials and culture temperature shape the aroma direction, while jar cleanliness and temperature control affect fermentation stability. These are company processes with limited public detail; what consumers can check directly are the ingredient list, standard and strength on the label.

## Products and brand layers

Besides Fenjiu, the group also has liqueur products such as Zhuyeqing. Under the Fenjiu brand there are series such as Qinghua, Laobaifen and Bofen, and products split further by strength, volume and packaging. The brand answers what it is called, the company answers who makes it, and the product answers what is in the bottle.

[Baijiu](ref:baijiu) is the broad category to which Fenjiu belongs, with light aroma as its main style. Mixing group history, brand age and product age is one of the most common errors in reading baijiu information.

## Common misconceptions

First, conflating the group with the joint-stock company. Their legal identities and disclosure duties differ.
Second, treating "China's first baijiu stock" as a third-party conclusion. It is a company statement.
Third, equating archaeological finds with the origin of modern process. Each needs separate argument.
Fourth, assuming light aroma means "no aroma". Light aroma stresses cleanliness and freshness, not absence.
Fifth, judging quality from packaging or price. Labels, standards and channels are more reliable.
