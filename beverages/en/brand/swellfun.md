# Swellfun

Swellfun is a high-end strong-aroma baijiu brand under [Sichuan Swellfun Co., Ltd.](ref:producer), launched in 2000. The brand is built on the Shuijingjie distillery site, and its products include Jingcui, Diancang, Jingtai and Zhenniang Bahao.![Swellfun category image](/media/photos/drink-style-light-aroma-baijiu-1c3af236.jpg)

## Brand definition

For Swellfun, a brand and a producer are two different entities. The brand answers "what is this bottle called and whom is it for"; the producer answers "who owns the trademark, who organises production and who bears legal responsibility". This page records the brand layer: name, product line, positioning and marks; see the producer entry for corporate history and ownership.

"Swellfun" is at once the company name, the brand name and a product name. A question such as "what does Swellfun taste like" needs a specific series first, because one brand covers products of different years, strengths, volumes and price bands.

## Owning producer

The brand's producer is [Sichuan Swellfun Co., Ltd.](ref:producer). The company listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in December 1996; its parent is Sichuan Chengdu Swellfun Group Co., Ltd., and Diageo plc is the actual controller. Ownership of the brand and trademark, the organisation of production and disclosure rest with that company and its subsidiaries.

The producer entry records the listing, foreign control and product-line information. This page keeps only what relates directly to the brand, so corporate history and brand history are not conflated.

## Product line

Public product material lists the Diyifang, Jingcui, Diancang, Jingtai and Zhenniang Bahao series. Diyifang and Jingcui sit higher, Diancang and Jingtai serve business and gifting, and Zhenniang Bahao targets a broader consumer group.

Strength, volume, packaging and price change by year and channel, and some products have regional or festive versions. Buyers should check the brand, product name, strength, net content, ingredients, producer and standard on the label.

On the product side, Jingcui's published price is 1699 yuan, aimed directly at the super-premium band; Diyifang, Diancang, Jingtai and Zhenniang Bahao serve image, business, gifting and broader consumer roles. Strengths, volumes and packs differ, so comparisons should first align series and specification.

## Positioning and price band

The brand is positioned in the high and super-high end, covering business banquets, gifting, collecting and banquet settings. Prices change with year, channel, promotions and pack size, so the Jingcui figure above is a single publicly reported price and should not be read as a permanent retail price.

A price band is market positioning, not a quality grade. Judging the liquid means looking at raw materials, process, storage time and label information, not price or packaging alone.

## History

In 2000 the company launched the Swellfun brand on the basis of the preserved distillery site, entering the market with a high-end product priced above the common industry level of the time. The brand makes the Shuijingjie distillery site central to its narrative, stressing the historical continuity of the "first workshop of Chinese baijiu".

Public information from the Swellfun Museum shows that the Shuijingjie distillery site is a national key cultural heritage protection unit, was listed among the 1999 top-ten national archaeological discoveries, and that the related brewing technique is national intangible cultural heritage. These designations concern the heritage and craft value of the site and technique, not a product quality certification.

"Launched in 2000" and the archaeological and conservation milestones of the site belong to different time scales; the continuous production age of an ancient workshop cannot be converted into the founding year of a modern brand.

The site's timeline is more specific: in 1998 the distillery found buried brewing remains while rebuilding a workshop and carried out excavations; in 1999 the site entered the national top-ten archaeological discoveries; in June 2001 it was designated a national key cultural heritage protection unit (fifth batch); and in 2008 the traditional Shuijingjie brewing technique entered the second national intangible cultural heritage list. These milestones show that the brand narrative rests on a site and a craft, not on a slogan.

## Name and marks

"Shuijingjie" was originally the name of a distilling workshop on Shuijing Street in Chengdu, and the brand carries the place and workshop name. The name points to the historic site and origin, but trademark ownership and production are determined by registration.

For Swellfun, trademarks, bottle shapes, colours and label layout belong to the brand's mark system, but counterfeit products can imitate the look. Buyers should check the producer, anti-counterfeiting marks, batch and official channel rather than bottle shape or dominant colour alone.

## Common misconceptions

First, conflating the brand with the joint-stock company. The brand is a market mark; the company is a legal entity.
Second, treating "Swellfun" as a single product. The brand has several series, including Jingcui, Diancang and Jingtai.
Third, treating the continuous production age of the distillery site as the founding year of the modern brand. They are different time scales.
Fourth, treating cultural-heritage protection or intangible-heritage status as product quality certification. They concern the site and the technique.
Fifth, treating price as a quality grade. Price is market positioning, not a process standard.

Swellfun is best read by starting with the product line, moving to the producer named on the label, and ending with the market it is sold in.
