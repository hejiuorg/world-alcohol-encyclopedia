# Artworks

- [Brouwer's The Drinker: A Tavern and a Face](brouwer-the-drinker.md) — A tavern genre scene by the Flemish painter Adriaen Brouwer, represented here by the Mauritshuis's Drunken Peasants at an Inn and often discussed under titles such as The Drinker. Drink, tobacco and noise strip everyone of sobriety; the picture is both comic observation and a cool look at human weakness.
- [Caravaggio's Bacchus: A Glass of Wine and a Basket of Fruit](caravaggio-bacchus.md) — Caravaggio's Bacchus, painted around 1598 and now in the Uffizi Galleries in Florence, shows a young wine god offering a glass to the viewer beside a minutely painted basket of fruit. It reads both as an invitation and as a puzzle about drunkenness and classical models.
- [An Interior with a Woman Drinking and Two Men: A Glass of Wine in Delft](de-hooch-woman-drinking.md) — Pieter de Hooch's interior, painted around 1658 and now in the National Gallery, London, shows a woman with her back to us holding a glass while two men use pipes as instruments. The picture stays open between singing, drinking and suggestion, and infrared imaging reveals a figure the painter removed.
- [The Night Revels of Han Xizai: a banquet turned into a painting](han-xizai-night-banquet.md) — A silk handscroll attributed to Gu Hongzhong of the Southern Tang, 28.7 by 335.5 cm, in the Palace Museum, Beijing, graded first class A. The page reads the five scenes of listening, dancing, resting, piping and farewell, explains how wine warmers and cups pace the night, and reviews the Song-copy and surveillance debates.
- [Heda's Still Life with Drinking Vessels: A Roemer and a Watch](heda-still-life-drinking-vessels.md) — A still life by the Dutch painter Willem Claesz. Heda, represented here by the Mauritshuis's Still Life with a Roemer and Watch. A large drinking glass, silver, food and a watch are painted in a near-monochrome light; the picture is both a study of drinking vessels and a still-life tradition about time and moderation.
- [The Lantingji Xu: a floating-cup gathering and a preface](lanting-ji-xu.md) — On the third day of the third month in 353, Wang Xizhi and his companions held a purification gathering at the Orchid Pavilion, drank and wrote poems, and Wang composed a preface. The original is lost; the Shenlong copy, ink on paper, 24.5 by 69.9 cm, is in the Palace Museum, Beijing. The page reads the text, the calligraphy, the floating-cup rule and the copy debate.
- [Li Bai Drunk: a subject painted again and again](li-bai-zui-jiu-tu.md) — Starting from Su Liupeng's Li Bai Drunk, a hanging scroll of 1844 in the Shanghai Museum (ink and colour on paper, 204.8 by 93.9 cm), the page traces the subject across centuries and distinguishes it from Liang Kai's Li Bai Walking and Reciting in Tokyo and a Boston scroll of Li Bai drinking with Emperor Minghuang.
- [Along the River During the Qingming Festival: taverns and urban rhythm in Bianjing](qingming-shanghe.md) — Zhang Zeduan's Northern Song handscroll, 24.8 by 528.7 cm, in the Palace Museum, Beijing, object number 00087177. The page uses the Sun Yang shop, the Shiqian retail outlet and the Rainbow Bridge to explain the hierarchy of breweries and wine shops, and reviews the disputes over the meaning of Qingming, the season and the scroll's completeness.
- [The Seven Sages of the Bamboo Grove and Rong Qiqi: a brick relief in a Southern Dynasties tomb](seven-sages-of-bamboo-grove.md) — A moulded brick relief excavated in 1960 from a Southern Dynasties tomb at Xishanqiao, Nanjing. The Nanjing Museum's two groups measure 78 by 242.5 cm and 78 by 241.5 cm, with name labels beside the figures. The page reads the eight poses, the place of wine among them, and the disputes over tomb date, patron and lost model.
- [Velázquez's The Triumph of Bacchus: Bringing Myth into the Crowd](velazquez-triumph-of-bacchus.md) — Velázquez's painting of about 1628–1629, now in the Museo del Prado, places Bacchus among a group of Spanish peasants. Cups, crowning and drinking bring myth down to everyday life; the picture is at once a parody of the classical figure and a new telling of Bacchic culture.
- [Vermeer's The Glass of Wine: A Drink and a Window](vermeer-wine-glass.md) — Vermeer's interior, painted around 1658–1660 and now in the Gemäldegalerie in Berlin, shows a woman at a table holding a glass while a man pours from a jug. The picture places drinking, hospitality and Dutch Golden Age domestic life in the same window light.
- [The Eight Immortals of the Wine Cup: eight drunks in one painting](yin-zhong-ba-xian-tu.md) — Built around two verifiable versions, one in the National Palace Museum, Taipei, traditionally attributed to Ren Renfa, and one in the Metropolitan Museum of Art, dated Ming or Qing; the page reads Du Fu's poem, the eight drinkers, the colophons and the false signatures that complicate the subject.

- [The Wedding at Cana: A Wall-Sized Feast with a Miracle at the Edge](veronese-wedding-at-cana.md) — Veronese's 1562–1563 canvas for the refectory of San Giorgio Maggiore, now at the Louvre, turns the Gospel miracle of water into wine into a Venetian banquet; the wine moves through servants, cupbearers and guests.
- [The Absinthe Drinker: Manet Puts a Marginal Man on a History-Painting Scale](manet-absinthe-drinker.md) — Manet's early canvas of about 1859, now at the Ny Carlsberg Glyptotek, shows a single cloaked man, an empty bottle at his feet and a small glass on a ledge; rejected by the 1859 Salon, it became a founding image of modern-life painting.
- [L'Absinthe: Degas Paints Two People Sitting Together and Looking Past Each Other](degas-absinthe.md) — Degas's canvas of 1875–1876, originally titled In a Café, hangs in the Musée d'Orsay; an actress and a painter share a Paris café table with a glass of absinthe, and the subject is the solitude of the modern city.
- [The Night Café: Van Gogh Paints a Gaslit Room as an All-Night Fever](van-gogh-night-cafe.md) — Van Gogh's September 1888 canvas at the Yale University Art Gallery shows an all-night café in Arles: red walls, a green billiard table, four lemon-yellow gas lamps and exhausted patrons.
- [The Peasant Wedding: Bruegel Paints a Village Feast as a Cross-Section of Society](bruegel-peasant-wedding.md) — Bruegel's 1568 panel in Vienna shows a Flemish peasant wedding: the bride before a green cloth, the bridegroom absent by custom, porridge carried on a door and beer poured from a large jug.
- [Literary Gathering: Emperor Huizong Paints a Scholars' Dinner as an Ideal of Civil Order](wen-hui-tu.md) — Traditionally attributed to Song emperor Huizong, this ink-and-colour silk scroll in Taipei shows a garden gathering of scholars, with wine cups, tea ware and fruit on a large table.
- [The Eighteen Scholars: A Tang Political Portrait Turned into Four Scenes of Literati Life](eighteen-scholars.md) — A set of four hanging scrolls in Taipei, formerly labelled Song but now often dated to the Ming, depicts the Eighteen Scholars of Tang Taizong in garden gatherings organised around the qin, chess, calligraphy and painting.
