# Meng Zhi Lan M9

Meng Zhi Lan M9 is a high-end product in [Yanghe](ref:brand)'s Blue Classic series and belongs to [strong-aroma baijiu](ref:strong-aroma). Official information lists 500 mL with strengths including 38% ABV, 40.8% ABV and 52% ABV, from Suqian, Jiangsu. M9 is a product code, not a vintage and not a grade certificate.

## Definition and position

Meng Zhi Lan is the series name, and M9 is a product code within it. The series also includes M3 and M6, with price and body design rising in that order. Treating "M9" as a year or as the "highest grade" mistakes a code for an assessment system.

The product belongs to the strong-aroma route of [baijiu](ref:baijiu), and the brand publicly describes it as "mellow-soft". Mellow-soft means a soft entry and smooth body, not a synonym for low strength or thin flavour; different strengths of the same product also differ clearly.

## Producer and brand

The product is made within the [group](ref:producer) system, the brand is carried by [Yanghe](ref:brand), and the listed company handles production and disclosure. Group, joint-stock company, brand and product are four layers with different data scopes.

When you see "M9 revenue", establish whether it is the listed company's product scope or a channel estimate; when you see a "new version", establish whether it is a packaging upgrade, a body adjustment or a channel exclusive.

## Origin and raw materials

Suqian lies in northern Jiangsu in the Hongze Lake basin, with a warm-temperate monsoon climate, distinct seasons and moderate rainfall. The area is rich in waterways, and water and soil suit distilling. Within the map of [China](ref:china)'s baijiu regions, Suqian is an important node of Jianghuai strong aroma.

The main materials are [sorghum](ref:sorghum) and other grains with wheat qu, using solid-state mud-pit fermentation and continued mash recycling. The microbial composition in [qu making](ref:qu-making) affects the aroma direction, and the grain ratio affects body structure.

## Brewing process

Strong aroma uses solid-state mud-pit fermentation, continued mash recycling and mixed steaming and boiling, followed by fractional cutting, graded storage and blending. Company material stresses low-temperature slow fermentation and longer fermentation cycles, which relate to the "mellow-soft" direction.

Exact fermentation parameters, base-spirit ratios and storage times are company process with limited public detail. What consumers can check directly are the ingredient list, standard, strength and volume on the label; style description is a sensory judgement, not a measurable index.

## Specification and label

Official information lists 500 mL as the common volume, with strengths including 38% ABV, 40.8% ABV and 52% ABV. The product standard for strong-aroma baijiu is commonly GB/T 10781.1, and the bottle label governs.

The producer, product name, aroma type, strength, volume, standard and anti-counterfeit information on the label are the basics for identity. Buy through official channels and check anti-counterfeit marks and batches rather than judging from the letters "M9" or the blue packaging.

## Flavour and tasting

The aroma is led by pit, grain and sweet notes, with a softer entry, a smooth body and a clean finish. At 52% ABV the heat is stronger, while 38% ABV is softer; strength affects entry and aroma release but does not establish a quality ranking.

For Meng Zhi Lan M9, taste from a small cup: smell at rest, then gently swirl and smell again. Let the liquid stay in the mouth and note the order of sweet, sour, bitter and savoury and the body thickness. Water lowers heat and opens aroma but changes balance, so add drop by drop and compare.

## Buying and storage

Prefer official channels and check anti-counterfeit marks, producer and batch. Gift boxes and channel editions should be checked against the label, and packaging differences should not be mistaken for body differences. For banquet orders, confirm batches in advance to avoid mixing.

For Meng Zhi Lan M9, unopened baijiu should be stored upright, away from light, in a cool place without strong odours. Once opened, drink it soon; prolonged exposure loses aroma. Home storage cannot reproduce distillery conditions, so purchase year should not be equated with quality gain.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating "M9" as a year or grade. It is a product code.
Second, treating "mellow-soft" as low strength or thin. It is a style direction, not an alcohol statement.
Third, assuming higher strength is more authentic. Strength serves product design.
Fourth, treating gift packaging as a body upgrade. Edition differences need official explanation.
Fifth, judging authenticity from price or packaging. Anti-counterfeit checks, channels and labels are more reliable.
