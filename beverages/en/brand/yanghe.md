# Yanghe

Yanghe is a [strong-aroma baijiu](ref:strong-aroma) brand within the [Jiangsu Yanghe Group Co., Ltd.](ref:producer) system, from Suqian, Jiangsu. The brand is known for its "mellow-soft" style and Blue Classic series, with lines including Hai Zhi Lan, Tian Zhi Lan and Meng Zhi Lan. Brand, series and product are three layers, and a purchase should be checked layer by layer.![Yanghe category image](/media/photos/drink-style-light-aroma-baijiu-1c3af236.jpg)

## Definition and position

Yanghe is first a brand name and belongs to the strong-aroma route of [baijiu](ref:baijiu). It is not the same concept as "Yanghe Group" or "Yanghe Co., Ltd.": the group and the company are legal entities, the brand is a name and product system, and the product is a particular bottle.

"Mellow-soft" is the company's public description of a style direction, meaning a softer entry and a smoother body, not a synonym for low strength or thin flavour. Reading mellow-soft as "no flavour" mistakes a style description for a quality judgement.

## Relationship with the producer

The brand is held within the [group](ref:producer) system, while the listed company handles production and disclosure. The group handles resources and industrial layout, the listed company handles production and sales, the brand handles names and series, and the product handles strength, volume and price. Scopes differ and data must not be mixed.

The group system also includes brands such as Shuanggou. Shuanggou is an independent brand, not named "Yanghe"; it should be kept separate from Yanghe's product lines rather than treated as another series of the same brand.

## Brand and product system

Blue Classic is the core series, split into Hai Zhi Lan, Tian Zhi Lan and Meng Zhi Lan, with price and body design rising in that order; Meng Zhi Lan itself divides into products such as M3, M6 and M9. Yanghe Daqu and other products cover more everyday occasions. Each series differs in base-spirit selection, storage and blending ratio.

A series name is not proof of grade, and price is not quality. Check the specific product name, producer, aroma type, strength and volume on the bottle rather than only "Blue Classic" or the packaging colour. Gift boxes and channel editions should also be checked against the label.

## Origin and raw materials

Suqian lies in northern Jiangsu in the Hongze Lake basin, with a warm-temperate monsoon climate, distinct seasons and moderate rainfall. The area is rich in waterways, and water and soil suit distilling. Within the map of [China](ref:china)'s baijiu regions, Suqian is an important node of Jianghuai strong aroma.

The main materials are sorghum and other grains with wheat qu, using solid-state mud-pit fermentation and continued mash recycling. The microbial composition of [qu making](ref:qu-making) affects the aroma direction, and the grain ratio affects body structure. The ingredient list and standard are checkable.

## Process and flavour

Strong aroma uses solid-state mud-pit fermentation, continued mash recycling and mixed steaming and boiling, followed by fractional cutting, graded storage and blending. Company material stresses low-temperature slow fermentation and longer fermentation cycles, which relate to the "mellow-soft" direction; specific parameters are company process with limited public detail.

The aroma is led by pit, grain and sweet notes, with a softer entry, a smooth body and a clean finish. Series and strengths differ clearly, and one description cannot cover all products. Mellow-soft does not mean low strength, and a high-strength product can also enter softly.

## Identity and labels

For Yanghe, the producer, product name, aroma type, strength, volume and standard on the label are the basics for identity. The product standard for strong-aroma baijiu is commonly GB/T 10781.1, but the specific product follows the bottle. Anti-counterfeit marks, batch and channel information also matter.

Counterfeits and similar names exist, and judging from packaging alone is risky. Official channels, checkable anti-counterfeit measures and clear labels are more reliable than price. Custom packaging common at weddings and business events should also be checked against the label.

## History and narrative

Common public corporate milestones are: the group was founded in May 1997, the joint-stock company was established in December 2002, and it was listed in November 2009. Earlier distilling tradition mostly belongs to local gazetteers and corporate narrative, and specific years need archival support.

"Mellow-soft" as brand language is a recent market expression and should not be projected backwards as an inherent property of ancient process. When reading historical narrative, separate checkable milestones, style description and brand rhetoric.

## Buying and drinking

The traditional serve is neat in a small cup at room temperature. At the table it suits Huaiyang cuisine, home-style stir-fries, river fish and light savoury dishes; too much heat amplifies the alcohol and too much sweetness covers the pit aroma. Banquets and everyday drinking are different occasions: the former stresses packaging and gifting, the latter value.

In mixed drinks, a high-strength product significantly changes the balance and sugar and acid need rebalancing. Ice is not traditional and lowers aroma release; for a lighter impression, choose a lower-strength product rather than a lot of ice.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating "mellow-soft" as low strength or thin. It is a style direction, not an alcohol statement.
Second, conflating group, joint-stock company and brand. The three are different layers.
Third, treating a series name as a vintage. A series name is not the age of the liquid.
Fourth, treating series products as "inferior". They are official product lines.
Fifth, judging authenticity from packaging or price. Anti-counterfeit checks, channels and labels are more reliable.
