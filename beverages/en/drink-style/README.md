# Drink styles

- [Abbey beer](abbey-beer.md) — An umbrella term for beers brewed by monasteries or brewed commercially under a monastery name and licence; Trappist beer must be brewed inside a Trappist monastery under monastic supervision, while abbey beer is mostly a licensed commercial product, and dubbel, tripel and quadrupel denote strength tiers rather than separate styles.
- [Absinthe](absinthe.md) — A high-strength spirit built on wormwood, fennel and anise and made by maceration and distillation; EU flavouring law sets 35 mg/kg and 10 mg/kg thujone ceilings, and the clouding on dilution is physics, not proof of hallucination.

- [Altbier](altbier.md) — A traditional top-fermented beer of Düsseldorf, copper-brown with medium-high hop bitterness and clean malt; cold-condit…
- [Amaretto](amaretto.md) — An Italian almond-flavoured sweet liqueur whose name means “slightly bitter”; often infused with almonds, apricot kernel…
- [Amaro](amaro.md) — The Italian family of herbal bitter liqueurs, made by macerating or distilling herbs, roots, bark and citrus and blending with syrup; usually 11% to 30% ABV, bittersweet, and used both as a digestif and as a mixing ingredient in Negroni, Manhattan variations and highballs.
- [Aperitif](aperitif.md) — A pre-meal drinks category defined by occasion rather than raw material, usually built around bitterness, dryness or freshness.
- [Aquavit](aquavit.md) — A Nordic spirit flavoured with caraway or dill, clear or amber from oak ageing; it belongs to the Christmas table and snaps culture rather than to the vodka family.
- [Armagnac](armagnac.md) — A grape brandy from Gascony, protected since 1936, traditionally made in a continuous still that gives roughly 52% to 60% ABV spirit with a broad, weighty aroma; it is divided into Bas-Armagnac, Ténarèze and Haut-Armagnac and keeps a strong vintage tradition.
- [Awamori](awamori.md) — An Okinawan rice spirit made from rice and black koji, with all the rice turned into koji and distilled once; legally a single-distilled shochu, usually bottled near 30% ABV and aged into kusu for three years or more.

- [Baijiu](baijiu.md) — A traditional Chinese distilled spirit made mainly from grains, saccharified and fermented with qu or yeast cultures, then distilled, aged and blended; aroma family is a common lens, not the only one.
- [Medicinal liquor](baijiu-medicinal.md) — A Chinese infused liquor made with a distilled or yellow-wine base and food-medicine ingredients; it is food, not medicine.
- [Baltic Porter](baltic-porter.md) — A dark high-gravity beer brewed with lager yeast around the Baltic, with dark chocolate, caramel, dried fruit and liquor…
- [Barleywine](barleywine.md) — A very strong British ale defined by high malt gravity, 8%–12% alcohol, a full body and bottle-ageing potential, split into English and American approaches.
- [Beer](beer.md) — A low-to-moderate-strength drink made by mashing and fermenting cereal, usually with hops, spanning a vast family of styles.
- [Bitters](bitters.md) — A concentrated alcoholic flavouring made from bitter herbs, roots, bark and citrus, usually 35% to 45% ABV and used by the dash or a few millilitres; it carries the legacy of nineteenth-century medicine and is the salt-and-pepper seasoning of the Old Fashioned, Manhattan and Negroni.
- [Blended Scotch Whisky](blended-scotch.md) — A Scotch whisky made by combining single malt from one or more distilleries with single grain from one or more distilleries; it sits alongside blended malt and blended grain as three distinct legal names, with grain providing the frame and malt providing the character.
- [Bock](bock.md) — A malt-driven strong lager from Einbeck that Munich brewers reshaped into a dark Bavarian specialty, spanning traditional bock, doppelbock, maibock and eisbock.
- [Bourbon Whiskey](bourbon.md) — An American whiskey category made mainly from corn and matured in new charred oak; a 51% corn minimum, distillation and barrel-entry limits, and the straight-ageing rules define its legal boundary.
- [Bourbon whiskey](bourbon-whiskey.md) — A page about the name and its market use: how bourbon whiskey layers spelling, geographical indications and label vocabulary on one category, and where it borders Tennessee, rye and blended bourbon.

- [Brandy](brandy.md) — A spirit distilled from wine or other fruit wine, including Cognac, Armagnac, Calvados and grape-pomace brandies.
- [Brandy de Jerez](brandy-de-jerez.md) — A brandy from Jerez, Spain, distilled from wine and aged in oak that held Sherry, using a solera system; vanilla, dried …
- [Cachaça](cachaca.md) — A Brazilian spirit distilled from fermented sugarcane juice at 38–48% ABV, legally distinct from molasses-based rum; its categories run from white and gold to barrel-aged, Premium and Extra Premium.

- [Calvados](calvados.md) — A Norman apple brandy distilled from cider and perry, protected by appellation since 1942; Pays d'Auge uses double pot distillation while other zones usually use a column, and at least two years in oak take the spirit from green apple to baked apple, vanilla and spice.

- [Chi aroma baijiu](chi-aroma-baijiu.md) — A baijiu aroma style from the Pearl River Delta, made from rice with xiaoqu semi-solid fermentation and fat immersion, giving a clean sweet profile with a distinct fatty note; GB/T 10781.5-2021 applies.
- [Cider](cider.md) — A fermented apple-juice drink; apple tannin and acidity build the frame, wild yeast, malolactic fermentation and residual sugar decide the style, and UK duty treats cider from 1.2% to 8.5% vol as its own class.
- [Cognac](cognac.md) — A grape brandy from the Cognac region of France, protected by appellation rules since 1936: Ugni Blanc dominates, the Charentais pot still distils twice, and VS, VSOP, XO and XXO carry legal minimum ages.
- [Compounded liquor](compounded-liquor.md) — A beverage made by blending, mixing or reprocessing a base of fermented wine, distilled spirit or food-grade alcohol with edible materials or additives; lujiu is a major branch, and the category is not a quality grade.
- [Corn Whiskey](corn-whiskey.md) — American whiskey made mainly from corn, legally at least 80% corn; it may be unaged or only briefly aged, with corn swee…
- [Cream Ale](cream-ale.md) — A pale American ale, often with corn or rice to lighten the malt body, crisp with fine foam and low bitterness; “cream” …
- [Crème de Cassis](creme-de-cassis.md) — A deep purple blackcurrant liqueur made by macerating fruit in neutral spirit and sweetening it; Dijon is its historic centre, and its high sugar and high acid make it the soul of Kir and Kir Royale.
- [Cyser](cyser.md) — A mead fermented from honey and apple juice, between cider and mead; apple, honey, spice and acidity, with styles from d…
- [Daiginjo sake](daiginjo-sake.md) — A named sake category with a polishing ratio of 50% or less and ginjo fermentation; brewing alcohol may be added within the legal cap, and the no-alcohol version is junmai daiginjo. High polishing concentrates aroma but raises broken-grain losses and cost.
- [Dessert Wine](dessert-wine.md) — A broad family of wines defined by residual sugar rather than a single regulation, covering botrytised, late-harvest, dried-grape, ice and fortified sweet styles; the source of the sugar, the supporting acidity and the serving temperature decide whether the result is balanced.
- [Dong aroma baijiu](dong-aroma-baijiu.md) — Also called medicinal aroma, represented by Dongjiu in Guizhou; it combines large and small qu, adds herbs to qu making and co-distils an aromatic mash with the grain mash, with local standard DB52/T 550-2013.
- [English ale](english-ale.md) — An umbrella term for traditional British top-fermented beers, covering bitter, pale ale, English IPA, porter and stout, with an emphasis on malt-hop balance, lower carbonation and cask real ale serving.
- [Feng-aroma baijiu](feng-aroma-baijiu.md) — A baijiu aroma type made from sorghum, barley, peas and wheat with daqu, through mixed steaming and distillation in loess mud pits, solid-state fermentation, solid-state distillation, jiuhai storage and blending; the current standard is GB/T 10781.6-2025, with 25.0%–68.0% ABV.
- [Fortified wine](fortified-wine.md) — Wine to which grape spirit is added during or after fermentation, raising strength and stabilising flavour; Port, Sherry, Madeira and Vermouth each have their own processes and rules.
- [Fruit Beer](fruit-beer.md) — An umbrella for beers fermented, infused or flavoured with fruit; from Belgian fruit lambic to German fruit wheat beer a…
- [Fruit Brandy / Eau-de-vie](fruit-brandy.md) — Clear spirits distilled from fermented fruit, generally called eau-de-vie; made from pear, cherry, plum, raspberry and m…
- [Fruit wine](fruit-wine.md) — A low-to-moderate-strength drink made by fermenting fruit juice, distinct from fruit liqueur and fruit brandy.
- [Fu-aroma baijiu](fu-aroma-baijiu.md) — A baijiu aroma type made from grain with both xiaoqu and daqu, through mud-pit solid-state fermentation, clean steaming and mixing, ageing and blending, with a composite 'strong aroma first, light aroma mid-palate, sauce aroma last' profile; the current standard is GB/T 10781.11-2021, with 25.0%–68.0% ABV.
- [Genever](genever.md) — A grain spirit flavoured with juniper from the Netherlands, Belgium and the northern French border, protected as an EU geographical indication in 2008; oude and jonge describe recipe styles rather than age, and its malt-wine body contrasts sharply with dry English gin.
- [Gin](gin.md) — A distilled spirit led by juniper, divided by production method and additives into London dry, distilled and compound gin.
- [Ginjo sake](ginjo-sake.md) — A named sake category requiring a polishing ratio of 60% or less and cool, long ginjo fermentation, with brewing alcohol capped at 10% of the polished rice weight; apple, banana and pear esters define the aroma.
- [Grappa](grappa.md) — An Italian pomace brandy whose name is protected as an EU geographical indication; the grapes must be grown and vinified in Italy and the spirit distilled there, with a 37.5% ABV minimum, a clear young style and a wood-aged style after at least 12 months.
- [Gueuze](gueuze.md) — A traditional Brussels beer made by blending lambic of different ages and refermenting it in the bottle; young beer supplies sugar and vigour, old beer supplies acidity and complexity, and the result is dry, finely sparkling and marked by barnyard, citrus and green-apple notes.
- [Highland Barley Wine](highland-barley-wine.md) — The Tibetan Plateau's family of drinks made from highland barley; everyday chang is a cloudy low-alcohol ferment, while the distilled qingke liquor of Huzhu in Qinghai has a geographical indication and a "four-times-clear" technique.
- [Honey Wine](honey-wine.md) — An alcoholic drink fermented from honey and water; honey is nitrogen-poor, acidic and very concentrated in sugar, so fermentation differs from wine, with alcohol from about 3% to over 18% and sweetness set by residual sugar.
- [Honjozo sake](honjozo-sake.md) — A tokutei meisho sake category with a polishing cap of 70%, a koji-rice share of at least 15%, and permission to add no more than 10% brewing alcohol by weight of white rice; usually cleaner and crisper than junmai, but honjozo is not a quality grade.
- [Ice Cider](ice-cider.md) — A sweet cider originating in Quebec, Canada, fermented from naturally frozen or cryo-concentrated apple juice; high in a…
- [Ice Wine](ice-wine.md) — A sweet wine made from grapes that freeze naturally on the vine and are pressed while frozen; German Eiswein and Canadian Icewine apply different harvest-temperature rules, Riesling and Vidal are common varieties, and yields are tiny while sugar and acidity are highly concentrated.
- [Imperial Stout](imperial-stout.md) — The strongest branch of the stout family, descended from the high-alcohol, heavily hopped black beer that Britain exported to Russia in the 18th century; its boundaries are deep roasted grain, a full body, alcohol warmth and long ageing potential, with modern barrel-aged variants.
- [India pale ale](ipa.md) — A top-fermented beer family defined by prominent hop aroma and bitterness, rooted in British export trade to India and now split into English, American and hazy branches, where bitterness is a variable rather than a grade.
- [Irish whiskey](irish-whiskey.md) — Whiskey distilled and matured on the island of Ireland from cereal grains, aged at least three years in wooden casks and bottled at no less than 40% ABV; it is a protected EU geographical indication with four main categories.
- [Japanese whisky](japanese-whisky.md) — Whisky mashed, fermented, distilled, matured and bottled in Japan; since 2021 an industry labelling standard has defined the name through raw materials, three years in wood and a 40% ABV bottling minimum.
- [Junmai sake](junmai-sake.md) — A sake category made only from rice, rice koji and water, with no added brewing alcohol. Junmai ginjo and junmai daiginjo also carry 60% and 50% polishing caps.
- [Kölsch](kolsch.md) — Cologne's pale top-fermented beer, cold-conditioned and filtered, marked by clean fruit, soft bitterness and service in 0.2-litre Stangen, with a geographically protected name.
- [Lager](lager.md) — An umbrella term for beers made with cool bottom-fermenting yeast and then stored near freezing; pale lager, dark lager and Pilsner all belong to it, and lager is a process family while Pilsner is a specific style.
- [Lambic](lambic.md) — A traditional beer of Belgium's Pajottenland and Senne valley, fermented spontaneously by wild yeast and bacteria; it uses a turbid mash, aged hops and long oak ageing, and is often blended from young and old lambic into gueuze, with cherry and raspberry fruit versions.
- [Laobaigan-aroma baijiu](laobaigan-aroma-baijiu.md) — A baijiu aroma style centred on Hengshui in Hebei; GB/T 10781.10-2024, in force from 2025-12-01, fully replaces GB/T 20825-2007, and ground-jar fermentation with separate steaming of grain and mash is the usual process description.
- [Light aroma baijiu](light-aroma-baijiu.md) — A baijiu aroma style led by ethyl acetate, made with separate steaming and earthen-jar fermentation for a clean, crisp profile; GB/T 10781.2-2022 applies, and aroma style is not a quality grade.
- [Limoncello](limoncello.md) — A bright yellow southern Italian liqueur made by steeping lemon peel in neutral spirit and blending it with sugar syrup; it takes its aroma from peel oil rather than juice and is classically served ice-cold after dinner.
- [Liqueur](liqueur.md) — A sweetened spirit flavoured with fruit, herbs, nuts, cream or coffee; bitters are a separate, less sweet category.
- [London dry gin](london-dry-gin.md) — The most representative dry gin style, with flavour required to come from redistribution, juniper dominant and sugar and colouring restricted; the name is a process category, not proof of origin.
- [Low-alcohol / Non-alcoholic Beer](low-alcohol-beer.md) — An umbrella for beers with limited or removed alcohol, made by halted fermentation, vacuum distillation, reverse osmosis…
- [Lujiu](lujiu.md) — A Chinese beverage made from a base of distilled spirit, fermented wine or food-grade alcohol with edible or medicinal-and-food materials, blended or reprocessed; GB/T 27588-2011 applies, and lujiu is not a medicinal liquor.
- [Madeira](madeira-wine.md) — A fortified wine from the Portuguese island of Madeira, shaped by heat through estufagem or by slow cask ageing in warm attics known as canteiro; styles run from dry Sercial to rich Malmsey, with acidity as the constant.

- [Mezcal](mezcal.md) — A Mexican agave spirit made by pit roasting and pot distillation; protected regions, agave variety and still type shape the style, while NOM-070-SCFI-2016 sets a 35% to 55% alcohol range and the ageing classes.
- [Mixed aroma baijiu](mixed-aroma-baijiu.md) — A baijiu aroma style that combines strong aroma and sauce aroma characters, represented by Baiyunbian and Kouzijiao; GB/T 23547-2009 applies, and aroma style is not a quality grade.

- [Old Tom Gin](old-tom-gin.md) — A sweetened gin style popular in eighteenth- and nineteenth-century England, made by adding sugar or sweet botanicals such as liquorice after distillation; it is not a legal category, and modern revivals usually sit at 40% to 45% ABV as the historical base for Tom Collins and Old Fashioned variations.
- [Other Drinks (Category Note)](other.md) — An explanatory container for marginal drinks that do not fit neatly under wine, beer, distilled spirits or compounded drinks; not a specific style and not a new legal category.
- [Ouzo](ouzo.md) — Greece's anise spirit, defined by aniseed, distillation and the milky cloud that appears when water is added; the EU protects the name as a geographical indication, with a minimum strength of 37.5% ABV.

- [Pale Ale](pale-ale.md) — A family of pale-malt, top-fermented beers with clearly readable hop aroma; English versions lean on malt and floral hops, American versions on citrus and resinous hops, forming a continuum with bitter and IPA rather than three isolated boxes.
- [Perry](perry.md) — A low-alcohol drink fermented from pear juice; English perry from the Three Counties is known for high-tannin pear varieties and a bittersweet structure, and it is not the same as apple cider with pear flavouring.
- [Pilsner](pilsner.md) — A pale lager born in Plzeň, Czechia, defined by very soft water, Saaz hops, pale malt and cool bottom fermentation, and central to understanding modern pale lagers.
- [Pisco](pisco.md) — A grape spirit distilled from freshly fermented juice in Peru and Chile, with separate protected appellations and production rules: Peru requires a single distillation, no added water, sugar or oak, while Chile permits oak maturation and sets strength tiers.
- [Plymouth Gin](plymouth-gin.md) — A gin style identified with the English city of Plymouth and produced at the Black Friars distillery; softer and earthier than London Dry, the standard bottle is 41.2% ABV and Navy Strength is 57% ABV, and it is a historical base for Pink Gin and the dry Martini.
- [Port](port.md) — A sweet fortified wine from Portugal's Douro Valley, made by adding grape spirit mid-fermentation and bottled at about 19%–20% ABV, with styles ranging from Ruby and Tawny to Vintage, LBV, Colheita and Crusted.
- [Porter](porter.md) — A dark top-fermented beer that arose in 18th-century London, given roast, coffee and cocoa flavours by brown malt and later roasted malt; named after market porters and related to stout, though not the same, with modern robust and Baltic porter branches.
- [Pulque](pulque.md) — A low-alcohol drink from central Mexico made by fermenting agave sap (aguamiel), usually 4% to 6% ABV; it is neither distilled nor aged, tastes sour, viscous and yeasty, and is served in pulquerías as white pulque or fruit-flavoured curado.

- [Rauchbier](rauchbier.md) — A German beer built on beechwood-smoked malt, best preserved in Bamberg, with smoke intensity running from delicate to intense across a spectrum rather than a fixed recipe.
- [Red wine](red-wine.md) — Wine made by fermenting dark-skinned grapes with their skins to extract colour and tannin; colour, tannin, acidity and alcohol together shape the style, not a vintage or regional label alone.
- [Rhum Agricole](rhum-agricole.md) — A rum distilled from fresh sugarcane juice in Martinique, Guadeloupe and other French Caribbean territories, protected by the Martinique AOC since 1996; it skips molasses, tastes of grass, olive, banana and citrus, and its vieux expression ages at least 3 years in oak.
- [Rice-aroma baijiu](rice-aroma-baijiu.md) — A baijiu aroma type made mainly from rice with xiaoqu, through semi-solid fermentation, distillation, ageing and blending, with ethyl lactate and beta-phenylethanol as the main aroma compounds; the current standard is GB/T 10781.3-2025, with a unified 25.0%–68.0% ABV.
- [Rice Wine](rice-wine.md) — A low-alcohol fermented drink made in China from glutinous rice with a sweet-rice starter, usually seen as jiuniang or laozao; it is sweet, cloudy and weak, and is neither huangjiu nor baijiu.
- [Rosé Wine](rose-wine.md) — A wine made from red grapes with brief skin contact or direct pressing; colour comes from the skins, not sweetness, and OIV residual-sugar thresholds divide dry to sweet into four bands, while Provence, Tavel and rosé Champagne point in different directions.
- [Rum](rum.md) — A spirit distilled from sugarcane juice or molasses, with white, aged, dark and agricole styles.
- [Rye Whiskey](rye-whiskey.md) — A whiskey built on rye as its main grain and matured in new charred oak; US rules require at least 51% rye and straight rye needs two years, while Canada treats Canadian Rye Whisky as the same standard as Canadian Whisky, with no minimum rye share.
- [Saison](saison.md) — A farmhouse ale brewed in the cool season in Wallonia and kept for summer farm work; it is defined by high attenuation, a dry finish and peppery, citrusy yeast aroma, and modern versions range from low-alcohol everyday beers to strong saisons above 8%.
- [Sake](sake.md) — A Japanese fermented rice drink made with rice, water, koji and yeast, classified by polishing ratio and brewing method.

- [Sauce-aroma baijiu](sauce-aroma-baijiu.md) — A baijiu style known for high-temperature qu, high-temperature stacking, stone pits with mud bottoms and multi-round fermentation. Style is not a quality grade.
- [Sauternes](sauternes.md) — The botrytised sweet white of southern Bordeaux, based on Sémillon with Sauvignon Blanc and Muscadelle; autumn fog from the Ciron river feeds noble rot, while the 1855 classification and the unique status of d'Yquem shaped its reputation.
- [Scotch whisky](scotch-whisky.md) — Whisky produced in Scotland under the Scotch Whisky Regulations 2009, in five categories: single malt, single grain, blended malt, blended grain and blended Scotch; it must mature in oak for at least three years, bottle at no less than 40% ABV and distil at below 94.8% ABV.

- [Sesame-aroma baijiu](sesame-aroma-baijiu.md) — A baijiu aroma type made mainly from grain, optionally with wheat bran, using daqu and fuqu as saccharifying and fermenting agents, with stacking, solid-state fermentation, solid-state distillation, ageing and blending; the current standard is GB/T 10781.9-2021, with high-strength spirit at 40%–68% ABV.
- [Sherry](sherry.md) — A fortified wine from the Jerez region of Spain, based mainly on Palomino and shaped either by biological ageing under flor (Fino, Manzanilla) or oxidative ageing (Oloroso, Amontillado, PX), then blended through the solera system.
- [Japanese shochu](shochu.md) — A Japanese distilled spirit under the liquor tax law, split into continuously distilled class A (under 36%) and single-distilled class B (up to 45%); honkaku shochu is made from rice, barley, sweet potato or other materials through koji, two-stage mashing and pot distillation, commonly 25% ABV, and is distinct from both Korean soju and Okinawan awamori.
- [Single Malt Scotch Whisky](single-malt-scotch.md) — A whisky distilled at one Scottish distillery from 100% malted barley in pot stills and matured in oak for at least three years; the 94.8% distillation ceiling and the 40% bottling floor mark its boundary from single-cask and cask-strength releases.
- [Single Pot Still Whiskey](single-pot-still-whisky.md) — An Irish whiskey category distilled in pot stills from a mash of malted and unmalted barley; one distillery, a 30% to 30% raw-material split, a 3-year minimum and a 94.8% distillation ceiling define it.
- [Sloe Gin](sloe-gin.md) — A traditional British gin liqueur, made by steeping sloe berries in gin with sugar; deep red, sweet-tart with almond and…
- [Soju](soju.md) — A Korean spirit ranging from traditional distilled soju to modern diluted soju, distinct from Japanese shochu.
- [Sotol](sotol.md) — A distilled spirit from northern Mexico made from desert spoon (Dasylirion), with a denomination of origin covering Chihuahua, Coahuila and Durango; protected in 2002, made under NOM-159-SCFI-2004 by pit roasting, fermentation and pot distillation, usually at 38% to 45% ABV.
- [Sour beer](sour-beer.md) — A category of beers given marked acidity by lactic or acetic fermentation; Belgian lambic and red-brown ales, German Berliner Weisse and Gose each form a tradition, and modern kettle souring makes production more controllable, with acidity a style axis rather than a defect or a grade.
- [Sparkling wine](sparkling-wine.md) — Wine containing carbon dioxide of fermentative origin that keeps bubbling after opening; traditional method, tank method and ancestral method are distinct, and Champagne is only one example.

- [Stout](stout.md) — A top-fermented dark beer given colour and coffee-cocoa flavour by roasted barley, named after stronger porter, represented by Dublin dry stout and split into sweet, oatmeal and imperial stout.
- [Strong-aroma baijiu](strong-aroma-baijiu.md) — A baijiu style associated with mud-pit solid-state fermentation, continuous grain topping and mixed steaming. Style is not a quality score.
- [Te-aroma baijiu](te-aroma-baijiu.md) — A baijiu aroma type made mainly from rice, with daqu cultured from wheat flour, wheat bran and distillers' grains, fermented in red conglomerate stone pits, then solid-state distilled, aged and blended; the current standard is GB/T 10781.7-2025, with 25.0%–68.0% ABV.
- [Tennessee whiskey](tennessee-whiskey.md) — A Tennessee-produced American whiskey with a corn-led mash, new charred oak maturation and maple-charcoal filtration before ageing, often labelled straight.
- [Tequila](tequila.md) — A Mexican spirit distilled from blue Weber agave, with 100% agave and mixto expressions and several age tiers.
- [Tokaji](tokaji.md) — The botrytised sweet wine system of Hungary's Tokaj region, built around Aszú; since 2013 it is labelled by grams per litre of residual sugar, Furmint and Hárslevelű supply the high-acid frame, and the range runs from Eszencia to Szamorodni.
- [Trappist Beer](trappist-beer.md) — Beer that must be brewed inside a Trappist monastery by monks or under their supervision, with proceeds going to the monastery and social work; only six breweries may currently use the Authentic Trappist Product mark, and their styles range from dubbel and tripel to quadrupel and dark strong ale.
- [Triple Sec](triple-sec.md) — A clear orange liqueur made by macerating sweet and bitter orange peels in neutral spirit, distilling and sweetening; it is a style, not a brand, and its border with curaçao lies in base spirit, colour and sweetness.
