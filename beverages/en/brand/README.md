# Brands

[返回English目录](../README.md)

- [Absolut](absolut.md) — A Swedish vodka brand now owned by Pernod Ricard, sold on single-origin production, winter wheat and continuous distillation, and known for its clear bottle, minimal label and long-running art collaborations.
- [Baiyunbian](baiyunbian.md) — A mixed-aroma baijiu brand from Songzi, Hubei, named after a line by Li Bai; known for a strong-head, sauce-tail style, with vintage, era and star series, and core vintages at fifteen, twenty and thirty years.
- [Changyu](changyu.md) — The wine brand of Yantai Changyu Pioneer Wine Co., Ltd.; the company was founded by Zhang Bishi in 1892, and the brand spans Jiebaina, chateau wines, ice wine and brandy.
- [Chivas Regal](chivas-regal.md) — A Scotch blended whisky brand operated by Chivas Brothers under Pernod Ricard, known for its 12-year and 18-year age statements and a smooth blending style, and one of the representative names in premium blended Scotch.
- [Daohuaxiang](daohuaxiang.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu brand from Yichang, Hubei, under Daohuaxiang Group; its name comes from a line by Xin Qiji, with Qingyang as the premium flagship, the Huoli family for banquets, Zhenpin for traditional channels and Shouchaojian as a soy-aroma experiment.
- [Dongjiu](dongjiu.md) — A Dong-aroma (also called medicinal-aroma) baijiu brand from Donggongsi, Zunyi, Guizhou, held by Guizhou Dongjiu Co., Ltd. Its process uses herbs in the qu, separate large and small qu, and twin cellars, with key products including Guomi Dongjiu and Guomi Baicaoxiang.
- [Fenjiu](fenjiu.md) — A light-aroma baijiu brand within the Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Group system, with series including Qinghua, Laobaifen and Bofen. This page covers brand structure, not the product page's flavour detail.
- [Greatwall](greatwall.md) — A domestic Chinese wine brand owned within COFCO and operated by COFCO Greatwall Wine Co., Ltd., built around star-rated series, estate wine and regional wines and covering everyday table wine to premium gifting; the producer and origin of a specific bottle should be checked on its label.
- [Guinness](guinness.md) — A beer brand originating in Dublin, Ireland and now owned by Diageo, known for its dark stout, roasted barley flavour and nitrogen-conditioned foam, and one of the defining names in the stout style.
- [Gujing Gongjiu](gujing-gongjiu.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu brand from Bozhou, Anhui, known for its "Nianfen Yuanjiang" series and products across several strengths.
- [Hennessy](hennessy.md) — A French Cognac brand operated by Moët Hennessy under LVMH, known for its VS, VSOP and XO grades and long-matured blended eaux-de-vie, and one of the defining names of the Cognac region.
- [Red Star](hongxing.md) — A light-aroma baijiu brand under Beijing Red Star Co., Ltd., built around the er-guotou cut-the-head-and-tail craft; the blue-bottle mellow series and green-bottle traditional series form the everyday core, with Red Star Gaozhao for gifting and commemoration.
- [Huanghelou](huanghelou.md) — A Wuhan baijiu brand held by Huanghelou Distillery, built around Southern School Great Light Aroma while also offering Chenxiang, Ecological Original Pulp, the Lou series and the strong-aroma Gengshang Cenglou; it covers banquets, gifting and upper-mid-market occasions.
- [Kuaijishan](huijishan.md) — A Shaoxing huangjiu brand of Kuaijishan Shaoxing Rice Wine Co., Ltd., originating in the 1743 Yunji winery in Dongpu and brewed from glutinous rice, wheat qu and Jian Lake water; the range includes five-year aged wine, Lanting and Shuixiang Guose within the Shaoxing geographical-indication system.
- [Jiannanchun](jiannanchun.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu brand from Mianzhu, Sichuan, held by Sichuan Jiannanchun (Group) Co., Ltd.; it is known for multi-grain strong aroma and the Tianyi Laohao pit complex, with key items including Crystal Jiannanchun, the Zhencang grade and Dongfanghong 1949.
- [Jiugui](jiuguijiu.md) — A fuyu-aroma baijiu brand under Jiugui Liquor Co., Ltd., made in Jishou, Xiangxi; Huang Yongyu named it and designed the sack-shaped bottle in 1987, and the range includes Neican, Jiugui and Xiangquan, known for three aromas in one sip and a multi-grain process.
- [Johnnie Walker](johnnie-walker.md) — A blended Scotch whisky brand originating in Scotland and now owned by Diageo, known for its square bottle and colour-coded Red, Black, Green, Gold and Blue Label range, from everyday mixing to high-end gifting.
- [Kouzijiao](kouzijiao.md) — A Jianxiang-aroma baijiu brand from Suixi, Huaibei, Anhui, known for a one-step Jianxiang process using multiple qu types and grains; the range covers the Kouzijiao vintage series, Lao Kouzi, Kouzifang and Kouzijiu, and the company listed in 2015.
- [Langjiu](langjiu.md) — A sauce-aroma baijiu brand from Erlang, Gulin, Sichuan, on the left bank of the Chishui River, held by Sichuan Langjiu Group Co., Ltd.; its main line includes Qinghua Lang, Hongyun Lang, Honghua Lang and Langpai Lang, with jianxiang products such as Langpai Tequ, Shunpin Lang and Xiaolangjiu.
- [Hengshui Laobaigan](laobaigan.md) — A Laobaigan-aroma baijiu brand under Hebei Hengshui Laobaigan Liquor Co., Ltd.; the company originates from the state-run Hengshui distillery formed in 1946 and listed in 2002.
- [Luzhou Laojiao](luzhou-laojiao.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu brand within the Luzhou Laojiao Group system, with core products including Guojiao 1573 and Luzhou Laojiao Tequ. The name relates to pit age, but the name is not the age of the liquid.
- [Niulanshan](niulanshan.md) — A baijiu brand of Beijing Shunxin Agriculture's Niulanshan Distillery, centred on er-guotou craft and the mass price band, with lines spanning light-aroma er-guotou, strong-aroma Niulanshan Chenniang and Bainian Niulanshan.
- [Shede](shed.md) — A high-end strong-aroma baijiu brand under Shede Spirits, launched in December 2001; positioned around the Shede cultural idea and aged-liquor reserves, with Pinwei Shede, Zhihui Shede, collectible Shede and crystal Shede in the range.
- [Suntory](suntory.md) — A Japanese beverage and alcohol brand whose drinks portfolio covers Japanese whisky, beer and spirits; its global spirits business has operated under the Beam Suntory name.
- [Swellfun](swellfun.md) — A high-end strong-aroma baijiu brand under Sichuan Swellfun Co., Ltd., launched in 2000. Built on the Shuijingjie distillery site, with products including Jingcui, Diancang, Jingtai and Zhenniang Bahao.
- [Taipai](taipai.md) — A Shaoxing huangjiu brand operated by Zhejiang Taipai Shaoxing Rice Wine Co., Ltd., built around handmade winter brewing, aged series and export packaging; it draws on the Shaoxing geographical indication and intangible-heritage narrative and spans everyday drinking to gifting.
- [Tsingtao Beer](tsingtao.md) — A beer brand originating in Qingdao in 1903 and held by Tsingtao Brewery Company Limited. Its main products are lagers, spanning the Classic 1903, pure draft, white beer and Augerta series.
- [Wuliangye](wuliangye.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu brand under Sichuan Yibin Wuliangye Group, named for five grains such as sorghum, rice, glutinous rice, wheat and corn. This page covers brand structure, not the product page's flavour detail.
- [Xifengjiu](xifeng.md) — A feng-aroma baijiu brand from Fengxiang, Shaanxi, held by Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Group Co., Ltd.; its range covers Hong Xifeng, Zhencang, Jiuhai Chencang, the old green bottle, Huashan Lun Jian and Guohua Ci, from mass-market liquor to premium gifting.
- [Xijiu](xijiu.md) — A sauce-aroma baijiu brand from Xishui, Guizhou, held by Guizhou Xijiu Co., Ltd. Its plant sits by the Erlangtan stretch of the middle Chishui River, and key products include Junpin Xijiu and Xijiu Jiaocang 1988.
- [Yanghe](yanghe.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu brand within the Jiangsu Yanghe Group system, known for its "mellow-soft" style and Blue Classic series, including Hai Zhi Lan, Tian Zhi Lan and Meng Zhi Lan.
- [Yingjia Gongjiu](yingjia-gongjiu.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu brand from Huoshan, Anhui, publicly positioned as the Chinese people's welcoming liquor, built around ecological brewing and cave storage; the range covers Master Edition, Yingjia Dongcang, Gold and Silver Star, Bainian Yingjia and Yingjia Gufang.
