The page states that the album is one of the best-selling records by an Italian artist worldwide, with more than twenty million copies sold. The venue is Piazza San Marco, not a theatre interior.

## How to attend

Seats are divided into “Plateatico” and “Archi”, the terrace and the arcade, at 200 euros per person. Each table has two to four seats depending on position; depending on the number booked, guests may share a table with others. Booking is through Caffè Florian's official channel, and the procedure is governed by the booking terms on the event page.

This rule matters for understanding Venetian square cafés: the seat itself is the unit of pricing, not merely “buy a drink and sit”. The 200-euro booking fee and the price of an everyday coffee follow two different logics; the former buys a view, a guaranteed seat and a service period. Third-party resale is not advisable, because refunds and shared-table rules are tied to the original booking.
