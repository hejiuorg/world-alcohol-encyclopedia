# Yingjia Gongjiu

Yingjia Gongjiu starts its story in Foziling, a small town in Huoshan county on the northern edge of the Dabie Mountains. The brand belongs to Anhui Yingjia Distillery Co., Ltd. and is sold as a strong-aroma baijiu with one of the clearest origin stories in the Hui liquor belt: mountain water, local grain, a reservoir valley and two caves used for storing base spirit. Its public slogan, "the Chinese people's welcoming liquor", places the brand in banquet and gifting settings rather than in connoisseur language.

Where many Anhui brands put the word "year" at the centre of their marketing, Yingjia puts "ecology" and "cave storage" there instead. Those two words explain most of the brand's appeal, and they also produce most of the confusion around it.![Yingjia Gongjiu category image](/media/photos/drink-style-light-aroma-baijiu-1c3af236.jpg)

## Origin: Foziling and the East Pi River

Huoshan lies in western Anhui, below the Dabie Mountains. The Foziling reservoir and the East Pi River shape the local water system, and the company locates its plant and cave-storage base in this landscape. Origin information matters because it identifies the general source of water, grain and storage conditions. It does not, by itself, establish a quality grade.

## Ecology is a position, not a certification

For years the company has used "ecological brewing" as its public position. In October 2018 the China Ecological Baijiu Research Institute was established at Yingjia, and the company began presenting water protection, grain bases, qu making, fermentation pits and storage as one system. The distinction that matters for a buyer is simple: "ecological" is a corporate position and a marketing term, not a state-defined grade for baijiu. What can be checked is the standard, ingredients, strength, net content and producer on the label, together with concrete designations such as National Geographical Indication Protected Product.

## The product ladder

The public product centre lists several families: Yingjia Gongjiu Master Edition, Yingjia Dongcang, Yingjia Gold and Silver Star, Bainian Yingjia and Yingjia Gufang. The Dongcang family is itself tiered into Dongcang 6, Dongcang 9, Dongcang 16 and Dongcang 20. Dongcang 20 is commonly offered at 52% vol and 42% vol in a 500 mL bottle, with water, sorghum, rice, glutinous rice, wheat and maize declared as ingredients. The multi-grain recipe is typical of Jianghuai strong-aroma practice and is intended to give a fuller aroma and a softer, sweeter palate.

## Price bands and occasions

The range runs roughly across four levels: everyday bottles and light-gift products below 100 yuan, banquet and county-channel products at 100-300 yuan, the main Dongcang range at 300-800 yuan, and the Master Edition and image products above 1000 yuan. A price band is positioning, not a quality grade. The same series can differ sharply between provinces, outlets and promotion cycles, so the specific product name, strength and batch matter more than the word "Dongcang" alone.

## Cave storage is a method, not an age grade

The company's cave base uses the Huangyan and Qingtan caves, which public material describes as staying at about 20℃ and 85% humidity through the year. The purpose is to let base spirit mature slowly in a stable, humid environment. This describes a storage environment, not a legal age category. There is no unified national standard for "cave-stored for X years", so Dongcang 6 and Dongcang 20 should be read as product names and tier markers, not as a guarantee that every drop in the bottle is exactly that old.

## Packaging and recognition

The main products use red, gold and dark gift boxes, while the Dongcang line relies on mountain motifs, cave-storage lettering and a heavier bottle to signal maturation. Packaging is part of brand recognition, but counterfeiters can copy colours and bottle shapes. Checking the producer name, anti-counterfeiting mark, batch code and sales channel is more reliable than judging by appearance.

## Channels and market

The company says its products sell in more than 20 provinces, municipalities and autonomous regions, but the core remains Anhui, especially banquet and group-purchase channels in Hefei, Lu'an and Fuyang. Outside the province it focuses on nearby markets such as Jiangsu, Shanghai and Zhejiang. The model is distributor-led, supported by terminal displays, banquet policies and festival gifting. That model is efficient in a regional market, but it also makes price order and channel inventory important.

## Historical points

Anhui Yingjia Distillery Co., Ltd. was founded on 28 November 2003. On 28 May 2015 it listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange, becoming the 16th listed company in the baijiu industry. In October 2018 the China Ecological Baijiu Research Institute was established at the company, moving "ecology" from a slogan into a research and production programme. The Dongcang range then gradually took over the mid-to-high end of the portfolio.

## Honours and controversy

The company's public profile lists National Geographical Indication Protected Product and China Time-honoured Brand, and says its traditional brewing technique has been entered on the intangible cultural heritage list. Controversy tends to centre on boundaries rather than scandals. First, "cave storage" has no unified national standard and can be mistaken for an age promise. Second, "ecology" is a position rather than a certification, and shorthand can imply a quality guarantee it does not carry. Third, regional liquor companies often face channel inventory and price inversion when they push into higher price bands; this is an industry-wide pressure rather than a problem unique to one brand.

## Reading a bottle

Begin with the product name: Master Edition, Dongcang, Gold and Silver Star, Bainian Yingjia or Gufang. Then check strength and net content, since 52% vol and 42% vol taste quite different. Next look at ingredients and the execution standard to see whether it is a multi-grain strong-aroma spirit. Finally check producer, batch and channel. Only those details together establish what the bottle actually is.

## Common misconceptions

First, do not conflate the brand with the joint-stock company; the brand is a market mark and the company is a legal entity. Second, "gongjiu" is historical and brand narrative, not a modern quality grade. Third, do not take 2003 as the brand's origin; 2003 is the founding year of the joint-stock company. Fourth, "ecological" is not a quality certification. Fifth, "Dongcang 20" does not mean that all the liquid was aged for 20 years.

**At a glance:** A strong-aroma baijiu brand from Huoshan, Anhui, publicly positioned as the Chinese people's welcoming liquor, built around ecological brewing and cave storage; the range covers Master Edition, Yingjia Dongcang, Gold and Silver Star, Bainian Yingjia and Yingjia Gufang. 

Yingjia Gongjiu is best read by starting with the product line, moving to the producer named on the label, and ending with the market it is sold in.
