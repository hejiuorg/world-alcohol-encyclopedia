# Shede

Shede is a case of turning a philosophical phrase into a baijiu brand. "Shede" is an everyday expression meaning to give something up in order to gain something, and in 2001 the company registered it as a high-end strong-aroma brand. The idea was to give up part of short-term scale in exchange for aged-liquor reserves and brand premium. The brand belongs to Shede Spirits Co., Ltd. and is made in Tuopai, Shehong, Sichuan. Understanding it means looking at the cultural packaging, the old Tuopai base, and the capital episode of 2020, when Tianyang Holdings occupied company funds and Fosun eventually took control.

## Tuopai: the old distillery base

Shede Spirits is based in Tuopai, Shehong, Sichuan. Its history traces to the Tai'an workshop of 1940; a state-run Tuopai Qujiu distillery was established in 1952, the joint-stock company was registered in 1993, and it listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in 1996 under code 600702. The company owns both Tuopai and Shede: Tuopai leans mass-market and nostalgic, Shede leans high-end and cultural. The brand did not appear from nowhere; it borrowed Tuopai's old pits, base-liquor reserves and production system.

## 2001: the launch

In December 2001 the company launched Shede on the basis of Tuopai Qujiu, targeting the high-end market. At the time the high-end strong-aroma band was already occupied by Wuliangye, Luzhou Laojiao and others, so Shede entered through a cultural concept rather than simply stressing pits or vintage. That choice shaped the brand's later communication: first the values of "shede", then the liquid.

## 2018: renaming and Chinese wisdom

In January 2018 the company name changed from Sichuan Tuopai Shede Spirits Co., Ltd. to Shede Spirits Co., Ltd., binding brand and company more tightly. In March the same year Zhihui Shede was launched and the brand adopted the slogan "Chinese wisdom". The renaming is a capital-market and brand-management point, not a product launch; the two are often mixed and should be kept apart.

## 2019: the aged-liquor strategy

In 2019 the company introduced an aged-liquor strategy, making longer-stored base liquor and blending liquor the core of differentiation. In substance, the strategy converts inventory time into product narrative: both Shede and other strong-aroma spirits use multiple grains, but Shede stresses base-liquor ageing and blending ratios. "Old liquor" is not a legal grade, and how much aged base liquor a given product contains should be checked against company disclosures and the label.

## The product ladder

Public product material lists Pinwei Shede, Zhihui Shede, collectible Shede and crystal Shede. Pinwei Shede is a widely distributed core item, Zhihui Shede sits higher, and the collectible and crystal series serve gifting, collecting and banquets. Strength, volume, packaging and execution standard differ between series, so one word "Shede" cannot describe them all.

## Price bands and occasions

Pinwei Shede commonly sits at 400-500 yuan as the main business-banquet item; Zhihui Shede at roughly 600-800 yuan; and collectible Shede can exceed 1000 yuan for gifting and collecting. This price structure and the aged-liquor narrative support each other: the higher the price, the more consumers ask about base-liquor age and blending ratio. A price band is positioning, not a quality grade.

## Packaging and recognition

Shede's main visual uses a dark bottle, calligraphic type and the two characters "Shede"; Pinwei and Zhihui are distinguished by bottle shape, colour and gift box, while collectible Shede uses heavier packaging to signal scarcity. Counterfeits often copy bottle shape and colour, so buyers should check the full producer name, anti-counterfeiting marks, batch and execution standard.

## Channels and market

Shede is strongest in Sichuan and expands into Henan, Shandong, Jiangsu and Guangdong through dealers, group purchases, restaurants and e-commerce. After Fosun took control, the company increased marketing and channel investment, and upper-mid products expanded faster, but the brand became more dependent on the health of banquet and business occasions. Since 2020 the baijiu industry has entered an adjustment period, and price inversion and inventory pressure have become common.

## Historical points

1940 Tai'an workshop; 1952 state-run Tuopai Qujiu distillery; 1996 listing; December 2001 launch of Shede; January 2018 renaming and March launch of Zhihui Shede; 2019 aged-liquor strategy. On 22 September 2020 the company's shares were placed under an other-risk warning because controlling shareholder Tianyang Holdings and its affiliates had occupied 475 million yuan of funds, including 440 million yuan in principal and 35 million yuan in interest. On 31 December 2020 Yuyuan paid 4.53 billion yuan for 70% of Tuopai Shede Group, indirectly holding 29.95% of Shede Spirits, and the Fosun group took control.

## Honours and controversy

Tuopai Qujiu has received the National Famous Liquor title, its traditional brewing craft is national intangible cultural heritage, and the company also holds China Time-honoured Brand status. Controversy has three strands. First, the 475 million yuan occupation by Tianyang Holdings in 2020 led to the ST designation; although the relevant parties were later not penalised, the governance problem left a lesson. Second, "old liquor" has no unified legal definition, so consumers cannot easily verify the true age and ratio of base liquor. Third, after Fosun took control, marketing spend rose, and growth quality and channel inventory need continued watching.

## Reading a bottle

First identify the series: Pinwei Shede, Zhihui Shede, collectible Shede or crystal Shede. Then check strength and volume. Next read ingredients and the execution standard to confirm a multi-grain strong-aroma spirit. Finally check producer, batch and anti-counterfeiting marks. A bottle that says only "Shede" without a series is giving incomplete information.

## Common misconceptions

First, do not conflate the brand with the joint-stock company. Second, Shede is not a single product. Third, "old liquor" is not a legal grade. Fourth, 2001 is not the year the company was founded. Fifth, Fosun's arrival is not direct evidence of a change in product quality.

To read Shede, start with the product line, then the producer named on the label, then the market it is sold in; no single check settles it.
