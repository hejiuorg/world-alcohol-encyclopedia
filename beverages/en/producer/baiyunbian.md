# Hubei Baiyunbian Distillery Co., Ltd.

Hubei Baiyunbian Distillery is a baijiu producer in Songzi, Jingzhou, Hubei, built in 1952 and known for its strong-sauce [mixed-aroma](ref:mixed-aroma) craft. The company and the brand share the three characters "Baiyunbian", but the legal entity, ownership structure and product series sit at different levels. Three things matter here: the 1952 plant history, the 2005 ownership reform, and the company's place in the mixed-aroma standard.![Hubei Baiyunbian Distillery category image](/media/photos/drink-style-light-aroma-baijiu-1c3af236.jpg)

## Legal entity and registration

Business registration shows that Hubei Baiyunbian Distillery Co., Ltd. was established on 13 September 2007, registered in Songzi as a joint-stock company. 1952 is the plant's founding year; 2007 is the registration year of the current joint-stock company. The two do not conflict: the first is the production starting point, the second the post-reform legal entity.

It is common in baijiu for plant history to predate company registration. Company history can start in 1952, but equity and financial reporting must use the post-2007 joint-stock company. The two should not be mixed.

## The 2005 reform and current ownership

The company's "development history" page records that in November 2005, under the provincial policy for county economies, Baiyunbian carried out a property-rights reform of "state-share exit, private ownership". After the reform, the new joint-stock company separated ownership from management and formed a clear equity structure.

The company is therefore no longer solely or majority state-owned. Public material does not disclose the current shareholder list and ratios item by item, and this page does not state specific figures. What can be confirmed is that it is a private joint-stock company, with ownership and management rearranged after the reform; equity changes should be checked against business registration.

## From a Songzi distillery to a mixed-aroma representative

After 1952 the plant long operated as a local state-owned distillery. It later developed a route combining strong and sauce aroma and named the brand "Baiyunbian", from Li Bai's line "borrow the moonlight from Dongting, and sail to buy wine where the clouds are white". The poem supplies a place and a wine image, not a process description.

After reform and opening up, the company went through restructuring and expansion and became one of Hubei's representative baijiu businesses. Dates for renaming, expansion and restructuring differ between sources; this page uses only the checkable founding year, the 2005 reform and the 2007 registration.

## Participation in the mixed-aroma standard

The company says that on 1 December 2009, a strong-sauce mixed-aroma baijiu standard with Baiyunbian as first drafting unit was published. That standard is GB/T 23547-2009, which sets definitions, sensory and physicochemical requirements for mixed-aroma products.

Taking part in drafting gives the company a voice in the category, but it does not mean every product automatically meets the highest grade. A bottle is judged by its declared standard, aroma type, strength and producer. A standard is a floor, not a rank.

## Site, region and storage

The plant is in Songzi, on the south bank of the middle Yangtze, with a warm, humid subtropical monsoon climate and distinct seasons suited to multi-round [fermentation](ref:fermentation). [Hubei](ref:hubei)'s river system and grain supply provide the basics, with sorghum and wheat as main raw materials.

Mixed-aroma production usually runs both strong- and sauce-aroma routes: the strong part uses mud pits, continued grain and mixed steaming; the sauce part uses high-temperature stacking, multiple fermentation rounds and repeated extraction. Base spirits from both are combined, stored and blended. The company does not publish verifiable plant-level capacity or base-liquor inventory, so this page does not fill them in.

## Product structure from the company's view

The main brand is Baiyunbian, with products across age statements, strengths, volumes and price points, plus packaging for banquets and gifting. The vintage, era and star series form the main bands, with individual products covered on the brand page.

From the company's side, product structure is about capacity allocation, channel coverage and price-band linkage; from the consumer's side, it is about what a given price buys. The two perspectives are different and cannot replace each other.

## Channels and regional market

Baiyunbian's core market is Hubei, with Songzi and Jingzhou as the home market and Wuhan and the Jianghan Plain as priorities. Channels are mainly distributors, tobacco-and-liquor shops and banquet group purchases, with supermarkets and e-commerce as supplements. Its mixed-aroma difference gives it a stable local consumer base.

Outside the province, the brand faces national names and local competitors. Public material does not disclose provincial sales shares, and this page does not speculate. The regional character remains clear, and mixed aroma is a niche nationally.

## Capital market status

Hubei Baiyunbian Distillery is not a listed company and has no publicly traded shares. ## Common misconceptions

First, confusing the 1952 plant founding with the 2007 company registration. The first is production history, the second the legal entity.
Second, reading "private ownership" as having no state background. Post-reform equity is a matter of registration.
Third, treating standard participation as a quality grade. A standard is a floor.
Fourth, treating the vintage number as bottle age. It is usually part of the product name.
Fifth, treating mixed aroma as a simple blend. It requires a complete arrangement of two process routes.
