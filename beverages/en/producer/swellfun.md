# Sichuan Swellfun Co., Ltd.

Sichuan Swellfun Co., Ltd. is a strong-aroma baijiu producer based in Chengdu, Sichuan. The company listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange on 6 December 1996; its parent is Sichuan Chengdu Swellfun Group Co., Ltd., and Diageo plc is its actual controller. The company's core brand is Swellfun, covering price bands from high-end to mid-market.

## Site and region

Chengdu lies in the west of the Sichuan Basin, on the Chengdu Plain, with a humid subtropical monsoon climate: much cloud, high humidity and mild winters. The plain has deep soils and ample water, conditions that support pit construction and long fermentation for strong-aroma baijiu.

Sichuan is a core producing region for strong-aroma baijiu, and Yibin, Luzhou, Mianzhu, Shehong and Chengdu each have different microclimates and water and soil conditions. The humid Chengdu Plain helps keep the microbial community in the pit mud stable, but the specific flavour still depends on the pits, qu, raw materials and process control.

A site in Chengdu does not mean every product uses the same plant or the same batch of base liquor. Product lines differ in raw materials, storage time and blending, and specific information should be taken from the label and public company material.

## Brands and products

The core brand is Swellfun. Public product material lists the main series as Diyifang, Jingcui, Diancang, Jingtai and Zhenniang Bahao. Diyifang and Jingcui sit higher, Diancang and Jingtai serve business and gifting, and Zhenniang Bahao targets a broader consumer group.

Strength, volume, packaging and price change by year and channel, and some products have regional or festive versions. Buyers should check the product name, strength, net content, ingredients, producer and standard on the label rather than the series name or packaging colour.

## Craft and style

The typical strong-aroma route is: multi-grain blending, qu making, solid-state fermentation in mud pits, continued use of the fermented grain, basket distillation, fractional collection, ceramic-jar storage and blending. The mud pit is central to strong-aroma production: the microbial community in the pit mud participates in the formation of flavour compounds such as ethyl hexanoate, and the longer a pit is used continuously, the more mature the mud becomes.

Swellfun emphasises the transmission of its traditional brewing technique. Technique transmission describes a process route and cultural background but cannot replace a judgement about a specific product: products at different prices and in different series may differ in base-liquor grade, storage time and blending ratio.

## History

On 6 December 1996 the company listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange. It later entered the high-end baijiu market under the Swellfun brand. In 2006 the company reached a cooperation intention with the Diageo group, becoming an important pilot for foreign investment in the baijiu industry; at the end of 2013 Diageo wholly controlled the company's largest shareholder, Sichuan Chengdu Swellfun Group Co., Ltd., and became the actual controller.

These points describe the shift from listing to foreign control. Foreign control affects governance and international channels but does not change the production process or the label system of strong-aroma baijiu.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating Swellfun only as a brand. It is also the company name and a product name.
Second, treating the parent company as the listed company. The parent is the holding platform; the listed company handles operations and disclosure.
Third, treating foreign control as a change of process. Control affects governance and channels, not the brewing process itself.
Fourth, treating a price band as a quality grade. Price is market positioning; judging the liquid means looking at raw materials, process, storage and the label.
