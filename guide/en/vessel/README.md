# Glassware

[返回English目录](../README.md)

- [Absinthe Glass](absinthe-glass.md) — A glass with a wide bowl and a narrow rim, usually paired with a carved sugar spoon. It is used for the absinthe water ritual, where the drink turns from green to milky white.
- [Beer Mug](beer-mug.md) — A large beer vessel with a handle and thick walls, common in German beer culture. Thick walls hold the cold, the handle insulates the hand and the wide mouth invites a big draught.
- [Champagne flute](champagne-flute.md) — A tall, narrow, slightly tapered stemmed glass used for Champagne and sparkling wine; the narrow body helps retain bubbles but opens aroma less than wider shapes.
- [Copita](copita.md) — A small stemmed tulip-shaped glass used for tasting Sherry in Spain, with a gathered bowl and a slightly flared rim, typically holding about 100–150 ml; also used for nosing some spirits.
- [Coupe glass](coupe-glass.md) — A stemmed, shallow bowl-shaped glass used for Champagne, sparkling wine and short drinks served without ice; the wide rim opens the aroma but lets bubbles fade quickly.
- [Decanter](decanter.md) — A glass vessel for separating sediment and letting wine meet air, usually with a wide base, a long neck and a stopper. Decanting time depends on age, body and aroma.
- [Goblet](goblet.md) — A stemmed glass with a wide bowl and an open rim, used for Belgian abbey and Trappist ales, some wines and table water; capacities commonly run from 250 to 500 ml.
- [Highball glass](highball.md) — A straight-sided, tall, fairly large long-drink glass used for drinks over ice and sparkling cocktails; capacity has no single standard, so adjust ice and soda to your own glass.
- [Hurricane Glass](hurricane-glass.md) — A tall glass with a slender body, a bulging middle and a flared rim, shaped like an old hurricane lamp. It is used for tropical cocktails and crushed-ice drinks.
- [Julep Cup](julep-cup.md) — A small metal cup, usually silver or pewter, used for the mint julep. Metal conducts cold quickly, frosts the wall and keeps the drink icy with crushed ice and mint.
- [Martini glass](martini-glass.md) — A conical, stemmed short-drink glass with a slightly narrowed rim, also called a cocktail glass, used for the Dry Martini, Margarita and Daiquiri; capacity has no single standard and pre-chilling matters more than shape.
- [Nick and Nora glass](nick-and-nora.md) — Named after the detective couple in the 1934 film The Thin Man; a small stemmed glass with a shallow bowl and wide rim, typically holding about 150–200 ml and suited to short drinks served without ice.
- [Pint glass](pint-glass.md) — A straight-sided beer glass holding 568 ml in the UK and 473 ml in the US; conical, nonic and handled jug versions are common, and capacity varies by market and product.
- [Red Wine Glass](red-wine-glass.md) — A stemmed glass with a wide bowl and a slightly narrowed rim, used for red wine. The wide bowl opens the aroma and the narrowed rim gathers it at the nose.
- [Rocks glass](rocks-glass.md) — A short, wide-mouthed short-drink glass used for spirits over ice and stirred cocktails; capacity has no single standard, so adjust ice to your own glass.
- [Shot Glass](shot-glass.md) — A small, thick-walled glass used for one-swallow servings of spirits, for measuring and for layered cocktails. It is built for convenience and intensity, not aroma.
- [Snifter](snifter.md) — A short-stemmed glass with a large bowl and a narrowed rim, traditionally used for Cognac and brandy; typical capacity is 300–800 ml, while the actual pour is far smaller.
- [Tasting glass](tasting-glass.md) — The small tulip-shaped glass defined by ISO 3591, commonly called the INAO glass, with a nominal capacity of 215 ml; used for comparative tasting of wine, spirits and sake.
- [Tiki Mug](tiki-mug.md) — A shaped ceramic or glass mug carved as a tiki god, mask or totem. It belongs to tropical cocktail culture, and its capacity and form are more theatrical than an ordinary glass.
- [Tulip glass](tulip-glass.md) — A stemmed glass with a wide bowl that narrows towards the rim, used for Champagne, beer and some short drinks; it balances aroma concentration and bubble retention.
- [Whisky Nosing Glass](whisky-nosing-glass.md) — A small tasting glass with a tulip-shaped bowl and a narrowed rim, used for nosing and sipping whisky. The rim gathers aroma and the short base fits the palm.
- [White Wine Glass](white-wine-glass.md) — A stemmed glass with a smaller bowl and a narrower rim, used for white wine. The smaller bowl limits oxidation and the narrowed rim holds the floral and acidic freshness.
