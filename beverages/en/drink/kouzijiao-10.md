# Kouzijiao 10 Year

"Kouzijiao 10 Year" is the common market name for the Ten-Year Type in the vintage series of [Kouzijiao](ref:brand); the formal product name is usually written as "Kouzijiao Ten-Year Type". It is a mixed-aroma baijiu, commonly bottled at 41% or 46% ABV in 500 mL and made in Huaibei, Anhui. The "10 years" in the name is a series designation, not a statement that every drop in the bottle spent ten years in storage.![Kouzijiao 10 Year category image](/media/photos/drink-style-light-aroma-baijiu-1c3af236.jpg)

## Definition and position

The vintage series of [Kouzijiao](ref:brand) uses numbers to separate product tiers. The Ten-Year Type sits in the brand's mid to upper-mid range, above it are higher-numbered vintage products, and below it are Laokouzi, Kouzifang and Kouzijiu. The number primarily serves product tiering and channel pricing, and only secondarily describes a style.

Mixed aroma is not an arbitrary blend of strong aroma and sauce aroma; it is a distinct process route with its own flavour identity. Understanding this bottle means looking at brand, producer, production area, process and exact specification together, rather than treating "mixed aroma" as a compromise between two other aroma types.

## Producer and brand

The product is made by [Anhui Kouzi Distillery Co., Ltd.](ref:producer) and the brand is [Kouzijiao](ref:brand). The company is based in Huaibei, Anhui, in the cultural belt of the Sui-Tang Grand Canal and the Huang-Huai river famous-liquor zone. It listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange on 29 June 2015, becoming the first listed mixed-aroma baijiu company.

Brand and listed company are two layers: the brand answers "what is it called and who is it for", while the listed company answers "who owns the trademark and who carries legal responsibility". The company also owns Laokouzi, Kouzifang and Kouzijiu, so the style of Kouzijiao cannot be used to describe every product it makes.

## Origin and raw materials

Suixi and Kouzi town in Huaibei form the core production narrative of Kouzi liquor. Company material presents local climate, water systems and microbial conditions as the basis of brewing and traces the local tradition back more than two thousand seven hundred years. This is regional and brand narrative, and it should not be converted directly into the founding year of the modern company.

The public product description emphasises "five grains and pure grain qu" and water from the "Sui-Tang ancient spring". [Sorghum](ref:sorghum) is the main source of the baijiu frame, rice adds sweetness, wheat participates in qu making and supplies fermentation power, and [water](ref:water) affects the cleanliness of the entry and the harmony of the body. The exact ratio is governed by the company process and the bottle label.

A five-grain recipe is a style route, not a quality grade. Treating "pure grain" as the only standard ignores the effect of qu, pits, fermentation length and storage on the body.

## Process

Mixed-aroma baijiu usually draws on parts of both the strong-aroma and sauce-aroma routes: qu making, pit fermentation, distillation and long storage each need separate control. [Qu making](ref:qu-making) determines the microbial community and aroma precursors, [fermentation](ref:fermentation) completes saccharification and alcohol production in the pit, and [distillation](ref:distillation) cuts the run by stage, with the middle section used as the main base spirit.

Company material says its core brewing craft has been listed as intangible cultural heritage and that it uses century-old pits for long fermentation and a three-step circulating storage system. Pit condition, fermentation temperature and storage time all affect the final style, so "Ten-Year Type" describes a product position and a process combination rather than a single number.

The base spirits are [blended](ref:blending) to unify aroma, body and finish. Blending manages batch variation and style consistency; it is not simple addition or dilution. When judging whether a mixed aroma is coherent, look at how the aromas connect and how the finish behaves, not only at the category label.

## Specification and label

Public product material gives the common specifications as 41% and 46% ABV in 500 mL, with the aroma type marked as mixed aroma. Baijiu industry terminology and aroma classification can be checked against GB/T 15109, while the specific product standard and grade are governed by the bottle label.

For Kouzijiao 10 Year, the producer, product name, aroma type, strength, net volume, raw materials, standard and anti-counterfeit information on the label are the basic identity checks. Buy through regular channels and verify the anti-counterfeit mark and batch rather than judging by packaging similarity or price alone.

## Aroma and tasting

The nose is led by pit aroma with grain and qu notes. The entry is mellow and sweet, the body is fairly harmonious, the middle opens and the finish is clean. At 41% ABV the spirit is softer and suits slow drinking with food; at 46% ABV the aroma releases more directly and the alcohol is more noticeable. Strength changes the entry and aroma presentation, but it is not a quality ranking.

For Kouzijiao 10 Year, taste in a small glass: smell the resting spirit first, then swirl gently and smell again. Let the liquid stay in the mouth and follow the order of sweet, sour, bitter and umami, along with body weight. Adding water lowers the alcohol and opens the aroma, but it also changes the balance, so add drop by drop and compare.

## History and market

Company material traces the brand back to the brewing tradition around Suixi and uses the Sui-Tang ancient spring, century-old pits and three-step circulating storage as its craft narrative. After the company listed in 2015, the vintage series became an important line for Kouzijiao's expansion into the mid-to-high price band.

The vintage series sits where a regional brand is extending into the national market. Prices move with vintage, channel and supply and demand, and this page gives no figures. Treating price as proof of quality, or the "10 years" as age in the bottle, are common misreadings.

## Buying and storage

For Kouzijiao 10 Year, buy through regular channels and check the anti-counterfeit mark, producer and batch. Gift boxes and channel-specific editions should be checked against the label, so that a packaging difference is not mistaken

For Kouzijiao 10 Year, unopened baijiu should be kept upright, away from light, in a cool place without strong odours. Once opened, drink it reasonably soon; prolonged exposure to air costs aroma. A home cellar cannot reproduce distillery storage conditions, so the year of purchase should not be treated as an automatic quality gain.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating "10 years" as age in the bottle. It is a series designation.
Second, treating mixed aroma as a simple blend of strong and sauce aroma. It has its own process and style identity.
Third, treating "pure grain" as the only quality indicator. Qu, pits, fermentation and storage matter just as much.
Fourth, assuming 46% ABV must be better than 41% ABV. Strength serves product design and drinking context.
Fifth, judging authenticity by price or packaging. Anti-counterfeit marks, channels and labels are more reliable.
