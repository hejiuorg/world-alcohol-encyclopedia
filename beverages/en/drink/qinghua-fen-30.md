# Qinghua Fenjiu 30

Qinghua Fenjiu 30 is a high-end product from [Fenjiu](ref:brand) and belongs to the light-aroma route of [baijiu](ref:baijiu), from Xinghuacun in Fenyang, Shanxi. It is commonly 53% ABV in 500 mL. Company material says it is blended with 30-year base spirit, so "30" first refers to product position and blending practice, not a uniform age for every bottle.![Qinghua Fenjiu 30 category image](/media/photos/drink-style-light-aroma-baijiu-1c3af236.jpg)

## Definition and position

Qinghua Fenjiu 30 is a product name, not a brand name or a company name. Under the brand there are also Laobaifen, Bofen and other series covering different price bands; the Qinghua series is positioned high, with Qinghua 30 as a representative product and Qinghua 20 as another version.

Light aroma is not assessed on the same scale as strong aroma or sauce aroma. It stresses cleanliness, freshness and clarity, and should not be judged low quality merely because the aroma is less intense. Style differences are route differences.

## Producer and brand

The product is made within the [group](ref:producer) system, the brand is carried by [Fenjiu](ref:brand), and the listed company handles production and disclosure. Group, joint-stock company, brand and product are four layers with different data scopes.

When you see "Qinghua 30 revenue", establish whether it is the listed company's product scope or a channel estimate; when you see a "Revival Edition", establish whether it is a packaging upgrade, a body adjustment or a channel-exclusive version.

## Origin and raw materials

Fenyang lies in central Shanxi with a temperate continental monsoon climate: distinct seasons and limited rainfall. The groundwater and loess environment around Xinghuacun suit separated jar fermentation. Within the map of [China](ref:china)'s baijiu regions, Fenyang is an important light-aroma node.

The main raw material is [sorghum](ref:sorghum), with barley and peas for qu. Sorghum starch and grain structure affect fermentation start-up, and the qu raw-material ratio affects the aroma direction. The ingredient list and standard are directly checkable.

## Brewing process

Light aroma uses repeated clean steaming and jar fermentation: the raw material and mash are steamed separately, with two fermentations and two distillations, and the mash is separated from soil to reduce pit-microbe influence. [Qu making](ref:qu-making) and jar management together decide how clean the aroma is.

After fermentation the spirit is [distilled](ref:distillation) with fractional cutting, and new spirit is stored before blending. Company material says 30-year base spirit is used in blending; this is company scope, and exact ratios and age composition should follow official information and the label rather than inference from the name.

## Specification and label

The common specification is 53% ABV in 500 mL; the Revival Edition changes packaging and bottle design. The product standard for light-aroma baijiu is commonly GB/T 10781.2, and the bottle label governs.

The producer, product name, aroma type, strength, volume, standard and anti-counterfeit information on the label are the basics for identity. Buy through official channels and check anti-counterfeit marks and batches rather than judging from the number "30" or the blue-and-white colour.

## Flavour and tasting

The aroma is clean and elegant, led by grain, qu and light floral-fruit notes; the entry is crisp, the body clean and the finish fresh. At 53% ABV the heat is noticeable, so sip slowly in small amounts and avoid drinking fast on an empty stomach.

Taste from a small cup: smell at rest, then gently swirl and smell again. Let the liquid stay in the mouth and note the order of sweet, sour, bitter and savoury and the finish. Water lowers heat but changes the light-aroma balance, so add drop by drop and compare.

## History and market

Common public corporate milestones are: the joint-stock company was restructured in December 1993 and listed in January 1994, and the group was restructured in March 2002. The Qinghua series represents the brand's move upmarket, and Qinghua 30 has long sat in the high-end price band.

Prices change with year, channel and supply and demand, and this entry gives no figures. Treating "30" as the age of the liquid, or treating a packaging generation change as a body upgrade, are common misreadings. Company history, brand age and product age should be read separately.

## Buying and storage

Prefer official channels and check anti-counterfeit marks, producer and batch. Edition differences such as gift boxes and the Revival Edition should be checked against the label and official information, and packaging differences should not be mistaken for body differences. For full cases, check seals and storage conditions.

For Qinghua Fenjiu 30, unopened baijiu should be stored upright, away from light, in a cool place without strong odours. Once opened, drink it soon; prolonged exposure loses aroma. Home storage cannot reproduce distillery conditions, so purchase year should not be equated with quality gain.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating "30" as the age of the liquid. It is first of all a product position and blending statement.
Second, treating light aroma as no aroma. It stresses cleanliness and freshness.
Third, treating a packaging generation change as a body upgrade. Edition differences need official explanation.
Fourth, assuming higher strength is more authentic. Strength serves product design.
Fifth, judging authenticity from price or packaging. Anti-counterfeit checks, channels and labels are more reliable.
