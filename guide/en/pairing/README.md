# Pairings

[返回English目录](../README.md)

- [Cheese and Wine: Match Texture, Salt and Fat](cheese-and-wine.md) — The texture, salt and fat of a cheese decide the direction of the wine; from fresh goat cheese to blue cheese, acidity, sweetness and body matter more than the old rule that red wine goes with every cheese.
- [Dessert and Port: Sweeter Than the Dessert](dessert-and-port.md) — The core of dessert and Port pairing is that the wine must not be less sweet than the dessert; choose Ruby, Tawny or White Port by the dessert's sugar, fat and intensity, with acidity and oxidative notes deciding whether the finish stays clean.
- [French cuisine with Burgundy: Pinot Noir and the dialogue of sauce](french-burgundy.md) — The butter, mushrooms, poultry and sauces of French cooking suit the light body, high perfume and fine tannin of red Burgundy; the match relies on aroma and acidity, not on body weight overwhelming the dish.
- [Grilled Meat and Beer: Balance in the Smoke](grilled-meat-beer.md) — Charcoal char, fat and salt meet the bubbles, bitterness and low temperature of beer. The key is choosing the right type and controlling temperature and pace, not treating beer as water that cuts grease.
- [Hot Pot and Baijiu: One Hot, One Strong](hotpot-baijiu.md) — Hot pot brings fat, chill heat and high temperature; baijiu brings high alcohol and concentrated aroma. The key is not "the hotter the dish, the stronger the spirit" but controlling entry strength, temperature and pace.
- [Italian cuisine with Sangiovese: tomato, olive oil and acidity](italian-sangiovese.md) — The tomato, olive oil, herbs and hard cheese of Italian cooking suit the high acidity, cherry fruit and dry tannin of Sangiovese; the match is made by aligning acidity, not by seeking the heaviest tannin.
- [Japanese cuisine with sake: umami and rice aroma in balance](japanese-sake.md) — The raw freshness, lightness and soy umami of Japanese food suit the rice aroma, amino-acid umami and soft acidity of sake; choose between junmai and ginjo according to fat and sauce.
- [Jiangzhe cuisine with Shaoxing yellow wine: one language of umami](jiangzhe-shaoxing.md) — The river fish, sauces and sweet-savoury balance of Jiangzhe cuisine share a language with the umami and mellow body of Shaoxing yellow wine; choose yuanhong, jiafan, shanniang or xiangxue according to the weight of the dish.
- [Korean cuisine with soju: grilled meat, kimchi and the small-cup rhythm](korean-soju.md) — The fermented heat, grilled fat and shared-table style of Korean food suit the lower strength, slight sweetness and small cups of diluted soju; the key is rhythm, temperature and the order of dishes, not drinking volume.
- [Mexican Food and Beer: Maize, Chilli and Lime in Balance](mexican-food-and-beer.md) — The maize, chilli, lime and fat of Mexican food meet the bubbles, bitterness and malt sweetness of beer in several layers; choosing lager, IPA or dark malt beer by cooking method and heat is more accurate than "ice-cold beer with everything".
- [Seafood and Champagne: Bubbles, Acidity and Salt](seafood-and-champagne.md) — The savoury freshness, salt and fat of seafood meet the bubbles, acidity and low tannin of Champagne; choosing the right sweetness, body and bubble intensity matters more than the idea that Champagne is only for celebrations.
- [Shandong cuisine with sauce-aroma baijiu: savoury weight and a long finish](shandong-sauce-aroma.md) — The savoury saltiness, scallion aroma and thick sauces of Shandong cooking meet the roast, floral and long finish of sauce-aroma baijiu; the match is made by equal palate weight, not by making the dish saltier.
- [Sichuan cuisine with strong-aroma baijiu: heat against cellar aroma](sichuan-strong-aroma.md) — Sichuan heat, fat and fermented seasonings meet the cellar and grain aroma of strong-aroma baijiu; the key is controlling intensity and rhythm, not using high-strength spirit to put out the fire.
- [Spanish cuisine with Rioja: ham, roast lamb and oak](spanish-rioja.md) — The ham, olives, roast lamb and paella of Spanish cooking suit Rioja reds based on Tempranillo; vanilla and spice from traditional oak carry the savoury weight and fat.
- [Thai cuisine with Riesling: high acid and aroma against heat](thai-riesling.md) — The sour heat, lemongrass, fish sauce and coconut milk of Thai food meet the high acidity and perfume of Riesling; choose dry to off-dry according to heat, sweetness and fat, not by "the sweeter the better". When ordering, check the dry or off-dry term, the alcohol level and the vintage, and serve at about 8 to 10 degrees Celsius.
- [Vegetarian Food and Wine: A Logic of Vegetables, Pulses and Mushrooms](vegetarian-and-wine.md) — Vegetarian food is not one flavour but a group of very different ingredients; choose wine by intensity, acidity, bitterness, umami and fat, which is more reliable than the idea that vegetables only go with white wine.
