# Routes

- [Alsace Grand Cru trail: Kaysersberg and Riquewihr](alsace-grand-cru-trail.md) — From Domaine Weinbach in Kaysersberg to Hugel in Riquewihr, both stops sit in the rain shadow of the Vosges. The route links the 51 Grand Cru sites, the four noble grapes and the logic of Vendanges Tardives and Sélection de Grains Nobles; the drive is short, the structure broad.
- [Armagnac trail: single estates of Bas-Armagnac](armagnac-trail.md) — From Château de Laubade in Sorbets, Gers, to Domaine d'Ognoas in Arthez-d'Armagnac, Landes, two single estates line up across the tawny sands of Bas-Armagnac. One uses a 1974 continuous still and three thousand casks for vintage work; the other grew from a medieval seigneurie into a departmental showcase. The route links continuous distillation, vintage labels and the Gascon countryside in a day and a half.
- [Barbados rum trail: Saint Lucy and Saint Philip](barbados-rum-trail.md) — From Mount Gay in the north of Saint Lucy to Foursquare in the south-east of Saint Philip, about an hour apart. One stop explains continuity through a 1703 well and pot-plus-column distillation; the other explains transparency through an open-house sugar factory and a no-added-sugar position.
- [Barossa wine trail: Magill Estate to Eden Valley](barossa-wine-trail.md) — A drive from Penfolds Magill Estate in the Adelaide foothills to Henschke at Keyneton in Eden Valley, linking the immigration history of the 1840s, old-vine Shiraz across two altitude bands, and Penfolds' tradition of experimentation.
- [Bavaria beer trail: Munich to Bamberg](bavaria-beer-trail.md) — From the Hofbräuhaus in Munich's old town to the Augustiner-Bräu brewery quarter in the west of the city, then by train to Schlenkerla in Bamberg's old town. Two stops in Munich and one in Bamberg, best spread over a day and a half or two days.
- [Belgium beer trail: lambic in Brussels, city ale in Bruges](belgium-beer-trail.md) — See Cantillon hand fermentation to the air in Anderlecht, then take the train to Bruges and watch De Halve Maan keep yeast in the tank. One day and two fermentation logics explain why Belgian beer resists a single sentence.
- [Bordeaux left bank: from La Cité du Vin to two Pauillac first growths](bordeaux-left-bank-chateaux.md) — Build a frame at La Cité du Vin on the Bacalan quays, then take a regional train north to Pauillac for the vineyards, houses and visiting limits of Lafite and Mouton. Three stops narrow from the civilisation of wine to two gravel slopes in one village.
- [Burgundy: Beaune, Meursault and the culture of the négociant](burgundy-grands-crus-drive.md) — Start with the charitable hospital space of the Hôtel-Dieu in Beaune, drive south to Meursault for a château and its cellars, then return to the old town and Joseph Drouhin. City, country estate and merchant house, in one loop.
- [Campbeltown: two distilleries, two streets](campbeltown-whisky-trail.md) — A walking route through Scotland's smallest protected whisky region, linking Springbank in Well Close with Glen Scotia on High Street. Less than a kilometre apart, the two represent a distillery that keeps the whole process on site and a Victorian plant that has changed hands repeatedly but kept its old scale, compressing Campbeltown's boom and retreat into half a day.
- [Champagne: a walk along the Avenue de Champagne in Épernay](champagne-avenue-walk.md) — On a street of about a kilometre and a half, read Champagne through its houses and its hills at once. Walk the façades, courtyards and cellar entrances, descend into Moët's 28 kilometres of galleries, then continue to Perrier-Jouët for its Art Nouveau house, cellars and Chardonnay-led cuvées.
- [Cognac trail: the house belt between Cognac and Jarnac](cognac-trail.md) — A belt of houses along a dozen kilometres of the Charente, with both stops inside Cognac town: the Hennessy quay and the Rémy Martin historic house. One shows a large house running ten tours as a brand flagship; the other breaks a Fine Champagne promise into three visitor sites. The background is six crus, Ugni Blanc, double distillation and the VS/VSOP/XO scale.
- [Cuba rum trail: Havana and Santiago de Cuba](cuba-rum-trail.md) — From the brand museum at Avenida del Puerto 262 in old Havana to the local industry museum at Calle San Basilio 358 in Santiago de Cuba. The cities are more than 800 km apart and share one thread: aguardiente to ron, 1862, nationalisation and the export system.
- [Dublin pub trail: from Lower Bridge Street to St James's Gate](dublin-pub-trail.md) — Start at the Brazen Head on the south bank of the Liffey and walk west to the Guinness Storehouse. One stop is an everyday pub pouring stout from the tap; the other is a brand exhibition in an old fermentation building. Half a day shows how Dublin sells and explains the same dark beer.
- [Irish whiskey: coast, city and midlands](ireland-whiskey-trail.md) — From Bushmills on the Antrim coast, south to the old Jameson distillery at Bow Street in Dublin, then west to the old plant at Kilbeggan. The three stops stand for a working distillery, a distillery that stopped and became a visitor centre, and a small-town plant revived by its community, so one drive holds three different periods of Irish whiskey.
- [Islay: three peat distilleries on the south coast](islay-whisky-trail.md) — From Port Ellen, follow the south-coast path to Lagavulin, Ardbeg and Laphroaig. The three distilleries sit close together, yet their styles run from slow-distilled heavy peat to a medicinal, sea-driven peat, so one walk connects wind, sea, malt and fire.
- [Japan: from Yamazaki Distillery to the sake kura of Fushimi and Nishinomiya](japan-sake-kura-trail.md) — Begin with the birthplace of Japanese malt whisky at Yamazaki, take the railway to Gekkeikan's Okura Sake Museum in Fushimi, then continue to the Hakushika Sake Museum in Nishinomiya. Water connects all three: the springs of Yamazaki, the soft water of Fushimi and the Nada transport network.
- [Japan's whisky trail: from Yamazaki to Hakushu by rail](japan-whisky-trail.md) — Yamazaki and Hakushu both belong to Suntory, yet they sit in Osaka Prefecture and Yamanashi Prefecture with most of Honshu between them. This route uses no car, linking the two distilleries by JR trains and a shuttle bus to compare two waters, two climates and two approaches to making spirit.
- [Jerez sherry route: one city, two houses, three paths of ageing](jerez-sherry-route.md) — From Lustau on Calle Arcos to González Byass on Calle Manuel María González, both in Jerez de la Frontera, this walk links the solera stack, biological flor ageing and oxidative ageing; it covers the order, the walk and short drives, booking and seasons, pairing, and the drink-drive boundary.
- [Kagoshima shochu route: Makurazaki to Miyakonojo](kagoshima-shochu-route.md) — From the Hanatogawa Distillery Meijigura in Makurazaki to Kirishima Factory Garden in Miyakonojo, both using sweet potato; the drive connects white and black koji, atmospheric and vacuum distillation, the 25- and 20-degree boundary, and jar fermentation and storage.
- [Kentucky bourbon trail: Frankfort, Bardstown and Loretto](kentucky-bourbon-trail.md) — From Buffalo Trace in Frankfort to Heaven Hill in Bardstown and Maker's Mark in Loretto. The three stops form a triangle of about an hour, half an hour and a little over an hour, and answer three different questions about large-scale brands, a family recipe matrix and a wheat farm.
- [Loire Chenin trail: Vouvray and Savennières](loire-chenin-trail.md) — From Domaine Huet in Vouvray to La Coulée de Serrant in Savennières, the two stops run through the middle Loire. Dry, off-dry, botrytised and sparkling Chenin, plus two biodynamic estates, connect the very different expressions of Vouvray and Savennières.
- [London classic bar walk: three hotel bars and a Fleet Street pub](london-classic-bar-walk.md) — From the American Bar at The Savoy via the Connaught Bar in Mayfair and DUKES Bar in St James's to Ye Olde Cheshire Cheese on Fleet Street. Three hotel bars set against one historic pub, linked on foot and by short taxi rides, for an evening that runs into the night.

- [Madeira wine route: two faces of the wine trade in central Funchal](madeira-wine-route.md) — A walk through central Funchal linking Blandy's Wine Lodge with Henriques & Henriques — a convent turned visitor cellar and a house founded in 1850 that still farms Quinta Grande; together they turn the question of why Madeira keeps into one about process, with booking, cruise traffic and pairing notes.
- [Maotai Town baijiu route: from the Culture City to the distillery](maotai-baijiu-town.md) — A one-day route through sauce-aroma baijiu at Maotai Town, Renhuai, Guizhou: start at the China Liquor Culture City for the history and craft, then move to the Maotai distillery on the Chishui River to understand the core production area, the annual rhythm and the limits of visiting; includes booking, tasting, buying and local transport.

- [Marlborough wine trail: Wairau gravel to Awatere sea wind](marlborough-wine-trail.md) — A self-drive route pairing Cloudy Bay near Blenheim with Yealands at Seddon, moving from the old riverbed gravels of the Wairau Valley to the coastal wind of the Awatere Valley in a single day.
- [McLaren Vale Wine Route](mclaren-vale-wine-route.md) — South from Adelaide into McLaren Vale, anchored by d’Arenberg and Wirra Wirra; a day of Mediterranean climate, century-o…
- [Mendoza wine trail: Luján de Cuyo to Maipú](mendoza-wine-trail.md) — A self-drive route from Catena Zapata in Luján de Cuyo to the Zuccardi family base in Maipú, both in Mendoza's Primera Zona, using the drive to explain 1,500-metre altitude, Andean snowmelt drip irrigation and Malbec across the Uco Valley's three altitude bands.
- [Mexico Tequila Trail](mexico-tequila-trail.md) — From Casa Herradura at Amatitlán to Fortaleza in Tequila town, two stops in the volcanic lowlands and their Blue Weber agave core. The first combines estate scale, brick dome ovens, wild yeast and modern milling; the second keeps a tahona, wooden vats and two old copper stills for small batches. The route also frames the CRT and NOM-006 rules.
- [Mosel Riesling trail: two steep-slope estates on the Mittelmosel and Saar](mosel-riesling-trail.md) — From Dr. Loosen in Bernkastel-Kues to Egon Müller in Wiltingen, the two stops follow the Mosel and Saar valleys. Slate slopes, hand work and the Riesling ripeness scale connect the Mittelmosel's parcel logic with the single hill of the Scharzhofberg.
- [Napa Valley: the modern winery belt after the 1976 Judgment of Paris](napa-valley-wine-route.md) — Start in Robert Mondavi's To Kalon vineyards, continue to Opus One, the joint venture between Mondavi and Baron Philippe de Rothschild, and finish at Beringer, founded in 1876. All three sit along St Helena Highway, yet they belong to three different times.
- [New York historic pubs: East Village to Gramercy](new-york-historic-pubs.md) — From McSorley's Old Ale House in the East Village via the White Horse Tavern in the West Village to Pete's Tavern by Gramercy Park. Three old pubs with three different kinds of “old”, linked mainly by subway and on foot, for an afternoon into the evening.
- [Oaxaca Mezcal Trail](oaxaca-mezcal-trail.md) — Start at Mezcaloteca in central Oaxaca city to build coordinates of variety and village, then head south to Real Minero in Santa Catarina Minas for earthen pits, wooden mallets and clay pots. Between them lie the Espadín fields of the central valleys, the palenques of Santiago Matatlán and the floor set by NOM-070.
- [Piedmont Barbaresco trail: Barbaresco to Serralunga](piedmont-barbaresco-trail.md) — From Gaja in Barbaresco to Fontanafredda in Serralunga d'Alba, two stops about half an hour apart. The drive links Nebbiolo, the legal differences between Barbaresco and Barolo, Langhe fog and late picking, and the single-vineyard and oak arguments. Gaja's visiting policy is not publicly confirmed, so contact the estate before going.
- [Porto and Douro route: old vines at Sabrosa, the Nacional block at Pinhão](porto-douro-route.md) — A drive along the Douro's right bank from Quinta do Crasto at Gouvinhas in Sabrosa to Quinta do Noval near Pinhão; both estates work schist slopes and make Douro DOC and Port, but one tells a story of estate-wide family continuity and the other of a single ungrafted block.
- [Prague beer trail: from U Fleků to U Medvídků](prague-beer-trail.md) — Walk from U Fleků, which brews a single dark lager, to U Medvídků, which packs a microbrewery into an old town house. Less than a kilometre apart, the two stops put two Prague attitudes to beer into one afternoon: one beer perfected, or one small brewery doing everything.
- [Provence rosé trail: Taradeau to Correns](provence-rose-trail.md) — From Château de Selle in Taradeau to Miraval in Correns, two stops about an hour apart. One day sets two Provençal rosé paths side by side: a working cellar that receives visitors by appointment, and a private estate that stays closed and sells only through channels. The drive also explains the Mistral, limestone and schist, and the direct-press method behind pale rosé.
- [Qingdao Beer Trail: museum, old brewery and Dengzhou Road](qingdao-beer-trail.md) — Start in the century-old halls and old equipment of the Tsingtao Beer Museum, walk the glass corridor of the still-working plant on the same site, then step onto Dengzhou Road Beer Street for a jug of draft beer and a plate of spicy clams. The stops are close together but tell three different stories: industrial history, a working factory and city nightlife.
- [Rioja: three century-old bodegas by Haro station](rioja-haro-bodegas.md) — From López de Heredia, walk to La Rioja Alta and then CVNE. All three stand in the small Barrio de la Estación beside Haro's railway station, yet represent extreme tradition, classic long ageing and a family brand; the route explains the district's history, the American-oak tradition, booking and arriving by train.
- [Saijo sake route: Kamotsuru to Kamoizumi on foot](saijo-sake-route.md) — A walk from Kamotsuru's No. 1 Brewery to Kamoizumi's Enju warehouse, both within a little over a kilometre on Saijo's Sakagura-dori; the route uses seven breweries as an industrial cluster and follows soft water, Yamada Nishiki, winter brewing, ginjo versus junmai ginjo labels and the spring kurabiraki openings.
- [Saint-Germain-des-Prés literary cafés: a half-day walk](saint-germain-literary-cafes.md) — A two-stop walking route around the Place Saint-Germain-des-Prés in Paris: sit first at Les Deux Magots looking onto the square, then cross the street corner to Café de Flore. The two are very close together, so this suits half a day of slow walking rather than sightseeing on a schedule.
- [Highlands whisky trail: Tain to the Drumochter pass](scotland-highlands-trail.md) — From Glenmorangie by the Dornoch Firth to Dalwhinnie at the edge of the Cairngorms, following the A9. One stop sits by the sea, the other by a mountain pass, and two hours of driving split the large Highland region into a light style and a heavier one.
- [Shaoxing Yellow Wine Trail: museum, Xianheng and the central cellar](shaoxing-yellow-wine-trail.md) — A one-day route that splits Shaoxing yellow wine into three layers. The three stops answer how it is made, how it is drunk and how it is stored.

- [Speyside malt trail: Dufftown, Craigellachie and Ballindalloch](speyside-malt-trail.md) — From Glenfiddich in Dufftown, via The Macallan Estate at Craigellachie, to Glenlivet at Ballindalloch. The three stops follow the A941 and A95, each within half an hour of the next, and represent family independence and volume, sherry casks and a new distillery, and a fruity style with a legalisation history.
- [Tennessee whiskey trail: Lynchburg and Cascade Hollow](tennessee-whiskey-trail.md) — From Jack Daniel's in Lynchburg to George Dickel at Cascade Hollow near Tullahoma. The two stops are half an hour to forty minutes apart, both complete their charcoal filtering in Tennessee, and use ambient and chilled methods to turn one process into two textures.
- [Tokyo bar route: two bartenders in Ginza](tokyo-bar-route.md) — From Bar High Five in Ginza 5-chome to Star Bar Ginza by Namiki-dori in 1-chome, both reached on foot; a short evening route contrasting no-menu custom drinks with a classic old-school counter.
- [Chianti Classico: from Florence to three estates](tuscany-chianti-classico.md) — A one-day drive from Florence to the Antinori winery at Bargino for contemporary architecture and gravity-flow winemaking, Castello di Brolio at Gaiole for the Ricasoli family and the 1872 formula, and Fattoria Fèlsina at Castelnuovo Berardenga for Sangiovese clones and plot differences.
