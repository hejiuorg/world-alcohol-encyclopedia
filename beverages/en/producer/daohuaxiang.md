# Hubei Daohuaxiang Distillery Co., Ltd.

Daohuaxiang is a baijiu company in Longquan, Yiling District, Yichang. The public knows the brand, but the legal layer is the registered entity "Hubei Daohuaxiang Distillery Co., Ltd." It is a non-listed joint-stock company, registered as a "joint-stock company (non-listed, invested or controlled by natural persons)", built around [strong-aroma baijiu](ref:strong-aroma) and extended through a group platform into packaging, logistics and related industries. Understanding the company means separating the distillery, the group platform and the brand.

## Corporate entities: distillery and group platform

The entity that directly makes and sells baijiu is Hubei Daohuaxiang Distillery Co., Ltd., registered in Longquan, Yiling District, Yichang, Hubei. The group-level platform is Daohuaxiang Group Investment Holding Co., Ltd., established on 14 November 2012 with registered capital of RMB 702.5 million; its legal representative is Cai Kaiyun, and its business scope is investment with own funds in food, beverages, packaging, plastics, chemicals, building decoration and related industries.

The distillery handles brewing, storage, blending and sales; the group platform handles industrial layout and resource allocation. The two cannot substitute for each other: an external investment by "Daohuaxiang Group" does not mean new capacity at the distillery, and product news at the distillery does not mean every group business is growing.

## Ownership type and current control

The National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System records Hubei Daohuaxiang Distillery as a non-listed joint-stock company invested or controlled by natural persons. Public material generally names Cai Kaiyun as chairman and manager, but the full shareholder list and individual ratios are not disclosed item by item on a checkable public page, so this producer does not state specific percentages.

That means two things: the company has no publicly traded shares, so outsiders cannot read its equity structure from periodic reports; and control arrangements can only be observed indirectly through registration changes and group personnel information. Any claim of a specific shareholding should be checked against registration records or company announcements.

## Expansion since 1992

Public material dates the company's starting point to 1992, when it began as a local distillery, then expanded capacity, entered Yichang and Wuhan, and gradually became a group. After group formation, the business scope extended into packaging, logistics and related industries, while baijiu remained the core.

1992 is the group's entrepreneurial milestone, not the launch date of every product under the Daohuaxiang brand. The launch and replacement dates of individual products should be read from packaging batches.

## Longquan site and industrial support

Longquan lies in eastern Yichang, in the hills of the middle and upper Yangtze, with a warm, humid subtropical monsoon climate suited to multiple rounds of fermentation. The water systems and grain supply of [Hubei](ref:hubei) provide conditions for brewing, and sorghum and wheat from western Hubei are common raw materials.

The plant and supporting industries are concentrated in Longquan, forming a pattern of "brewing locally, support nearby". Public material does not disclose verifiable numbers of branch plants, fermentation pits or base-liquor inventory, and this producer does not fill them in.

## Strong-aroma process and capacity organisation

Strong aroma uses mud-pit solid-state fermentation, continued mash with fresh grain, and mixed steaming and firing; after unloading, the spirit is cut by segment, stored by grade and blended. [Qu-making](ref:qu-making) is mainly based on wheat, and the microbial composition of the qu shapes the pit aroma and grain aroma; [fermentation](ref:fermentation) temperature and cycle determine body and cleanliness.

Raw materials include sorghum, wheat, rice, glutinous rice and maize, in different proportions by product. Published process parameters are limited and capacity figures have no single definition; the label, standard and batch are the reliable basis. Strong-aroma products commonly cite GB/T 10781.1, but each product's declared standard governs.

## Product structure from the company's view

From the company's perspective, the portfolio is a combination of "premium flagship + banquet mainstay + everyday value": Qingyang carries the premium image, the Huoli family covers banquets and daily drinking, Zhenpin serves traditional channels, and Shouchaojian explores soy-aroma flavour. In 2024 the company launched Qingyang Appreciation, Zunya and Crystal editions; at the 2025 distributor conference it released 5 series and 15 new products.

These moves show the company using premium products to raise the ceiling and multiple series to cover different channels and price points.

## Channels and regional market

Daohuaxiang's core market is Hubei, with Yichang as its base and Wuhan and the western Hubei city cluster as priorities. Channels are mainly distributors, tobacco-and-liquor shops and banquet group purchases, with e-commerce and livestreaming as supplements. Group-level packaging and logistics support can lower local fulfilment costs, but they also mean managing several business segments at once.

Outside the province, the brand faces competition from national names and local brands alike. Public material does not disclose provincial sales shares, and this producer does not speculate.

## Capital market and financing status

Daohuaxiang Distillery is not a listed company, and the group platform has no publicly traded shares. Outsiders therefore cannot obtain revenue, profit, capacity and shareholder structure from periodic reports; they can refer to business registration, company news, industry association information and local government material.

That does not mean there is no capital activity. The group investment holding platform shows that the business is managed through a holding company, but specific financing, capital increases and equity changes must be checked against registration records. Mistaking the "group" for a "listed company" is a common reporting error.

## Governance and the limits of public information

The company website provides products, news and events; the business registration system provides the entity and ownership type. They answer different questions. The website stresses product upgrading, while registration only confirms the legal entity and status.

For Hubei Daohuaxiang Distillery, public material does not disclose the full shareholder list, plant-level capacity, base-liquor inventory or related-party details. When such figures appear, ask whether they come from registration, a company announcement or a promotional article. This producer does not fill in unverifiable ratios or capacity.

Mixing the two layers leads to inferences such as "a diversified group means better liquor" or "a new chairman changes the recipe". Reading them separately keeps corporate governance and product choice in their own places.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating Daohuaxiang Group as a single distillery. The group also has packaging, logistics and other brands.
Second, treating "invested or controlled by natural persons" as an absence of modern governance. A non-listed joint-stock company still has a board and management.
Third, treating 1992 as the starting point of every product. The group's founding year is not a product launch year.
Fourth, treating "Qingyang" as an aroma type. It is a series name; read the label for aroma and strength.
