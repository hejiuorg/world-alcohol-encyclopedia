# Producers

[返回English目录](../README.md)

- [Hubei Baiyunbian Distillery](baiyunbian.md) — A baijiu producer in Songzi, Jingzhou, Hubei, built in 1952 and restructured in 2005 through a state-share exit and private ownership reform; it is known for strong-sauce mixed-aroma craft and took part in drafting GB/T 23547.
- [Beam Suntory](beam-suntory.md) — A multinational spirits company formed when Japan's Suntory Holdings bought the American Beam Inc in 2014; it places American bourbon, Canadian whisky, Scotch and Japanese whisky under one group, runs its operating base from Chicago, and takes its Chinese name from the two existing names Beam and Suntory.
- [Brown-Forman](brown-forman.md) — A spirits company founded in Louisville, Kentucky in 1870, centred on Jack Daniel's, Old Forester and Woodford Reserve; the founding family retains control through dual-class shares, and in 2024 the company completed the Sonoma-Cutrer sale and exited its Duckhorn stake.
- [Yantai Changyu Pioneer Wine Co., Ltd.](changyu.md) — A wine producer in Yantai, Shandong. Zhang Bishi founded the Changyu winemaking company in 1892; in 1997 a joint-stock company was established by public offer and listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange, followed by an A-share listing in 2000.
- [Hubei Daohuaxiang Distillery](daohuaxiang.md) — A baijiu producer in Longquan, Yiling District, Yichang, Hubei, registered as Hubei Daohuaxiang Distillery Co., Ltd.; it is a non-listed joint-stock company controlled by natural-person shareholders, with Cai Kaiyun as chairman, built around strong-aroma baijiu and extended into packaging and logistics.
- [Diageo](diageo.md) — A multinational drinks group formed in 1997 by the merger of Guinness and Grand Metropolitan, headquartered in London and owning brands such as Johnnie Walker, Smirnoff, Baileys, Tanqueray and Guinness.
- [Guizhou Dongjiu Co., Ltd.](dongjiu.md) — A baijiu producer in Donggongsi, Zunyi, Guizhou. Dongjiu is a dong-aroma (also called medicinal-aroma) baijiu; production resumed in 1957, it joined China's old eight famous liquors in 1963, and a 2008 Guizhou local standard named it the representative of dong-aroma.
- [Dynasty](dynasty.md) — The continuation of a Sino-French joint-venture winery founded in Tianjin in 1980, now the Hong Kong-listed Dynasty Fine Wines group under stock code 828; as of 30 June 2025 Tianjin Food Group held 42.80% and Rémy Cointreau Asia Pacific 23.90%.
- [Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Group Co., Ltd.](fenjiu-group.md) — A wholly state-owned company in Xinghuacun, Fenyang, Shanxi, restructured in March 2002. Its listed arm is Shanxi Xinghuacun Fenjiu Distillery Co., Ltd. (600809). This page separates group, listed company and brand.
- [COFCO Greatwall Wine Co., Ltd.](greatwall.md) — A domestic wine platform managed by COFCO and operating under the Greatwall brand, with production and sourcing bases in the Shacheng-Huaizhuo basin of Hebei, the eastern foothills of Helan Mountain in Ningxia and Jieshishan in Qinhuangdao; the producer of a specific bottle should be checked on its label.
- [Anhui Gujing Distillery Co., Ltd.](gujing-distillery.md) — A baijiu producer based in Gujing Town, Bozhou, Anhui, and the production entity behind the Gujing Gongjiu brand. This page separates company identity, brand and specific products.
- [Beijing Red Star Co., Ltd.](hongxing.md) — A baijiu producer in Huairou, Beijing, whose predecessor, the Huabei Liquor Monopoly Experimental Factory, was founded in May 1949; it was the first state-run distillery in Beijing and is known for Red Star Erguotou and the traditional Beijing er-guotou craft.
- [Huanghelou Distillery](huanghelou.md) — The legal entity is Wuhan Tianlong Huanghelou Distillery Co., Ltd., with sites in Wuhan, Xianning and Suizhou. In May 2016 Gujing Gongjiu acquired 51% for RMB 816 million and took control; the company is mainly a light-aroma producer but also makes strong-aroma products.
- [Kuaijishan Shaoxing Rice Wine Co., Ltd.](huijishan.md) — A huangjiu producer in Shaoxing, Zhejiang, whose origins trace to the Yunji winery founded in 1743; it is one of the representative producers of traditional Shaoxing wine and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in 2014.
- [Sichuan Jiannanchun (Group) Co., Ltd.](jiannanchun-group.md) — A baijiu group based in Mianzhu, Sichuan, registered in 1996. Its core brand Jiannanchun is a multi-grain strong-aroma baijiu, and the group was restructured in 2003 when state capital withdrew and management and employees took shares.
- [Jiugui Liquor Co., Ltd.](jiuguijiu.md) — A fuyu-aroma baijiu producer in Jishou, Hunan. Its predecessor, the Jishou distillery, was founded in 1956; the company listed on the Shenzhen Stock Exchange in 1997 and became a COFCO group member in 2016.
- [Anhui Kouzi Distillery Co., Ltd.](kouzi.md) — A Jianxiang-aroma baijiu producer in Huaibei, Anhui. It listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in June 2015 and was the first listed Jianxiang-aroma baijiu company, with brands including Kouzijiao and Lao Kouzi.
- [Sichuan Langjiu Group Co., Ltd.](langjiu-group.md) — A baijiu group based in Erlang, Gulin, Sichuan, on the left bank of the Chishui River. It was registered in 1998, and its core brand Langjiu is a sauce-aroma baijiu. Around 2002 the Baoguang group acquired control, and the company became privately owned.
- [Hebei Hengshui Laobaigan Liquor Co., Ltd.](laobaigan.md) — A Laobaigan-aroma baijiu producer in Hengshui, Hebei. It originates from the state-run Hengshui distillery set up in 1946 and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in October 2002, with brands including Hengshui Laobaigan, Bancheng Shaoguo, Wuling and Wenwang Gongjiu.
- [Luzhou Laojiao Group Co., Ltd.](luzhou-laojiao-group.md) — A wholly state-owned enterprise group in Luzhou, Sichuan, founded in December 2000 and ultimately controlled by the Luzhou municipal SASAC; as of the end of the third quarter of 2026 it held 26.05% of Luzhou Laojiao Co., Ltd. (stock code 000568), whose controlling shareholder it is.
- [LVMH](lvmh.md) — A French luxury group formed in 1987 by the merger of Moët Hennessy and Louis Vuitton, headquartered in Paris; its drinks business is run by Moët Hennessy, 34% of which is held by Diageo, and in 2025 wines and spirits revenue was EUR 5.358 billion, down 5% organic.
- [Beijing Shunxin Agriculture Niulanshan Distillery](niulanshan.md) — A baijiu producer in Niulanshan town, Shunyi district, Beijing. Its predecessor, the state-run Niulanshan distillery, was founded in 1952; it is now a branch of Shunxin Agriculture and is known for er-guotou craft and products such as Niulanshan Chenniang.
- [Pernod Ricard](pernod-ricard.md) — A multinational wine and spirits group formed in 1975 by the merger of two French anise-flavoured spirits houses, Pernod and Ricard; headquartered in Paris and owning brands such as Absolut, Chivas Regal, Ballantine's and Martell.
- [Shede Spirits Co., Ltd.](shed.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu producer in Tuopai, Shehong, Sichuan. Its history traces to the 1940 Tai'an workshop; a state-run Tuopai distillery was set up in 1952, the joint-stock company was registered in 1993 and listed in 1996.
- [Sichuan Swellfun Co., Ltd.](swellfun.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu producer in Chengdu, Sichuan. It listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange on 6 December 1996; its parent is Sichuan Chengdu Swellfun Group, and Diageo plc is its actual controller.
- [Zhejiang Taipai Shaoxing Rice Wine Co., Ltd.](taipai.md) — A huangjiu producer in Shaoxing, Zhejiang, making Shaoxing wine under the Taipai brand; public material presents handmade winter brewing and export markets as its main features, within the Shaoxing geographical-indication system.
- [Tsingtao Brewery Company Limited](tsingtao.md) — A brewery based in Qingdao, Shandong. Its predecessor, the Germania Brewery Company Qingdao, was founded by German and British merchants in 1903; the joint-stock company was registered in 1993 and its H shares and A shares were listed the same year.
- [Sichuan Yibin Wuliangye Group Co., Ltd.](wuliangye-group.md) — A baijiu group based in Yibin, Sichuan, formed in 1998 from the Yibin Wuliangye Distillery. Its listed arm is Yibin Wuliangye Co., Ltd. This page separates group, listed company, brand and product.
- [Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Group Co., Ltd.](xifeng-group.md) — A baijiu producer in Baoji, Shaanxi. Its predecessor, the state-run Shaanxi Xifeng Distillery, was founded in 1956; the company became a joint-stock company in 1999 and was renamed a group company in 2008. Its core product Xifengjiu is a feng-aroma baijiu.
- [Guizhou Xijiu Co., Ltd.](xijiu.md) — A baijiu producer in Xijiu town, Xishui county, Guizhou, on the middle Chishui River. Its origins reach back to a Ming workshop; the state enterprise was formed in 1952, it joined the Moutai group in 1998, and it became a joint-stock company through a whole-entity conversion in 2022.
- [Jiangsu Yanghe Group Co., Ltd.](yanghe-group.md) — A baijiu group based in Suqian, Jiangsu, with public registration showing a May 1997 founding. Its listed arm is Jiangsu Yanghe Distillery Co., Ltd. (002304), listed in 2009.
- [Anhui Yingjia Distillery Co., Ltd.](yingjia.md) — A strong-aroma baijiu producer in Huoshan, Anhui. The joint-stock company was founded in 2003 and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in May 2015; its public positioning is ecological brewing.
