# Qinghualang

Qinghualang is a high-end sauce-aroma product from Sichuan Langjiu, commonly 53% ABV in 500 mL and made in Erlang town, Gulin county, Sichuan, on the left bank of the Chishui River. It belongs to the sauce-aroma route of [baijiu](ref:baijiu). Since 2021 the company has described its position as "left bank of the Chishui River, estate sauce-aroma spirit" and has promoted a fifth generation nationwide.

## Definition and position

Qinghualang is a product name, not a region and not an aroma type. It is made within the [Langjiu group](ref:producer) system and carried by the [Langjiu brand](ref:brand). The brand also includes Honghualang and other sauce-aroma products plus strong-aroma and mixed-aroma lines, so the performance of Qinghualang should not be applied wholesale to "Langjiu style".

The product sits in the high-end price band and carries brand image and business-gifting functions. The "blue-and-white" in the name refers to packaging and visual language, not to blue-and-white porcelain as a craft and not to the body or the age of the spirit. Understanding a bottle still comes back to aroma type, strength and batch.

## Producer and brand

The production system is in Erlang town, Gulin county, Luzhou, Sichuan. The company's public chronology records 1957 as the resumption of production, after which the product structure grew into sauce aroma, strong aroma and mixed aroma. The group level holds brands and assets, while the joint-stock company handles production, sales and disclosure; the two should not be treated as one entity.

Langjiu's sauce-aroma products store base spirit in natural caves, and Tianbao Cave and Dibao Cave are commonly named storage sites. Temperature and humidity inside are relatively stable, so spirit in clay jars changes slowly in a steadier environment. Cave storage is a storage condition, not a guarantee of age; the effect still depends on base quality, container and time.

## Origin and raw materials

Erlang town lies in southern [Sichuan](ref:sichuan) on the left bank of the Chishui River. The valley is deeply cut, hot and humid in summer and relatively mild in winter, conditions that suit open qu making, pile fermentation and long storage. Water and geological conditions in the Chishui valley are often treated by local producers as part of the brewing environment.

Sauce-aroma products use sorghum as the raw material and wheat for high-temperature qu. [Sorghum](ref:sorghum) must withstand repeated steaming, and glutinous sorghum with a high proportion of amylopectin is better suited; after wheat is made into qu and piled and turned, it forms a community of bacteria, moulds and yeasts that supplies enzymes and aroma precursors. Ratios and the qu culture are company processes.

## Brewing process

The public route for sauce-aroma baijiu is usually summarised as one production cycle a year, two rounds of grain addition, nine steamings, eight fermentations and seven spirit draws. Base spirit from each round differs in style, is stored separately and is then blended into the finished product. High-temperature qu, pile fermentation and multi-round drawing together build the composite aroma.

[Qu making](ref:qu-making) sets qu aroma and fermentation power; during [distillation](ref:distillation) the run is cut by fraction, with head and tail separated and the middle run used as the main base. New spirit is stored by round and grade, and blending unifies aroma, body and finish. Blending is not simple addition, nor can dilution fix a structural problem. Round ratios, storage periods and the use of older spirit are company information, and this entry describes only the public framework.

## Specification and label

The common specification is 53% ABV in 500 mL; gift boxes, full cases and different generations may vary in packaging and volume. Strength is shown as [alcohol by volume](ref:abv), where 53% ABV means 53 mL of ethanol in 100 mL of liquid at 20°C. The product standard for sauce-aroma baijiu is commonly GB/T 26760, and the bottle label governs.

The producer, product name, aroma type, strength, volume, standard and batch on the label are the basics for identity. Fifth-generation products, commemorative editions and channel editions should be distinguished by label; "estate" and "cave-stored" describe production and storage systems, not the exact age of the liquid. Price information is highly time-sensitive, and this entry gives no figures.

## Flavour and tasting

Qinghualang typically shows a pronounced sauce aroma with roasted, baked and floral-fruit notes, a relatively soft entry, a full body, an opening mid-palate and a long finish. The 53% ABV strength is noticeable, and the assessment should look at whether the body carries the alcohol and whether the finish is clean rather than only at how "hot" it feels.

Taste from a small cup: smell at rest, then gently swirl and smell again. Let the liquid stay in the mouth and note the order of sauce aroma, roasted notes and sweetness. Comparison with sauce-aroma products in the same price band is useful, but keep the same temperature, cup and approximate vintage; otherwise the conclusion is unreliable. Ice or heavy dilution is not the traditional way to drink it and will flatten aroma and change structure.

## History and market

The company's public chronology records 1957 as the resumption of production and notes that the brewing tradition around Erlangtan goes back further. Around 2017 Qinghualang was promoted as "one of China's two major sauce-aroma baijiu"; in 2021 the positioning was upgraded to "left bank of the Chishui River, estate sauce-aroma spirit", followed by promotion of the fifth generation. Positioning changes reflect corporate strategy, not a change in the product standard.

Qinghualang has long sat in the high-end price band and is a common choice for business banquets and gifting. Prices change with year, channel and supply and demand, and this entry gives no figures. Treating an advertising position as proof of quality, or a packaging generation as a body upgrade, are common misreadings; generational differences should be checked against company information and the label.

## Buying and storage

Prefer official channels and check anti-counterfeit marks, producer, batch and generation. Gift boxes and channel editions should be checked against the label, and packaging differences should not be mistaken for body differences. For full cases, check seals, production dates and storage conditions; a price far below the usual range should first raise questions about source and authenticity.

For Qinghualang, unopened baijiu should be stored upright, away from light, in a cool place without strong odours. Once opened, drink it soon; prolonged exposure loses aroma. Home storage cannot reproduce distillery or cave conditions, so purchase year should not be equated with quality gain.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating Qinghualang as the whole Langjiu brand. The brand has several series and aroma types.
Second, treating "blue-and-white" as a body feature. It mainly refers to packaging.
Third, treating cave storage as a guarantee of age. It is a storage condition.
Fourth, treating an advertising position as a quality standard. It is communication language.
Fifth, judging quality from packaging generations. Generational differences need official explanation.
