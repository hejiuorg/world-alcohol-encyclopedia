# Guizhou Xijiu Co., Ltd.

Guizhou Xijiu Co., Ltd. is based in Xijiu town, Xishui county, Guizhou, with its plant around the Erlangtan ferry crossing on the middle Chishui River. Its origins reach back to a Yin-family baijiu workshop of the Ming Wanli era. The state enterprise was formed through an acquisition in 1952, the company joined the Moutai group in 1998, and in 2022 it was converted as a whole into a joint-stock company. Its core products are sauce-aroma [baijiu](ref:baijiu).

## Company identity

This page records the producer layer: company name, registered place, founding date and organisational form. The joint-stock company handles production, sales and disclosure, while the holding-group layer involves investment and industry layout. Public material assigns 1952 to state formation, 1998 to joining the Moutai group and 2022 to the whole-entity conversion; these three points cannot be merged into one founding year.

When discussing "Xijiu", first establish whether the subject is the joint-stock company, the holding group, the brand or a bottle. Registry entries, official profiles and SASAC pages do not use identical names or definitions, so establish the entity before checking a figure.

## Site and region

Xijiu town lies in northern [Guizhou](ref:guizhou), on the middle Chishui River. The valley is deeply cut, hot in summer and humid, which suits the open qu making and multi-round fermentation of sauce-aroma baijiu. Local sorghum, water and the valley's microbial environment together form the base of the style. The Erlangtan ferry area is part of the Chishui sauce-aroma belt.

A site in Xijiu town does not mean every product is filled in the same workshop. Product lines, production bases and contract arrangements should be checked against the production address and the standard on the label. Regional conditions are external factors only and cannot replace checks on a specific product.

## Brands and products

Xijiu is the core brand, with sauce-aroma baijiu as its main line. In public product material, Junpin Xijiu is positioned at the high end and Jiaocang 1988 is a widely distributed core item; strength, volume, standards and prices for different series change by year, so buyers should use the current label and official channels.

The number in "Jiaocang 1988" is part of the product name, not the age of the liquid in the bottle. The actual age, base-liquor composition and blending ratio should be checked against company information and cannot be inferred from the name alone.

## Craft and style

The basic sauce-aroma route is: sorghum as the raw material, qu made at the Dragon Boat Festival, grain added at the Double Ninth Festival, then [qu making](ref:qu-making), high-temperature stacking, multi-round fermentation and fractionated [distillation](ref:distillation), followed by graded storage and blending. The amylopectin and tannin content of [sorghum](ref:sorghum) affects yield and aroma precursors, while clay-jar storage lets the body oxidise and integrate slowly.

A process description states the style route only and cannot replace a judgement about a specific product. Products at different grades from the same company may differ in raw-material grade, round ratio, storage time and blending plan; the standard and grade on the label are more worth checking than promotional language.

## History

In 1952 local authorities acquired private workshops and formed a state enterprise, bringing Xijiu into factory production. In October 1998 the company joined the Moutai group as a wholly owned subsidiary and expanded capacity and product lines with group support. In 2022 Guizhou Xijiu Co., Ltd. was created by converting Guizhou Moutai Distillery (Group) Xijiu Co., Ltd. as a whole; its controlling shareholder is Guizhou Xijiu Investment Holding Group.

"Formation", "joining" and "whole-entity conversion" are events of different kinds. Brand narrative can be traced back to a Ming workshop, but that is cultural lineage, not the continuous production history of the modern company.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating 1952 as the founding year of the joint-stock company. State formation and joint-stock establishment are different points.
Second, treating the 1998 joining of the Moutai group as today's ownership. The whole-entity conversion took place in 2022, and the controlling shareholder is Xijiu Investment Holding Group.
Third, treating "Jiaocang 1988" as an age statement. The number is part of the product name.
Fourth, treating the Chishui region as a guarantee of quality. The region is only one condition.
Fifth, conflating the group, the joint-stock company and the brand. Their legal identities differ.
