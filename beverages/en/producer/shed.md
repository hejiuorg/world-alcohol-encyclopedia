# Shede Spirits Co., Ltd.

Shede Spirits Co., Ltd. is a strong-aroma baijiu producer in Tuopai, Shehong, Sichuan. Its history traces to the Tai'an workshop founded by a local merchant in 1940; in 1952 the Shehong county government bought the workshop and set up the state-run Sichuan Tuopai Qujiu Distillery. The joint-stock company was registered in 1993 and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in 1996. The company owns two baijiu brands, Tuopai and Shede.![Shede Spirits Co., Ltd. category image](/media/photos/drink-style-light-aroma-baijiu-1c3af236.jpg)

## Company identity

This producer records the producer layer: company name, registered place, founding date and organisational form. The joint-stock company is the listed entity and handles production, sales and disclosure; the brand is a market mark, and a product is a specific bottle. When discussing "Shede", first establish whether the subject is the company, the brand or a bottle.

Public material uses different starting points for "origin": the corporate history goes back to the 1940 Tai'an workshop, the state-run distillery dates from 1952, the joint-stock company was registered in 1993 and the listing followed in 1996. This producer uses 1993 for the founding year and lists the other points in the history section.

## Site and region

Shehong lies in the hilly central part of the Sichuan Basin, in the Fujiang river catchment, with a mild, humid climate and distinct seasons. Sichuan is one of the core producing regions for strong-aroma baijiu, and Yibin, Luzhou, Mianzhu, Shehong and Chengdu each have different climate, soil and water conditions that shape different house styles.

A site in Shehong does not mean every product uses the same workshop, the same fermentation pit or the same batch of base liquor. Product lines differ in raw materials, strength, storage time and blending, and specific information should be taken from the label and public company material.

## Brands and products

The company owns the Tuopai and Shede brands. Tuopai serves mass-market and banquet settings, with products such as Tuopai Teji and Tuopai Qujiu; Shede targets the mid-to-high end and business gifting, with products such as Pinwei Shede, Zhihui Shede and the collectible Shede series.

The two brands share a production system but differ in positioning, price band and channel. Buyers should check the brand, product name, strength, net content, ingredients, producer and standard on the label rather than the word "Shede" alone.

The brand-to-product relation is one-to-many: one brand has different years, volumes, strengths and exhibition versions, and prices change accordingly.

## Craft and style

The typical strong-aroma route is: multi-grain or single-grain blending, qu making, solid-state fermentation in mud pits, continued use of the fermented grain, basket distillation, fractional collection, ceramic-jar storage and blending. The maturity of the pits, the quality of the qu, the fermentation cycle and storage time all affect aroma and mouthfeel.

"Old liquor" is a selling point the company emphasises: base liquor and blending liquor stored for a certain number of years. The proportion of old liquor affects body and harmony, but "old liquor" is not a legal grade, and the exact age and ratio are part of the company's formula and are usually not fully disclosed on the label.

For Shede Spirits Co., Ltd., a process description states a style route only and cannot replace a judgement about a specific product. Products at different prices and in different series within the same company may differ in base-liquor grade, storage time and blending ratio.

## History

In 1940 a local merchant, Li Mingfang, bought an old workshop in Tuopai, Shehong, and founded the Tai'an workshop. In 1952 the Shehong county government bought the workshop and set up the state-run Sichuan Tuopai Qujiu Distillery. On 28 July 1993 the company was approved to establish a joint-stock company by private placement and registered with the Shehong county administration for industry and commerce; it listed in 1996 as the third listed company in the baijiu industry. In 2009 it received the China Quality Award, becoming the third baijiu company to do so.

These points describe the shift from workshop to state-run distillery to listed company, but 1940 is not the year the joint-stock company was founded, and an award is not proof of quality for every product.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating 1940 as the year the joint-stock company was founded. 1940 is the start of the Tai'an workshop.
Second, treating Shede and Tuopai as the same brand. They differ in positioning, price band and channel.
Third, treating "old liquor" as a legal grade. It is a company description, and age and ratio should be checked against public material.
Fourth, treating the China Quality Award as a quality certificate for a single product. It concerns corporate quality management.
Fifth, treating the Shehong site as the raw-material origin of every product. Use the producer and origin on the label.
