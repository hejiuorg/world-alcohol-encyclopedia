# Moutai 1935

Moutai 1935 is a [sauce-aroma baijiu](ref:baijiu) product released by Guizhou Moutai in 2022, commonly 53% ABV and 500 mL, produced in Renhuai, Guizhou. The 1935 in the name points to a historical year in the brand narrative, and its exact meaning should be checked against company statements; batch, packaging and anti-counterfeit information should be checked on the label and through official channels.![Moutai 1935 category image](/media/photos/drink-style-light-aroma-baijiu-1c3af236.jpg)

## Definition and position

Moutai 1935 is a specific product, not another name for [Moutai liquor](ref:moutai) and not a general term for the Moutai range. Moutai liquor is the company's premium core product, while Moutai 1935 is a product extending into the next price band, and the two differ in price, channels and output.

By category it belongs to the sauce-aroma type within [baijiu](ref:baijiu), made mainly from sorghum and wheat. The year in the product name is not bottle age and cannot be used to infer how long the base liquor was stored; judging a specific wine means checking the label, batch and company information.

## Origin and ingredients

The product is made in Renhuai, in the sauce-aroma baijiu region of the Chishui River basin in [Guizhou](ref:guizhou). The local climate is warm and humid, and the valley terrain and water conditions provide the basis for production; different plants and workshops within the region still have micro-climatic differences.

The main ingredients are [sorghum](ref:sorghum) and [wheat](ref:wheat). Sorghum supplies the starch and flavour precursors for fermentation, while wheat is used to make qu. Variety, origin and grade of the raw materials affect fermentation performance, but the detailed formula and proportions are company process information that labels may not fully disclose.

## Qu-making and fermentation

Sauce-aroma baijiu uses high-temperature qu, and [qu-making](ref:qu-making) is the key step that sets the flavour skeleton. Qu blocks are cultured in a hot environment, and the microbial community and enzymes act together in later fermentation; qu-making temperature, turning timing and storage time are process details.

Fermentation uses a solid-state, multi-round approach, with grain and qu undergoing several rounds of [fermentation](ref:fermentation) in pits or fermentation facilities. Differences in temperature, humidity and handling between rounds create different layers of aroma and taste, which is one reason sauce-aroma baijiu is flavour-complex.

## Distillation and blending

After fermentation the material is [distilled](ref:distillation), with liquor collected separately by round and by cut. Liquor from different rounds and cuts differs markedly in aroma and taste and must be stored separately before [blending](ref:blending).

Blending is not simple mixing: it uses liquor from different rounds and years to adjust aroma, taste and structure. Company material usually describes the process route without disclosing full proportions, and blending should not be understood as adding external flavourings.

## Specification and label

The common format is a 500 mL bottle at 53% ABV, as sauce-aroma baijiu. The label usually carries the product name, strength, net volume, producer, origin and standard; buyers should check anti-counterfeit marks and batch information.

Packaging and gifts may differ between channels and batches, and packaging differences do not equal differences in the liquid. The 1935 in the product name is brand narrative, not a vintage statement or a quality grade.

## Flavour and tasting

The aroma is led by sauce aroma, roast and baked notes, often with layers of flowers, fruit and nuts; the entry is mellow, the body fairly full, and the finish long. Batches differ in aroma intensity and body weight, and a short rest after opening helps the aroma open.

Serve at about 18-22°C in small cups, slowly. Too cold suppresses aroma, while too warm accentuates alcohol. Drink with water and food, and avoid drinking quickly on an empty stomach.

## Drinking and pairing

Moutai 1935 suits the hotter, spicier dishes of Sichuan and Hunan cooking, and also works with barbecue, braised food and nuts. Its high strength and rich aroma call for small, slow sips rather than large quick drinks.

After opening, close the cap tightly, keep out of light and finish within a short period. Baijiu loses aroma with prolonged air contact, and repeated opening accelerates flavour change; if a bottle is not finished, decant into a smaller bottle to reduce air contact.

## Buying and storage

Buy through official channels or authorised dealers and check anti-counterfeit marks, batch and packaging integrity. Products in the next price band are prone to price swings and channel differences, and a promotional price does not represent long-term positioning.

For Moutai 1935, unopened baijiu should be stored upright, away from light, in a cool place, avoiding heat and strong odours. High-strength baijiu is not suited to long refrigeration; home storage should pay attention to sealing and moisture, and the year of purchase should not be equated simply with an improvement in quality.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating Moutai 1935 as a substitute for Moutai liquor. The two differ in positioning, price and output.

Second, treating "1935" as bottle age. It is a year in the brand narrative.

Third, treating sauce aroma as the only baijiu aroma type. Baijiu also includes strong-aroma, light-aroma and mixed-aroma types.

Fourth, judging quality by price alone. Channel, batch and storage condition matter equally.

Fifth, treating high strength as a quality guarantee. Alcohol content is not flavour and balance.
