# Sichuan Jiannanchun (Group) Co., Ltd.

Sichuan Jiannanchun (Group) Co., Ltd. is based in Mianzhu, Sichuan, and is a corporate group whose main business is [baijiu](ref:baijiu). Public company-registry information shows that it was registered in September 1996; in 2003 it completed a restructuring in which state capital withdrew and management and employees took shares. The core brand Jiannanchun is a [strong-aroma baijiu](ref:strong-aroma), and representative products are covered under [Jiannanchun](ref:jiannanchun) and [Crystal Jiannanchun](ref:crystal-jiannanchun).

## Company identity

This producer records the producer layer: group name, registered place, founding date and organisational form. The group is the holding platform for the brand and assets, while production, sales and product specifications are handled by subsidiary companies. When discussing "Jiannanchun", first establish whether the subject is the group, the brand or a bottle; the names are the same, but their legal identities and duties differ.

Public registry information gives the registered address as Chunyi Street, Mianzhu, Sichuan, and the registration date as 17 September 1996. Registered capital and similar figures change with later registrations, so this producer does not treat them as permanent facts.

## Site and region

Mianzhu lies at the north-western edge of the Sichuan Basin, below the Longmen Mountains, and is administered by Deyang. The climate is mild, water and grain supplies are stable, and the area is one of Sichuan's traditional strong-aroma baijiu regions. Within the wider map of [China](ref:china)'s baijiu regions, Mianzhu belongs to the Sichuan strong-aroma belt together with Yibin, Luzhou, Shehong and other places.

A site in Mianzhu does not mean every bottle is filled in the same workshop. Product lines, contract manufacturing and production-base arrangements should be checked against the production address and the standard printed on the label. Regional advantage comes from water, fermentation pits, the microbial environment and long practice, not from a geographical label alone.

## Brands and products

Jiannanchun is the group's core brand, and its main product line is multi-grain strong-aroma baijiu. In public product material, Crystal Jiannanchun is a widely distributed core item; higher-priced products are distinguished by cellar storage time, vintage or limited-release concepts. Product lines, strength, volume and suggested retail prices change by year, so buyers should use the current label and official channel information.

Brand history often traces the name back to "Jiannan Shaochun" of the Tang dynasty and cites the relevant record in the Old Book of Tang. This is brand and cultural narrative: it shows the link between the name and local distilling history, but it is not the continuous production age of a modern plant and cannot be converted directly into the age of the liquid in a bottle.

## Craft and style

The basic multi-grain strong-aroma route is: sorghum, rice, glutinous rice, wheat and maize are blended in proportion, then taken through [qu making](ref:qu-making), solid-state fermentation in mud pits, continued mash recycling, distillation, fraction cutting, storage in clay jars and blending. A multi-grain recipe adds aromatic layers, while the microbial community in the mud pits contributes to the main strong-aroma flavour compounds.

A process description only states the style route; it cannot replace a judgement about a specific product. Products at different grades from the same company may differ in raw-material grade, fermentation length, fraction cutting and storage time. The standard and quality grade on the label are more worth checking than promotional language.

## History

In September 1996 the company was registered on the basis of the former Mianzhu Jiannanchun Distillery. In 2003, under provincial and local requirements for an orderly withdrawal of state capital, the enterprise was restructured: trademarks and other intangible assets remained with the government, the remaining state capital withdrew, and a structure of management control with employee shareholding was formed. The holding and exit arrangements for employee shares later produced several disputes, and in 2012 employee shareholders stopped work.

In 2025 financial media reported that state capital had taken a stake again. Ownership changes with transactions, so specific percentages should be checked against the latest registry and company announcements; this producer does not treat any single point in time as final.

## Common misconceptions

First, conflating the group, the brand and the product. Their legal identities and duties differ.
Second, treating the Tang-dynasty record of "Jiannan Shaochun" as the founding year of the modern plant. Cultural narrative and production history are different things.
Third, treating the registered place as the origin of every product. Use the production address on the label.
Fourth, treating "multi-grain" as a guarantee of quality. The recipe is only a style route; grade and process execution decide the result.
Fifth, using the ownership structure of one year to explain the company today. Ownership changes, so use the latest registry information.
