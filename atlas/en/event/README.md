- [Oktoberfest: a folk festival that grew out of an 1810 wedding](oktoberfest.md) — Oktoberfest is not a simple beer exhibition but Munich’s annual folk festival on the Theresienwiese. It began on 12 October 1810 with the wedding of Crown Prince Ludwig and Princess Therese, continued as a horse race, and is now maintained by the City of Munich and Bavarian breweries. The 2026 edition ran from 19 September to 4 October and is over.
- [Fête des Vignerons: a wine festival held once in a generation](fete-des-vignerons.md) — The Fête des Vignerons in Vevey is organised by the Confrérie des Vignerons, first staged in its “true” festival form in 1797 and then held roughly once a generation, with intervals of 14 to 28 years. It turns vine-growing, harvest, winemaking and local life into an open-air spectacle and was inscribed on UNESCO’s Intangible Cultural Heritage list in 2016. The most recent edition ran from 18 July to 11 August 2019.
- [Tales of the Cocktail: from a bar walk to the drinks industry’s annual meeting](tales-of-the-cocktail.md) — Tales of the Cocktail began in New Orleans in 2002 as Ann Tuennerman’s walking tour of historic bars and grew into a global cocktail and spirits conference. The 24th edition ran from 19 to 24 July 2026 in New Orleans, with education, tastings, networking and the Spirited Awards. It is neither a pure drinking party nor a government-run trade show.
- [ProWein: Düsseldorf in March, the trading floor for wine and spirits](prowein.md) — ProWein was first held in Düsseldorf in 1994 under the name ProVins, with 321 exhibitors, 1,517 visitors and a single hall. It is now Europe’s leading trade fair for wine and spirits and is open only to trade visitors. The 2027 edition is scheduled for 7–9 March at Messe Düsseldorf. It is not a consumer tasting or a regional festival.
- [Vinitaly: Verona’s annual showcase for Italian wine](vinitaly.md) — Vinitaly first appeared in Verona on 22–23 September 1967 as the Italian Wine Days, became a full trade fair in 1971 and gained “international” status in 1978. Today it is organised by Veronafiere and is the central trade event for Italian wine and spirits. The 58th edition ran from 12 to 15 April 2026 at Veronafiere and is over.
- [Great American Beer Festival: from a 1982 hotel gathering to an open-air beer festival](great-american-beer-festival.md) — The Great American Beer Festival was first held in 1982 at the Harvest House in Boulder, Colorado, with only 24 breweries, 47 beers and 800 attendees. Organised by the Brewers Association, it is one of the United States’ most important craft-beer festivals and competitions. The 2026 edition ran on 10–11 October at Denver’s Levitt Pavilion, the first outdoor edition, and is over.
