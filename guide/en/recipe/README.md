# Recipes

[返回English目录](../README.md)

- [Aperol Spritz recipe (IBA-approved version)](aperol-spritz.md) — 90 ml Prosecco, 60 ml Aperol and 30 ml soda water built over ice in a large stemmed glass and garnished with an orange slice; this page covers the 1919 origin, the pouring order, what the soda actually does and the low-alcohol trap.
- [Cosmopolitan recipe (IBA version)](cosmopolitan.md) — 40 ml citron vodka, 15 ml Cointreau, 15 ml fresh lime juice and 30 ml cranberry juice make the four-part Cosmopolitan ratio; this page explains the 1980s pink vogue, the sweetness and acid of cranberry juice, shaking and straining, and the lemon-peel aroma.
- [Daiquiri recipe (IBA version)](daiquiri.md) — 60 ml Cuban white rum, 20 ml fresh lime juice and two bar spoons of fine sugar, stirred to dissolve the sugar and then shaken with ice, strained into a chilled cocktail glass with no garnish in the IBA version; this page explains each step of a very simple structure.
- [Dry Martini recipe (IBA version)](dry-martini.md) — 60 ml gin and 10 ml dry vermouth stirred over ice and strained into a chilled martini glass, garnished with lemon peel or a green olive; this page explains how dryness, stirring, ice and glass shape decide the result.
- [Gin Fizz recipe (IBA version)](gin-fizz.md) — 45 ml gin, 30 ml fresh lemon juice and 10 ml sugar syrup are shaken and strained before a splash of soda water goes in; this page explains why soda comes last, the dry 45:30:10 frame, the egg-white Silver Fizz and the ways carbonation is lost.
- [Mai Tai recipe (IBA version)](mai-tai.md) — 30 ml amber Jamaican rum plus 30 ml Martinique molasses rum, with 15 ml orange curaçao, 15 ml orgeat, 30 ml lime juice and 7.5 ml sugar syrup; this page covers the two-rum division of labour, the tiki background and the pour-with-ice method.
- [Manhattan recipe (IBA version)](manhattan.md) — 50 ml rye whiskey, 20 ml sweet vermouth and 1 dash of Angostura bitters, stirred and strained into a chilled cocktail glass; this page covers the 5:2 frame, what a single dash does, why the drink is stirred and how dilution and vermouth freshness decide the result.
- [Margarita recipe (IBA version)](margarita.md) — 50 ml 100% agave tequila, 20 ml orange liqueur and 15 ml fresh lime juice, shaken and strained into a chilled glass with an optional half salt rim; this page explains how the sweet-sour balance, the salt rim and dilution decide the drink.
- [Mint Julep recipe (IBA version)](mint-julep.md) — 60 ml bourbon, 4 fresh mint sprigs, 1 tsp powdered sugar and 2 tsp water, gently pressed in the cup and stirred with crushed ice until the cup frosts; this page covers why crushed ice is the body, the pressing technique, the frost signal and the racing tradition.
- [Mojito recipe (IBA version)](mojito.md) — 45 ml Cuban white rum, 20 ml fresh lime juice, six mint sprigs, two teaspoons of white cane sugar and soda water, built in the glass and garnished with mint and lime; this page explains the order of pressing, dissolving, ice and bubbles.
- [Negroni recipe (IBA version)](negroni.md) — 30 ml each of gin, Campari and sweet vermouth, stirred and strained into a rocks glass over ice, garnished with orange peel; this page explains ratio, technique, equipment and substitution limits.
- [Old Fashioned recipe (IBA version)](old-fashioned.md) — 45 ml bourbon or rye whisky, one sugar cube, a couple of dashes of Angostura bitters and a little water, built in a rocks glass and garnished with orange peel or a slice and a cocktail cherry; this page explains the ratio, the method and the limits of substitution.
- [Sazerac recipe (IBA cognac version)](sazerac.md) — 50 ml cognac, 10 ml absinthe for the rinse, 1 sugar cube and 2 dashes of Peychaud's bitters, stirred and strained into a chilled rocks glass with lemon peel; this page explains the rinse, the sugar work, the frozen glass and the choice of bitters.
- [Singapore Sling recipe (IBA proportions, built)](singapore-sling.md) — 30 ml gin, 15 ml cherry brandy, 7.5 ml each of Cointreau and Bénédictine, 120 ml pineapple juice, 15 ml lime juice, 10 ml grenadine and 1 dash of Angostura bitters, built in a highball glass and garnished with pineapple and cherry.
- [Whiskey Sour recipe (IBA version)](whiskey-sour.md) — The Whiskey Sour is built on 45 ml bourbon, 25 ml fresh lemon juice and 20 ml sugar syrup, with egg white optional; this page covers the dry shake, the aroma of fresh lemon, how far to shake, glass and ice, and the departures common at the bar.
- [Zombie recipe (IBA version)](zombie.md) — 45 ml Jamaican dark rum, 45 ml gold Puerto Rican rum and 30 ml demerara rum with lime juice, falernum, Donn's Mix, grenadine, bitters and pastis, shaken with crushed ice and poured into a highball; this page explains the three-rum split, the two-per-customer legend and the crushed-ice trade-off.
