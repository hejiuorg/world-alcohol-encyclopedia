# Xijiu

Xijiu is a sauce-aroma baijiu brand from Xishui, Guizhou, held by [Guizhou Xijiu Co., Ltd.](ref:producer). Its plant sits by the Erlangtan stretch of the middle Chishui River, and the brand covers price bands from everyday banquets to high-end gifting. Key products include Junpin Xijiu and Xijiu Jiaocang 1988.

## Brand definition

A brand and a producer are two different entities. The brand answers "what is this bottle called and whom is it for"; the producer answers "who owns the trademark, who organises production and who bears legal responsibility". This brand records the brand layer: name, product line, positioning and marks; see the producer entry for corporate history, ownership and subsidiary roles.

A brand is also not a single product. One brand can have several series, strengths, volumes and price bands, so a question such as "how much does Xijiu cost" or "what does Xijiu taste like" needs a specific product first.

## Owning producer

The brand's producer is [Guizhou Xijiu Co., Ltd.](ref:producer). Its predecessor can be traced to a Yin-family workshop of the Wanli era of the Ming dynasty; it became a state-run enterprise through acquisition in 1952, joined the Moutai group in 1998, and converted into a joint-stock company in 2022. The joint-stock company holds the brand and assets, and organises product specifications and sales.

The producer entry records the plant location, the entry into the Moutai group and the joint-stock conversion. This brand keeps only what relates directly to the brand, so corporate history and product history are not conflated.

## Product line

Public product material lists Junpin Xijiu, Xijiu Jiaocang 1988, Jinzuan Xijiu, Jinzhi Xijiu and Yinzhi Xijiu, alongside Zhijiao, Xijiu Gan'en 98 and the Xijiu Impression series. Junpin Xijiu targets high-end gifting and business use, Jiaocang 1988 is a widely distributed core item, and the Jinzuan, Jinzhi and Yinzhi series cover mid-market and everyday banquets.

Strength, volume, packaging and standards change by year, and some series have gift boxes, regional versions or channel-specific versions. For Jiaocang 1988, the public product page lists 53% vol in a 500 ml format, but packaging and batch notes differ by year. Buyers should check the producer, strength, net content and standard on the label rather than the series name alone.

## Positioning and price band

The brand covers a band from mid-market to high-end: the Jinzhi, Yinzhi and Jinzuan series serve everyday banquets and mass gifting, Jiaocang 1988 carries business and banquet demand, and Junpin Xijiu reaches high-end gifting. Prices change with year, channel and promotions, so this brand gives no specific retail price and does not treat one promotion as long-term positioning.

A price band is market positioning, not a quality grade. Judging the liquid means looking at raw materials, process, storage and the label standard, not price or packaging alone. Vintage wording on sauce-aroma products also needs care: the year printed on the bottle, the base-liquor vintage and the blending ratio are different concepts.

## History

Brand narrative traces its origin to the Yin-family workshop of the Wanli era of the Ming dynasty and stresses the Chishui River location. This is local distilling history and brand narrative: it shows the link between the name and the regional tradition, but it is not the continuous production age of a modern plant and cannot be converted into the age of the liquid in a bottle.

The modern company has clearer points: state-run formation in 1952, entry into the Moutai group in 1998, and conversion into a joint-stock company in 2022. During the Moutai period the company expanded its product structure and capacity; after becoming independent, it increased brand and market investment to form today's structure centred on Junpin Xijiu and Jiaocang 1988.

## Name and marks

"Xijiu" takes its name from the producing place, Xishui, and is also written as "Guizhou Xijiu". The brand name overlaps heavily with the place and the company, so discussions need to separate them: Xishui is a producing area, Guizhou Xijiu Co., Ltd. is the production entity, and Xijiu is the market mark.

Buyers should check the trademark, producer, anti-counterfeit marks and batch information. Similar names and counterfeit packaging exist, and bottle shape, colour or a single character alone are not reliable. Official channels and authorised stores reduce risk but do not replace label checks.

## Common misconceptions

First, conflating the brand with the joint-stock company. The brand is a market mark; the company is a legal entity.
Second, treating the Ming-dynasty workshop record as the modern founding year. Cultural narrative and production history differ.
Third, treating Jiaocang 1988 as the whole brand. It is one core item.
Fourth, treating the "cellar storage" wording as the age of the liquid. Use the label and company information.
Fifth, treating price as a quality grade. Price is market positioning, not a process standard.

Xijiu should be read from its producing town and its sauce-aroma process. The distillery stands in the middle reaches of the Chishui River, and the brand has passed through several ownership changes within the Moutai group; those are company-history facts, not bottle-age claims. Junpin and the Jiaocang series occupy different positions. A number in a product name is a naming device, not proof of the age of the liquid.
