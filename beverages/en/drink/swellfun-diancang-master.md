# Swellfun Collection Master Edition

"Swellfun Collection Master Edition" is a strong-aroma product in the high-end line of [Swellfun](ref:brand). Public material gives the common specifications as 52% and 38% ABV in 500 mL, made in Chengdu, Sichuan. It pairs the historical narrative of the Shuijing Street distillery site with a high-end price band, and understanding it means separating brand, producer, site and exact specification.![Swellfun Collection Master Edition category image](/media/photos/drink-style-light-aroma-baijiu-1c3af236.jpg)

## Definition and position

Collection Master Edition sits at the high end of Swellfun's range, layered above Jingcui, First Workshop, Jingtai and Zhenniang No. 8, and aimed mainly at business banquets and gifting. It is still [strong-aroma baijiu](ref:strong-aroma), not a separate aroma type, and not a statutory "vintage liquor" grade.

"Collection Master Edition" is a product name, not a quality certification. Public reporting describes it as a core product in Swellfun's push into the high end; that market language explains the price band and channel position, not an independent judgment of the body.

## Producer and brand

The product is made by [Sichuan Swellfun Co., Ltd.](ref:producer) and the brand is [Swellfun](ref:brand). The company listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in December 1996, its parent is Sichuan Chengdu Swellfun Group, and its actual controller is Diageo.

Brand and listed company are two layers: the brand answers "what is it called and who is it for", while the listed company answers "who owns the trademark and who carries legal responsibility". Swellfun is at once a company name, a brand name and a product name, so a series name is needed when discussing a specific flavour.

## Origin and site

Swellfun is based on the Chengdu Plain in the Min River catchment, with a humid climate suited to open qu making and pit fermentation. Within the map of [Sichuan](ref:sichuan)'s strong-aroma regions, Chengdu forms a different style band from Yibin, Luzhou and Mianzhu.

The brand narrative centres on the Shuijing Street distillery site. Public information shows that the site was selected among China's top ten archaeological discoveries in 1999 and is a nationally protected key cultural relic; the related brewing craft is listed as national intangible cultural heritage. These designations speak to the heritage value of the site and the craft, not to a product quality certification.

The site's continuous production history reaches back several hundred years, but the modern brand was launched in 2000. These are different time scales, and the production age of the old workshop should not be converted into the founding year of the modern company.

## Process

The basic strong-aroma path is mud-pit solid-state fermentation, continued mash, mixed steaming and cutting the distillate by stage. [Qu making](ref:qu-making) determines the microbial community and aroma precursors, [fermentation](ref:fermentation) completes saccharification and alcohol production in the pit, and [distillation](ref:distillation) cuts the run by stage, with the middle section used as the main base spirit.

New spirit is stored by grade and round, then [blended](ref:blending) to unify aroma, body and finish. Blending is a technical balance, not simple addition or dilution. Company material stresses the continuity of the site and the craft, but specific base-spirit ages, ratios and batches are production information, and buyers should rely on the label and company disclosures.

## Specification and label

Public reporting gives the common specifications as 52% and 38% ABV in 500 mL, with the aroma type marked as strong aroma. Baijiu industry terminology and aroma classification can be checked against GB/T 15109, while the specific product standard and grade are governed by the bottle label.

The producer, product name, aroma type, strength, net volume, raw materials, standard and anti-counterfeit information on the label are the basic identity checks. Buy through regular channels and verify the anti-counterfeit mark and batch rather than judging by bottle shape or main colour.

## Aroma and tasting

The nose is led by pit aroma, grain aroma and sweet aroma. The entry is mellow and sweet, the body is fairly full, the middle opens and the finish is clean. At 52% ABV the aroma releases more directly and the alcohol is more noticeable; at 38% ABV the spirit is softer and better suited to slow drinking with food. Strength changes the entry and aroma presentation, but it is not a quality ranking.

For Swellfun Collection Master Edition, taste in a small glass: smell the resting spirit first, then swirl gently and smell again. Let the liquid stay in the mouth and follow the order of sweet, sour, bitter and umami, along with body weight. Adding water lowers the alcohol and opens the aroma, but it also changes the balance, so add drop by drop and compare.

## History and market

The company listed in 1996, the archaeological findings at the site drew attention in 1999, and the Swellfun brand was launched in 2000. Collection Master Edition later became an important product in the high-end band, and public reporting shows several price adjustments, indicating that its position shifts with the market.

The product sits where a regional famous liquor is extending into the national high-end market. Prices move with vintage, channel and supply and demand, and this entry gives no figures. Treating price as proof of quality, or the age of the site as bottle age, are common misreadings.

## Buying and storage

For Swellfun Collection Master Edition, buy through regular channels and check the anti-counterfeit mark, producer and batch. Gift boxes and channel-specific editions should be checked against the label, so that a packaging difference is not mistaken for a difference in the liquid. For a full case, also inspect the seal and storage conditions.

For Swellfun Collection Master Edition, unopened baijiu should be kept upright, away from light, in a cool place without strong odours. Once opened, drink it reasonably soon; prolonged exposure to air costs aroma. A home cellar cannot reproduce distillery storage conditions, so the year of purchase should not be treated as an automatic quality gain.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating the site's production age as the founding year of the modern brand. The brand was launched in 2000.
Second, treating a cultural-relic or intangible-heritage designation as product quality certification. Those designations concern the site and the craft.
Third, treating Collection Master Edition as a separate aroma type. It is still strong-aroma baijiu.
Fourth, assuming 52% ABV must be better than 38% ABV. Strength serves product design and drinking context.
Fifth, judging authenticity by price or packaging. Anti-counterfeit marks, channels and labels are more reliable.
