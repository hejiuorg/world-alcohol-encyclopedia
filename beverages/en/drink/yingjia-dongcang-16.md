# Yingjia Dongcang 16

"Yingjia Dongcang 16" is a strong-aroma product in the cave-storage series of [Yingjia Gongjiu](ref:brand), commonly bottled at 52% or 42% ABV in 500 mL and made in Huoshan, Anhui. It combines multi-grain strong-aroma production, a long fermentation cycle and cave storage in one product story. The "16" in the name is a series number, not the age of the liquid in the bottle.

## Definition and position

The cave-storage series occupies the upper-mid and business-banquet tier in Yingjia's portfolio, above the mainstream Yingjia Gongjiu line and below or alongside the gift-oriented Master Edition. Understanding this bottle means looking at the [brand](ref:brand), the [producer](ref:producer), the exact specification and the storage method together, not only at the words "cave storage".

"Dongcang 16" is a product name, not a quality grade and not proof of age. The "ecological cave storage" language in company material describes a storage environment and a product line; buyers should still check strength, volume, raw materials and producer on the label.

## Producer and brand

The product is made by [Anhui Yingjia Gongjiu Co., Ltd.](ref:producer) and the brand is [Yingjia Gongjiu](ref:brand). The company was established on 28 November 2003, registered in Foziling town, Huoshan county, Anhui, and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange on 28 May 2015.

Group, listed company, brand and product are four layers: the brand answers "what is it called and who is it for", the listed company answers "who owns the trademark and who carries legal responsibility", and the product answers the exact strength, volume and batch. Treating "ecological brewing" as a third-party certification confuses a company positioning with an audited standard.

## Origin and raw materials

Huoshan sits on the northern slope of the Dabie Mountains. The area around the Foziling reservoir has relatively high humidity and good forest cover, and company material presents these conditions as the basis of its "ecological production area". Local spring water filtered through bamboo roots is called "scraping water" locally; in 2018 the Foziling reservoir was selected as an excellent source of "Chinese good water". This is regional background, not a substitute for testing the water actually used in production.

The public product label lists water, sorghum, rice, glutinous rice, wheat and corn as raw materials. [Sorghum](ref:sorghum) provides the frame and structure, rice and glutinous rice add sweetness, wheat brings qu aroma and fermentation power, and corn adds body. [Water](ref:water) is used in qu making, fermentation and strength reduction, and its quality affects how clean the entry feels.

A multi-grain recipe is a style route, not a league table in which more grains means better quality. Ratios change between product lines, and the ingredient order and standard on the label are more reliable than advertising language.

## Process and cave storage

The basic strong-aroma path is mud-pit solid-state fermentation, continued mash, mixed steaming and cutting the distillate by stage. [Qu making](ref:qu-making) uses a medium-temperature "bao-bao" qu as the saccharifying and fermenting agent; the microbial mix in the qu shapes pit aroma and grain aroma. [Fermentation](ref:fermentation) runs continuously in mud pits, and company material says the cycle is about 90 days, with several dozen steps from raw grain intake to finished bottling.

After the pit, [distillation](ref:distillation) separates the head, middle and tail of the run, with the middle used as the main base spirit. New spirit is stored by grade and round, then [blended](ref:blending) to unify aroma, body and finish. Blending is a technical balance, not simple addition or dilution.

Cave storage happens at the maturation stage. Company material says the Huangyan and Qingtan caves stay at about 20°C and about 85% humidity, where cave microbes interact with the base spirit and speed up mellowing. Cave storage can make the storage environment more stable and change the pace of maturation, but it does not change the aroma type, and the word "cave storage" alone cannot establish a quality grade.

## Specification and label

Public product material gives the common specifications as 52% and 42% ABV in 500 mL, with the aroma type marked as strong-aroma baijiu. The product standard for strong-aroma baijiu is commonly GB/T 10781.1, and the bottle label governs in each case.

For Yingjia Dongcang 16, the producer, product name, aroma type, strength, net volume, raw materials, standard and anti-counterfeit information on the label are the basic identity checks. Buy through regular channels and verify the anti-counterfeit mark and batch rather than judging by packaging similarity or price alone.

## Aroma and tasting

The nose is led by pit aroma, grain aroma and sweet aroma. The entry is mellow and sweet, the body is fairly full, the middle expands and the finish is clean. At 52% ABV the alcohol is more assertive and the aroma releases more directly; at 42% ABV the spirit is softer and better suited to slow drinking with food. Strength changes the entry and aroma presentation, but it is not a quality ranking.

For Yingjia Dongcang 16, taste in a small glass: smell the resting spirit first, then swirl gently and smell again. Let the liquid stay in the mouth and follow the order of sweet, sour, bitter and umami, along with body weight. Adding water lowers the alcohol and opens the aroma, but it also changes the balance, so add drop by drop and compare.

## History and market

After the company listed in 2015, the cave-storage series became an important line for Yingjia's move into the upper-mid price band. Company material presents "ecological brewing" as a long-term positioning and keeps promoting the water source, the production process and the cave base. These statements help explain the product background but are not a third-party quality rating.

The series sits where a regional brand is extending into the national market. Prices move with vintage, channel and supply and demand, and this entry gives no figures. Treating price as proof of quality, or the "16" as age, are common misreadings.

## Buying and storage

For Yingjia Dongcang 16, buy through regular channels and check the anti-counterfeit mark, producer and batch. Gift boxes and channel-specific editions should be checked against the label, so that a packaging difference is not mistaken for a difference in the liquid. For a full case, also inspect the seal and storage conditions.

Unopened baijiu should be kept upright, away from light, in a cool place without strong odours. Once opened, drink it reasonably soon; prolonged exposure to air costs aroma. A home cellar cannot reproduce cave conditions, so the year of purchase should not be treated as an automatic quality gain.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating "16" as age in the bottle. It is a series number.
Second, treating every Yingjia product as cave-stored. Cave storage is only one product line.
Third, treating "ecological brewing" as third-party certification. It is a company positioning.
Fourth, assuming 52% ABV must be better than 42% ABV. Strength serves product design and drinking context.
Fifth, judging authenticity by price or packaging. Anti-counterfeit marks, channels and labels are more reliable.
