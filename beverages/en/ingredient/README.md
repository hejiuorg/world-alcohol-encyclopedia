# Ingredients

- [Agave](agave.md) — Agave is a succulent genus whose heart, or piña, is rich in inulin-type fructans; after cooking and hydrolysis it is fermented and distilled into tequila and mezcal, with species, maturity and roasting method shaping flavour.
- [Apple](apple.md) — The apple is the fruit of Malus domestica in the rose family, and the combination of sugar, acid and tannin defines cider style; cider apples are grouped as sweet, sharp, bittersweet and bittersharp by acidity and tannin, and the fruit can also be distilled into apple brandy and Calvados.
- [Barley](barley.md) — An annual cereal of the genus Hordeum; after malting it becomes the base material for beer and whisky, and in baijiu daqu it is often paired with wheat and peas.
- [Buckwheat](buckwheat.md) — Buckwheat is a pseudocereal in the Polygonaceae family and contains no gluten; it is used in buckwheat beer, soba shochu and some regional baijiu, bringing earthy, honeyed and grainy notes, while its protein and starch require separate adjustments in saccharification and fermentation.

- [Glutinous rice](glutinous-rice.md) — A rice type whose starch is almost entirely amylopectin, becoming very sticky and soft after cooking; it is a typical raw material for yellow wine and some sweet rice drinks, and often appears in multi-grain baijiu recipes.
- [Grape](grape.md) — The fruit of Vitis vines; wine grapes are selected for sugar, acid, pigment and tannin balance, unlike table grapes bred for eating.
- [Highland Barley](highland-barley.md) — Naked barley of the Tibetan Plateau, cold- and drought-tolerant and early-ripening, the base of tsampa, barley wine and …
- [Honey](honey.md) — Honey is the sweet substance bees make from nectar, dominated by glucose and fructose; it is the sole sugar source of mead and can add aroma to beer, liqueurs and spirits, but low nitrogen, high sugar and low pH make fermentation hard to start.
- [Hops](hop.md) — Hops are the female flower cones of a perennial climbing plant in the Cannabaceae family; alpha acids isomerise during the boil to give bitterness, essential oils give aroma, and polyphenols and antibacterial compounds affect foam, clarity and shelf life.
- [Juniper](juniper.md) — Juniper berries are the female cones of Juniperus communis and rich in terpenes such as alpha-pinene and myrcene; they are the legally required core flavour of gin, and distillation method and dose decide whether juniper leads or blends with other botanicals.
- [Maize](maize.md) — An annual cereal of the genus Zea with high starch content and fat concentrated in the germ; it is a common ingredient in multi-grain baijiu and a core raw material in American bourbon and some beer adjuncts.
- [Millet](millet.md) — Millet is the hulled grain of foxtail millet (Setaria italica) and a general name for small-seeded cereals such as proso and pearl millet; in China it is used in huangjiu and millet wine, and in Africa and Asia for traditional cloudy grain beers, with starch and protein shaping saccharification and flavour.
- [Molasses](molasses.md) — Molasses is the syrup left after sugar crystallisation, containing sucrose, glucose, fructose and minerals; it is the main sugar source for rum and a common industrial yeast medium, and its grade and composition shape fermentation speed and flavour.
- [Oats](oat.md) — Oats are an annual cereal of the genus Avena with high fat and beta-glucan content; they bring silky body and haze to beer and are traditional in oatmeal stout, but fat and viscosity make saccharification, lautering and distillation harder than with barley.

- [Potato](potato.md) — Potato is a tuber of the nightshade family, rich in starch and water, and the core material for potato vodka in Poland, …
- [Rice](rice.md) — The grain left after rice is husked and milled, and an important starch source for baijiu, yellow wine and sake; japonica and indica rice differ in starch structure, stickiness and steaming behaviour, which directly affect saccharification and fermentation.
- [Rye](rye.md) — Rye is an annual cereal of the genus Secale, hardy and tolerant of poor soil; it is the required base of American rye whiskey and brings a spicy, dry grain character to beer, while beta-glucans and proteins make saccharification and lautering harder.
- [Sorghum](sorghum.md) — An annual cereal of the genus Sorghum and one of the main grain ingredients in Chinese baijiu; the balance of starch, protein, fat and tannin in the grain shapes fermentation and flavour.
- [Sugarcane](sugarcane.md) — Sugarcane is a tall grass of the genus Saccharum whose stems store sucrose; pressing the juice and fermenting it directly yields cane-juice rum and Brazilian cane spirit, while refining sugar first and fermenting the molasses gives the other major rum route.
- [Water](water.md) — The most easily overlooked yet ubiquitous ingredient in brewing; its minerals, hardness and pH affect saccharification, fermentation, microbial activity and final flavour, and have long shaped where distilleries and breweries are sited.
- [Wheat](wheat.md) — An annual cereal of the genus Triticum, used as a staple food and as a key ingredient in qu making, multi-grain baijiu, beer and whisky; the balance of protein and starch shapes both qu quality and the body of the drink.
- [Yeast](yeast.md) — Yeast is a single-celled fungus that turns sugar into ethanol and carbon dioxide and produces esters, higher alcohols and other flavour compounds; strain, pitching method and temperature steer fermentation, making yeast the most important and most overlooked variable in brewing.
