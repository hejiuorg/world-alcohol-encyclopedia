# Tools

[返回English目录](../README.md)

- [Bar spoon](bar-spoon.md) — A long-handled bar tool for stirring cocktails, guiding pours and measuring small volumes; length, bowl capacity and spiral shaft affect stirring efficiency and handling.
- [Bottle Opener](bottle-opener.md) — A simple tool for crown-cap beer bottles, common as a bar blade, wall-mounted opener or key shape. It grips the cap's lower edge and lifts steadily.
- [Channel knife](channel-knife.md) — A bar knife with a small V- or U-shaped notch that pulls long, even strips of citrus peel; notch width, sharpness and cutting depth determine aroma release and whether the garnish holds its shape.
- [Citrus press](citrus-press.md) — A manual tool that uses a lever or screw mechanism to press half a citrus fruit; cup size, stability and acid-resistant materials determine juice yield, cleaning and service life.
- [Cocktail pick](cocktail-pick.md) — A thin pin used to hold olives, cherries, citrus peel or onions; common lengths run 10–15 cm, and material, length, point treatment and corrosion resistance decide whether it holds the garnish without getting in the way.
- [Cocktail shaker](cocktail-shaker.md) — A bar tool for shaking cocktails, commonly in three-piece and two-piece forms; body, seal and straining method affect chilling, dilution and working efficiency.
- [Corkscrew](corkscrew.md) — A tool that uses a helix and a lever to pull a cork. The screw must be centred, the pull vertical and slow, and a broken cork needs care.
- [Fine strainer](fine-strainer.md) — The second strainer in double straining, used to catch fruit fibre, herb fragments and ice shards; mesh fineness, frame stability and cleaning affect clarity and speed.
- [Hydrometer](hydrometer.md) — A glass instrument that reads liquid density by buoyancy, with density, specific gravity, alcohol and sugar scales; it needs temperature correction and measures density, not alcohol directly.
- [Ice Bucket](ice-bucket.md) — A bucket-shaped vessel for chilling bottles, usually metal or glass. An ice-water mix chills faster than ice alone, the water should cover the wine and the time depends on the drink.
- [Jigger](jigger.md) — A small bar measure for fast, repeated doses of liquid; dual-ended capacity, clear markings and a controlled lip affect consistency and speed in service.
- [Mixing glass](mixing-glass.md) — A vessel used with a bar spoon for stirred cocktails; wall thickness, capacity and rim shape affect stirring efficiency, dilution and the final temperature.
- [Muddler](muddler.md) — A rod-shaped bar tool for gently pressing herbs, fruit and sugar in the glass; length, head shape and material affect force control, cleaning and glass safety.
- [Speed pourer](speed-pourer.md) — A spout fitted to a spirit bottle that restricts flow and allows one-handed, continuous pouring; it buys speed and consistency, not accuracy, which still belongs to the jigger.
- [Strainer](strainer.md) — A bar tool that keeps ice, crushed ice and solid residue out of the glass; Hawthorne and julep strainers are the common types, with a fine-mesh strainer used for a second pass.
- [Thermometer](thermometer.md) — A tool for measuring liquid, bottle or cellar temperature, common as a probe, infrared or stick-on model. Readings must settle, calibration must be checked and the probe placed where it represents the whole.
- [Y-peeler](y-peeler.md) — A two-handed peeler with a Y- or T-shaped handle that cuts wide, thin ribbons of citrus peel or vegetable skin; blade type, cutting gap and pressure decide thickness and how much pith comes along.
