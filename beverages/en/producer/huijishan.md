# Kuaijishan Shaoxing Rice Wine Co., Ltd.

Kuaijishan Shaoxing Rice Wine Co., Ltd. is a huangjiu producer in Shaoxing, Zhejiang, and its products belong to the Shaoxing type of [huangjiu](ref:yellow-wine). The company trades under the [Kuaijishan](ref:brand) brand, with a representative product in [Kuaijishan Chunzheng 5](ref:chunzheng). This producer records the producer layer: company identity, site, brand structure and craft tradition.

## Production area and site

Shaoxing lies in northern Zhejiang south of the Qiantang River, with a subtropical monsoon climate: humid, rainy and with four distinct seasons. The Jian Lake water system is an important water source for Shaoxing wine. Traditional practice values winter brewing, using low winter temperatures to slow fermentation and limit unwanted microbes, and the habit continues today.

## Brewing tradition

The typical Shaoxing route is: steep rice, steam, cool, mix with qu, ferment in vats, stir (kaipa), press, pasteurise, fill jars and age. Glutinous rice supplies starch, [wheat qu](ref:qu-making) handles saccharification and part of the aroma formation, and wine starter is used to start fermentation. Stirring regulates temperature and oxygen and is the key step in steering fermentation.

After [fermentation](ref:fermentation) the liquid is pressed off, pasteurised and stored in jars. Ceramic jars are slightly permeable, and the wine slowly oxidises and esterifies during storage to form aged aroma. Company material emphasises hand-made winter brewing and long storage in jars; these statements are useful clues to the craft, but exact parameters vary by product line.

## Company history

Public material traces Kuaijishan to the Yunji winery of 1743. Public material states that in 1915 Shaoxing wine won an award at the Panama-Pacific International Exposition, an experience the company repeatedly cites as part of its brand history. The company then continued production in Shaoxing and gradually built a product structure centred on aged wine.

In 2014 the company listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange and entered the capital markets. The listing brought capacity expansion and brand investment, but the main business remained Shaoxing huangjiu. Overlaying the three identities of long-established brand, geographical-indication product and listed company makes the company's position easier to understand.

## Industry and market

The Shaoxing huangjiu industry is characterised by many small and medium producers and strong seasonality, and the pace of production during winter brewing differs from the rest of the year. Kuaijishan sits within this cluster and must maintain traditional craft while facing structural issues such as regionally concentrated consumption and limited cultivation of younger drinkers.

Huangjiu spans a wide price range, from everyday cooking and table wine to gift and aged products. ## Common misconceptions

First, treating 1743 as the founding year of the modern company. It is the workshop origin in the company narrative.
Second, treating "Kuaijishan" as only one bottle. It is at once a company name, a brand name and a product-series name.
Third, treating an age number as the legal age of the liquid. Age statements should be read with the company's product explanation.
Fourth, treating the geographical indication as a quality grade. It fixes origin and process requirements.
Fifth, judging authenticity by price or packaging. Channels, anti-counterfeit marks and labels are more reliable.
