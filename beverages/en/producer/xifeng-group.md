# Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Group Co., Ltd.

Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Group Co., Ltd. is based in Baoji, Shaanxi. Its predecessor was the state-run Shaanxi Xifeng Distillery, founded in 1956. The company became a joint-stock company in 1999 and was renamed a group company in 2008. Its core product Xifengjiu is a feng-aroma [baijiu](ref:baijiu), covered in the [Xifengjiu](ref:xifeng) and [Hong Xifeng](ref:hongxifeng) entries.![Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Group Co., Ltd. category image](/media/photos/drink-style-light-aroma-baijiu-1c3af236.jpg)

## Site and region

Xifengjiu comes from the Fengxiang area of western Guanzhong, Shaanxi, now part of Fengxiang District in Baoji. The climate is a warm-temperate continental monsoon type, with distinct seasons, concentrated rainfall and relatively dry air, which suits qu making and storage. Local sorghum, water and pit conditions together form the base of the feng-aroma style. Within the map of [China](ref:china)'s baijiu regions, Fengxiang is one of the most representative famous-liquor origins in the north-west.

The registered address, production site and filling location are not necessarily the same. ## Brands and products

Xifengjiu is the core brand and belongs to the feng-aroma type. Public product material lists series such as Hong Xifeng and Xifeng 1964, with different positions and body designs; the usual strength is 55% vol. Product lines, volumes, strengths, standards and prices change by year, so buyers should rely on the current label and official channels.

Statements such as "vintage" and "wine-sea storage" should be read with the label explanation. A wine sea is a storage vessel, not the exact age of the liquid in the bottle; the age, base-liquor composition and blending ratio should be checked against company information. Similar names and counterfeit products exist in the market, so checking the producer, aroma type, strength and anti-counterfeit information matters more than packaging.

## Craft and style

Feng-aroma uses sorghum as the raw material, with barley, wheat and peas for qu making. The mash undergoes solid-state fermentation in earthen cellars, is distilled in fractions through [distillation](ref:distillation), then stored in wine seas and blended into the finished product. The temperature and ratio in [qu making](ref:qu-making) affect saccharification and aroma precursors, while the earthen cellar environment creates a microbial structure different from mud pits.

A wine sea is a barrel-shaped container woven from willow-like strips and lined with traditional materials, in which the liquor changes slowly. Its breathability and lining take part in oxidation and aroma integration and form part of the feng-aroma style. Feng-aroma is often described as lying between light-aroma and strong-aroma, but it is a type formed by its own process, not a simple blend of the other two.

## History

In 1956 the state-run Shaanxi Xifeng Distillery was founded, moving Xifengjiu from workshop production into factory production. In July 1996 the Shaanxi provincial government listed the distillery in the second batch of pilot enterprises for a modern enterprise system. In October 1999 the Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Co., Ltd. was restructured on the basis of the distillery. In October 2008 the company was renamed Shaanxi Xifeng Liquor Group Co., Ltd.; it increased capital twice in 2009 and 2010. In 2010 the Xifeng Liquor Distillery Group Co., Ltd. was converted into an investment-holding group.

"Founding", "restructuring" and "renaming" are three different points in the company's history and cannot be merged into one founding year. The brand history can be traced further back to local distilling traditions, but that is cultural narrative, not the continuous production history of the modern company.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating 1999 as the start of all Xifeng history. The joint-stock restructuring and the state-run distillery's founding are different points.
Second, treating feng-aroma as a simple compromise between light and strong aroma. It is a type formed by its own process.
Third, treating the wine sea as ordinary packaging. It takes part in storage and flavour formation.
Fourth, treating the registered place as the origin of every product. Use the production address on the label.
Fifth, using "vintage" or "wine-sea" wording in place of a specific age. Vessel and age are different things.
