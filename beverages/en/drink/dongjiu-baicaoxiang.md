# Dongjiu Baicaoxiang

Dongjiu Baicaoxiang is a high-end product from Guizhou Dongjiu in the Dong aroma type, commonly 54% ABV in 500 mL and made in Donggongsi town, Huichuan district, Zunyi, Guizhou. Its main process features are herbs in qu, the combined use of small and large qu, and double-mash steam distillation. The Dong aroma type is neither sauce, strong nor light aroma, and it needs its own flavour framework.

## Definition and position

"Baicaoxiang" is a product name, not an aroma-type name. It is produced by [Guizhou Dongjiu](ref:producer) and carried by the [Dongjiu brand](ref:brand). The "hundred herbs" in the name corresponds to the herbs-in-qu process narrative; it does not mean visible herbs in the bottle and does not imply health or medicinal effects. It is a Dong-aroma product within [baijiu](ref:baijiu).

The product sits in the high-end price band and carries brand image and gifting functions. The brand also has Guomi, Zhencang and Micang series with different strengths, volumes and positions. When discussing "the Dongjiu taste", separate the product, strength and herbal qu differences; the performance of one product should not stand for the whole brand.

## Producer and brand

The producer is Guizhou Dongjiu Co., Ltd., based in Donggongsi town, Huichuan district, Zunyi. Public company history records 1957 as the resumption of production, while business registration shows the joint-stock company established in October 1994. Resumption, award at a national evaluation and restructuring are events of different kinds and should not be merged into one founding year.

Dongjiu's process and formula have been listed as state secret technology since 1983, with the scope and term set by the competent authorities. Public material can describe the process framework but not the full formula. Writing and communication should avoid turning "state secret" into a marketing badge and should not invent formula details.

## Origin and raw materials

Donggongsi town lies in northern [Guizhou](ref:guizhou) near the urban area of Zunyi, in a humid subtropical climate with distinct seasons. Local water and air conditions favour open qu making and long fermentation. Origin conditions are external factors and do not replace checking the label of a specific product.

Raw materials include sorghum, rice and wheat. [Sorghum](ref:sorghum) is the main fermenting grain, while [rice](ref:rice) and wheat are used to make small or large qu and serve as carriers for the herbs. Herbs participate in forming the microbial community during qu making and affect qu aroma and later fermentation. The herb varieties, ratios and handling are company secrets, and this entry does not speculate.

## Brewing process

Dongjiu's public process features are the combined use of small and large qu and double-mash steam distillation. Large qu and small qu are cultured separately, with different microbial composition and enzyme activity; during distillation the fermented mashes are steamed together so aroma compounds transfer and layer. This route differs from single-large-qu sauce aroma and from mud-pit continued-mash strong aroma.

[Qu making](ref:qu-making) is the base of Dong aroma, with herbs participating in qu culture to form a distinctive qu aroma; during [fermentation](ref:fermentation) the mashes for small and large qu are handled separately; during [distillation](ref:distillation) steam distillation carries aroma into the spirit. New spirit is stored and blended to unify style. Specific rounds, temperatures and ratios are company secrets, and this entry describes only the public framework.

## Specification and label

The common specification is 54% ABV in 500 mL. Strength is shown as [alcohol by volume](ref:abv), where 54% ABV means 54 mL of ethanol in 100 mL of liquid at 20°C. The Dong aroma type was formally established by a Guizhou local standard in August 2008, and Dongjiu is its representative; the aroma type, standard and producer on the label are the key to identity.

The product name, aroma type, strength, volume, producer, standard and batch on the label are the basics for identity. Gift boxes, commemorative and channel editions should be distinguished by label. Terms such as "hundred herbs" and "state secret" are product naming and process narrative, not efficacy claims; cultural wording on packaging should not be read as proof of quality.

## Flavour and tasting

Dong aroma typically shows ester, herbal and pit notes interwoven, a soft entry, a round body, sweetness and a relatively long finish. It differs from the roasted note of sauce aroma, the pit note of strong aroma and the clean crispness of light aroma, so other aroma-type standards should not be applied. The 54% ABV strength is noticeable, but whether the body carries the alcohol deserves more attention.

Taste from a small cup: smell at rest, then gently swirl and smell again. Let the liquid stay in the mouth and note the order of herbal, ester and sweet notes and whether the finish is clean. Comparison with other Dongjiu series is useful, but keep the same temperature and cup. Ice or heavy dilution is not the traditional way to drink it and will flatten aroma and change structure.

## History and market

Public company milestones include the 1957 resumption of production, selection among China's old eight famous spirits in 1963, state secret technology status from 1983, establishment of the joint-stock company in 1994 and the August 2008 Guizhou local standard for Dong aroma. The company has since received titles such as China Time-honoured Brand and intangible cultural heritage; the exact lists and batches follow official announcements.

Baicaoxiang sits in the high-end price band and is a common choice for business banquets and gifting. Prices change with year, channel and supply and demand, and this entry gives no figures. Treating "state secret" as a quality guarantee, or "hundred herbs" as a health effect, are common misreadings; the first is a secrecy system and the second does not fit food-labelling rules.

## Buying and storage

Prefer official channels and check anti-counterfeit marks, producer, batch and specification. Different series and packaging editions should be checked against the label, and gift-box differences should not be mistaken for body differences. For full cases, check seals, production dates and storage conditions; a price far below the usual range should first raise questions about source and authenticity.

For Dongjiu Baicaoxiang, unopened baijiu should be stored upright, away from light, in a cool place without strong odours. Once opened, drink it soon; prolonged exposure loses aroma. Home storage cannot reproduce distillery conditions, so purchase year should not be equated with quality gain.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating "hundred herbs" as a health effect. It describes qu making.
Second, grouping Dong aroma under sauce or strong aroma. Dong is a separate type.
Third, treating "state secret" as a quality grade. It describes a secrecy system.
Fourth, treating the product name as an aroma type. Baicaoxiang is one product.
Fifth, judging authenticity from price or packaging. Anti-counterfeit checks, channels and labels are more reliable.
