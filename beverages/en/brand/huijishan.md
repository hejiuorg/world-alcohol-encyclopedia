# Kuaijishan

Kuaijishan is one of the Shaoxing huangjiu brands most easily summed up by the word "year", and also one of the easiest to misread through it. The brand belongs to Kuaijishan Shaoxing Rice Wine Co., Ltd., is based around Dongpu in Shaoxing, uses Jian Lake water, and brews from glutinous rice and wheat qu. Its narrative begins with the Yunji winery of 1743, but what actually defines a bottle is the product series, sugar type, age claim and label information, not the single symbol "Shaoxing" or "five years".

## Dongpu and Jian Lake: a concrete origin

Dongpu is a water-town west of Shaoxing city, criss-crossed by rivers and lakes and historically dense with wineries. Zhou Jiamu founded the Yunji winery there in 1743; public material says the name means "master brewers gathered like clouds". Jian Lake is the core water source for Shaoxing huangjiu, and its mineral and microbial conditions are treated locally as a reason for the wine's softness and freshness. Locating the origin in Dongpu and Jian Lake is more informative than saying only "made in Shaoxing".

## 1743: the workshop origin

1743 is the year most often quoted for the brand, and it refers to the founding of the Yunji winery, not to the modern joint-stock company. The winery changed hands and names several times, but the production site and craft tradition continued. When discussing brand age, keep the workshop origin and the legal entity apart: one is regional narrative, the other a legal person.

## 1915: the Panama-Pacific gold award

Public material states that in 1915 Shaoxing wine won a gold award at the Panama-Pacific International Exposition. It was an early international appearance for Shaoxing huangjiu and became an important point in Kuaijishan's brand history. The award is a historical event, not a modern quality certification; it records the judgement of that exposition, not the quality of every bottle sold today.

## From state winery to listed company

In 1956 the state allocated funds to build a central ageing warehouse for Shaoxing wine at the Yunji winery. In 1998 Kuaijishan and Lanting brand Shaoxing wine became the designated banquet huangjiu for the Great Hall of the People in Beijing. In 2000 the company was among the first to obtain National Geographical Indication Protected Product certification. It was renamed Kuaijishan Shaoxing Wine Co., Ltd. in 2005, became a joint-stock company on 2 July 2007, and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange on 25 August 2014 under code 601579. In 2016 Kuaijishan became a designated huangjiu for the G20 Hangzhou summit. These points belong to capacity, honours, ownership and capital markets respectively and should not be flattened into one "time-honoured brand" story.

## Product lines: age, Lanting and restaurant

The first line is aged wine, graded by ageing years; five-year aged wine is one of the more widely distributed tiers, represented by [Kuaijishan Chunzheng 5](ref:chunzheng). An age statement corresponds to product design, not legal proof of the age of the liquid. The second line is premium and gift products, with Lanting one of the better-known examples, positioned above everyday products in packaging and price. The third line covers restaurant and custom-channel products, common at banquets and in regional markets. Strength, sugar type, volume and standard can differ between series, and one product's parameters cannot describe the whole brand.

## Price bands and occasions

Five-year aged wine commonly sits at 20-40 yuan and suits everyday meals and cooking; Lanting is roughly 100-300 yuan and serves gifting and business; higher-aged and limited products can exceed 500 yuan. Huangjiu pricing is driven not only by age but by evaporation losses in storage, packaging, channel and brand premium. A price band is positioning, not a quality ladder.

## Packaging and recognition

Everyday Kuaijishan products mostly use dark glass or ceramic jars, with labels stressing "Shaoxing wine", "five years" and Jian Lake motifs; Lanting uses cleaner bottle shapes and calligraphic type to signal culture. Buyers should check the full producer name, address, strength, net volume, sugar type (dry, semi-dry, semi-sweet or sweet) and execution standard, not just the three characters "Kuaijishan".

## Channels and market

Kuaijishan is strongest in Jiangsu, Zhejiang and Shanghai, with restaurant, supermarket, e-commerce and gift channels running in parallel. Shaoxing huangjiu has strong regional drinking habits: local consumers know the brands and age language, while markets outside the region depend more on restaurant promotion and gifting. The company also sells custom wine and base wine, which may never appear on a normal shelf and therefore deserve closer label checks.

## Honours and controversy

The company's public identity includes China Time-honoured Brand, China Well-known Trademark and National Geographical Indication Protected Product, along with the 1915 exposition gold award and the 2016 G20 designation. Controversy centres on age claims: huangjiu lacks a unified test comparable to those used for distilled spirits, so an age statement is usually a product-design value, and consumers may read it as the exact age of every drop. A second pressure is the broader modernisation of Shaoxing huangjiu, whose sweetness, strength and drinking occasions remain traditional and whose appeal to younger drinkers is still an industry question.

## Reading a bottle

First identify the series: Chunzheng 5, Lanting, Shuixiang Guose or a restaurant custom product. Then check sugar type and strength, since semi-dry and semi-sweet styles differ widely. Next read ingredients (glutinous rice, wheat, water) and the execution standard. Finally check producer and origin. For a gift box, confirm whether it carries geographical-indication status.

## Common misconceptions

First, 1743 is not the founding year of the joint-stock company. Second, "five years" is not the precise age of all liquid in the bottle. Third, "Shaoxing wine" is not a generic term open to any huangjiu; it is protected as a geographical indication. Fourth, an exposition gold award is not a modern quality certification. Fifth, price is not a quality grade.
