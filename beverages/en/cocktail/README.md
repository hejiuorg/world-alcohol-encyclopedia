# Cocktails

[返回English目录](../README.md)

- [Americano](americano.md) — A low-alcohol aperitif of Campari, sweet vermouth and soda water, dating to 1860s Milan. The balance of bitter, sweet and bubbles makes it the direct predecessor of the negroni.
- [Aperol Spritz](aperol-spritz.md) — A low-alcohol aperitif of Aperol, Prosecco and soda water in the classic three-two-one ratio, defined by orange peel, gentle bitterness, sweetness and persistent bubbles.
- [Aviation](aviation.md) — Recorded in 1916 by Hugo Ensslin of New York's Hotel Wallick in Recipes for Mixed Drinks, the Aviation combines gin, maraschino, lemon juice and crème de violette; it is pale violet to grey-blue and usually garnished with a cherry.
- [Bee's Knees](bees-knees.md) — A Prohibition-era gin sour in which honey syrup replaces sugar, with sweetness and texture set by the honey-to-water ratio.
- [Bellini](bellini.md) — A Venetian classic of Prosecco and white peach purée, usually dated to Harry's Bar in 1948 and credited to Giuseppe Cipriani; the ratio is commonly two parts Prosecco to one part purée, with peach season and temperature deciding the flavour.
- [Between the Sheets](between-the-sheets.md) — A classic of white rum, cognac, orange liqueur and lemon juice, sitting between the Sidecar and the margarita. The name belongs to Paris bar culture and carries a deliberate ambiguity.
- [Blood and Sand](blood-and-sand.md) — A classic of equal parts Scotch, cherry liqueur, sweet vermouth and orange juice, shaken and deep red. Named after the 1922 bullfighting film, it is rich and balanced.
- [Bloody Mary](bloody-mary.md) — A long drink of vodka, tomato juice, lemon, Worcestershire sauce, hot sauce, salt and pepper, garnished with celery; its origin is often traced to Harry's New York Bar in Paris, though the people and dates are disputed.
- [Boulevardier](boulevardier.md) — A Paris classic of whiskey, Campari and sweet vermouth, first printed in 1927, with equal-parts and 1.5:1:1 versions both in use.
- [Brandy Alexander](brandy-alexander.md) — A dessert cocktail of equal parts brandy, white crème de cacao and cream, shaken and topped with grated nutmeg. Its name and origin are disputed, but it is a fixture of early-twentieth-century American bar culture.
- [Caipirinha](caipirinha.md) — Brazil's national cocktail, made by muddling cachaça, lime and sugar over ice; the IBA recipe uses 60 ml cachaça, one lime and two teaspoons of sugar, and muddling and ice decide the result.
- [Champagne Cocktail](champagne-cocktail.md) — A classical cocktail of a sugar cube, Angostura bitters and Champagne, with optional cognac. Bubbles rise from the cube, and the bitters' spice meets the wine's acidity.
- [Clover Club](clover-club.md) — A classic cocktail of gin, lemon juice, raspberry syrup and egg white, shaken to a fine foam; balanced sour-sweet and usually garnished with a raspberry; the name comes from an American club of the same name in the early 20th century.
- [Corpse Reviver #2](corpse-reviver-2.md) — A classic sour of equal parts gin, orange liqueur, Lillet Blanc and lemon juice, with a rinse of absinthe and an anise-citrus character.
- [Cosmopolitan](cosmopolitan.md) — A classic cocktail of citron vodka, Cointreau, lime juice and cranberry juice, shaken and strained into a glass; tart and refreshing, pink in colour, commonly garnished with an orange twist or lime slice.
- [Daiquiri](daiquiri.md) — A classic sour of white rum, lime juice and fine sugar, shaken and strained. Its structure is so simple that any imbalance in acidity, dilution or temperature shows at once.
- [Dark 'n' Stormy](dark-n-stormy.md) — A Bermuda classic of dark rum, ginger beer and lime, with a trademark held by Gosling's. Ginger's heat against the rum's caramel makes a strong contrast, and the simple build is very sensitive to the quality of the ginger beer.
- [Dry Martini](dry-martini.md) — A classic cocktail of gin and dry vermouth, stirred and strained into a chilled glass, garnished with lemon peel or olives. Dryness is a matter of vermouth ratio, not the name.
- [Espresso Martini](espresso-martini.md) — A modern classic of vodka, coffee liqueur, espresso and syrup, hard-shaken and served with a dense coffee foam and three coffee beans.
- [French 75](french-75.md) — A sparkling short drink of gin, lemon, sugar and Champagne, commonly traced to Harry's New York Bar in Paris during the First World War and named after a French 75 mm field gun.
- [Gimlet](gimlet.md) — A short drink of gin and lime cordial, or gin with fresh lime and syrup, whose acidity, sweetness and texture depend on which lime you use.
- [Gin Fizz](gin-fizz.md) — A short drink of gin, lemon juice, syrup and soda water; the IBA version uses 45 ml gin, 30 ml lemon juice and 10 ml syrup, shaken and strained into a slim glass, topped with a splash of soda and served without ice; egg white makes a Silver Fizz, cream and orange flower water a Ramos Gin Fizz.
- [Grasshopper](grasshopper.md) — A dessert cocktail of green crème de menthe, white crème de cacao and cream, shaken into a pale green drink that tastes of mint chocolate. The New Orleans origin is the best-known account.
- [Hemingway Daiquiri](hemingway-daiquiri.md) — White rum, grapefruit, lime and maraschino with no sugar, dry and strong, tied to Havana's El Floridita and the "Papa Doble".
- [Irish Coffee](irish-coffee.md) — A hot drink of coffee, Irish whiskey, brown sugar and lightly whipped cream, layered so the cream floats. It began at Foynes airport in Ireland in the 1940s; temperature, sugar and cream texture decide the result.
- [Kir Royale](kir-royale.md) — A French classic of crème de cassis and Champagne, descended from the Kir of Dijon. Deep red, finely bubbled and sharply balanced between sweet and sour, it is one of the simplest Champagne cocktails.
- [The Last Word](last-word.md) — An equal-parts cocktail of gin, green Chartreuse, maraschino liqueur and lime juice, defined by herbal intensity, cherry-pit aroma and acidity, and a symbol of the 2000s modern-classic revival.
- [Long Island Iced Tea](long-island-iced-tea.md) — A highball of five spirits, lemon juice, syrup and cola that looks like iced tea and is far stronger than it appears. The origin story in 1970s Long Island is disputed.
- [Mai Tai](mai-tai.md) — The tiki classic created by Trader Vic in Oakland in 1944; the IBA version pairs 30 ml Jamaican rum and 30 ml Martinique molasses rum with orange liqueur, orgeat and lime, shaken and poured unstrained into a double rocks glass with pineapple, mint and lime peel.
- [Manhattan](manhattan.md) — A classic cocktail of rye whiskey, sweet vermouth and bitters, stirred and strained into a glass, garnished with a cherry. The structure is simple, with sweetness and spice unfolding in layers.
- [Margarita](margarita.md) — A classic cocktail of tequila, orange liqueur and fresh lime juice, shaken and served with an optional salt rim. Sweetness, sourness and salt are part of the structure.
- [Martinez](martinez.md) — A classic of gin, sweet vermouth, maraschino and orange bitters, dating to the 1880s in the United States. It bridges the Manhattan and the dry martini, with a delicate bitter-sweet balance.
- [Mimosa](mimosa.md) — A brunch cocktail of equal parts sparkling wine and orange juice, low in alcohol and light on bubbles; its name comes from a yellow flower, and its simple formula is sensitive to temperature and ratio.
- [Mint Julep](mint-julep.md) — A classic cocktail of bourbon, mint and sugar served over crushed ice in a filled glass, usually garnished with a mint sprig; cool and sweet, and a signature drink of American Southern horse-racing culture.
- [Mojito](mojito.md) — A long drink of white rum, lime, mint, cane sugar and soda. Press the mint, use plenty of ice, and let acidity, sweetness and bubbles decide whether it refreshes.
- [Moscow Mule](moscow-mule.md) — A long drink of vodka, ginger beer and lime, balanced by ginger heat, carbonation and fresh lime acidity, usually served over ice in a copper mug or highball glass.
- [Negroni](negroni.md) — A classic cocktail of equal parts gin, bitter aperitif and sweet vermouth. Cocktail identity and recipe versions are recorded separately.
- [Old Fashioned](old-fashioned.md) — A classic cocktail of whiskey, sugar, bitters and water. A little sugar and water open up the spirit, and the simple structure lets the whiskey speak for itself.
- [Old Pal](old-pal.md) — A classic cocktail of equal parts rye whiskey, Campari and dry vermouth, stirred and strained into a chilled glass, usually garnished with lemon peel; bitter and dry in style.
- [Paloma](paloma.md) — A refreshing long drink of tequila, grapefruit and lime, defined by grapefruit bittersweetness, an optional salt rim and lively carbonation.
- [Paper Plane](paper-plane.md) — A modern classic of equal parts bourbon, Aperol, amaro and lemon juice, balancing bittersweet herbs with bright citrus.
- [Penicillin](penicillin.md) — A modern classic of blended Scotch, lemon, honey-ginger syrup and a float of Islay whisky, recognised by its smoke and ginger heat.
- [Piña Colada](pina-colada.md) — A tropical cocktail of white rum, coconut cream and pineapple juice. A version existed in the 1930s, the modern drink was fixed at the Caribe Hilton in Puerto Rico in 1954, and sweetness and ice decide whether it works.
- [Pink Lady](pink-lady.md) — A classic sour of gin, applejack, lemon juice, grenadine and egg white; the pink comes from grenadine and the egg white gives a fine foam. The name is usually linked to the 1911 Broadway musical The Pink Lady.
- [Pisco Sour](pisco-sour.md) — A sour of pisco, lime juice, syrup and egg white, shaken into a silky foam and finished with a few dashes of Angostura bitters.
- [Ramos Gin Fizz](ramos-gin-fizz.md) — A New Orleans classic of gin, citrus, cream, egg white and orange flower water, shaken long and topped with soda, with a cloud of foam. Henry C. Ramos fixed it at Meyer's Restaurant in 1888.
- [Sazerac](sazerac.md) — A classic cocktail originating in New Orleans, made with cognac, a sugar cube and Peychaud's bitters in the IBA version, with an absinthe rinse; the rye whiskey version is the post-Civil War substitution tradition.
- [Sidecar](sidecar.md) — A classic sour of cognac, orange liqueur and fresh lemon juice, usually built at 50:20:20, with an optional sugar rim.
- [Singapore Sling](singapore-sling.md) — A classic cocktail of gin, cherry liqueur, citrus juice, pineapple juice and a little bitters, shaken and strained into a hurricane glass; the IBA version contains no soda and is garnished with pineapple and a maraschino cherry.
- [Tequila Sunrise](tequila-sunrise.md) — A layered highball of tequila, orange juice and grenadine, shading from orange to red. It became popular in 1970s California and reached a wide audience through a rock band's tour.
- [Tom Collins](tom-collins.md) — A long drink of Old Tom gin, lemon juice, syrup and soda water; the IBA lists it under John Collins (45 ml gin, 30 ml lemon juice, 15 ml syrup, 60 ml soda), notes Old Tom for a true Tom Collins, and serves it over ice in a Collins glass with lemon and cherry.
- [Vieux Carré](vieux-carre.md) — A New Orleans classic of rye whiskey, Cognac, sweet vermouth, Bénédictine and two bitters, defined by its double base and herbal sweetness.
- [Whiskey Sour](whiskey-sour.md) — A classic cocktail of whiskey, lemon juice and sugar syrup, shaken and served, optionally with an egg-white foam; commonly garnished with a lemon slice or cherry.
- [Zombie](zombie.md) — A tiki classic created by Donn Beach in Hollywood in 1934, built on Jamaican, Puerto Rican and Demerara rums with falernum, Donn's Mix and grenadine; strong and usually garnished with a mint sprig.
