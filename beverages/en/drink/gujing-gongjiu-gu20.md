# Gujing Gongjiu Gu20

Gujing Gongjiu Gu20 is the common name for a high-end product in [Gujing Gongjiu](ref:brand)'s Nianfen Yuanjiang series, often written officially as "Nianfen Yuanjiang Gu20". It belongs to [strong-aroma baijiu](ref:strong-aroma), commonly 52% ABV in 500 mL, from [Bozhou, Anhui](ref:bozhou). "Gu20" is a product name, not a uniform promise about the age of the liquid.![Gujing Gongjiu Gu20 category image](/media/photos/drink-style-light-aroma-baijiu-1c3af236.jpg)

## Definition and position

"Nianfen Yuanjiang" is the series name, and "Gu20" is a product marker within it. The series also includes products such as Gu16 and Gu8 at different positions. Treating "20" as the actual age of every bottle mistakes a product name for a vintage statement.

The product belongs to the strong-aroma route of [baijiu](ref:baijiu) and is positioned in the brand's high-end band. Brand, series and product are three layers: the brand answers what it is called, the series answers which line it belongs to, and the product answers what is in the bottle.

## Producer and brand

The product is made by [Anhui Gujing Distillery Co., Ltd.](ref:producer), and the brand is carried by [Gujing Gongjiu](ref:brand). Company identity, ownership and listing information sit at a different level from brand narrative and product flavour, and should be checked separately.

When you see "Gu20 revenue", establish whether it is the listed company's product scope or a channel estimate; when you see a "new edition", establish whether it is a packaging upgrade, a body adjustment or a channel exclusive. Data and narrative must not be mixed.

## Origin and raw materials

Bozhou lies in northwestern Anhui on the Huaibei plain, with the Guo River running through it and a warm-temperate semi-humid monsoon climate: cold dry winters and hot rainy summers. Gujing town is the core production area, and local water and pit conditions form the base for brewing. Within the map of [China](ref:china)'s baijiu regions, Bozhou is a strong-aroma node.

The main raw material is [sorghum](ref:sorghum) with wheat qu, and some products add rice, glutinous rice and corn. Sorghum starch and grain integrity affect fermentation start-up, and the grain ratio affects aroma structure. The ingredient list and standard are directly checkable.

## Brewing process

Strong aroma uses solid-state mud-pit fermentation, continued mash recycling and mixed steaming and boiling. [Qu making](ref:qu-making) uses wheat as the main material, and the microbial composition of qu affects pit and grain aroma; the longer a pit is used continuously, the more stable the pit-mud ecology.

After removal, the mash is cut into fractions, new spirit is stored by grade, and blending unifies the style. Blending balances aroma, body and finish rather than simply adding water to lower strength. Water used for reduction needs treatment to avoid off-flavours; exact parameters are company information with limited public detail.

## Specification and label

The official product page lists 52% ABV in 500 mL. The product standard for strong-aroma baijiu is commonly GB/T 10781.1, and the bottle label governs.

The packaging uses crackle celadon and a specific vessel shape, which is packaging language and not proof of body grade. The producer, product name, aroma type, strength, volume, standard and anti-counterfeit information on the label are the basics for identity. Buy through official channels.

## Flavour and tasting

The aroma is led by pit, grain and sweet notes, with a mellow sweet entry, a relatively soft body and a clean finish. Different series and strengths differ clearly, and one description cannot cover all products. At 52% ABV the heat is stronger, so sip slowly in small amounts.

Taste from a small cup: first check whether the pit aroma is clean and free of obvious off-notes, then take a small sip to feel the sweetness and body thickness. Water lowers heat and opens aroma but changes structure; ice is not traditional.

## Buying and storage

Prefer official channels and check anti-counterfeit marks, producer and batch. Gift boxes and zodiac commemorative editions should be checked against the label, and packaging differences should not be mistaken for body differences. For full cases, check seals and storage conditions.

For Gujing Gongjiu Gu20, unopened baijiu should be stored upright, away from light, in a cool place without strong odours. Once opened, drink it soon; prolonged exposure loses aroma. Home storage cannot reproduce distillery conditions, so purchase year should not be equated with quality gain.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating "Gu20" as the age of the liquid. It is a product name.
Second, treating "Nianfen Yuanjiang" as the actual year of every drop. It is a series name.
Third, treating the vessel shape as a body grade. Packaging and body are separate.
Fourth, assuming higher strength is more authentic. Strength serves product design.
Fifth, judging authenticity from price or packaging. Anti-counterfeit checks, channels and labels are more reliable.
