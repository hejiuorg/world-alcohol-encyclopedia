# Niulanshan Chenniang

"Niulanshan Chenniang" is a strong-aroma baijiu under the [Niulanshan](ref:brand) brand, nicknamed "Bai Niuer" on the market and commonly 42% ABV in 500 mL. It is made to the solid-liquid baijiu standard and positioned as an everyday spirit and mass-catering product. "Chenniang" is a product name, not a promise about the age of the liquid.![Niulanshan Chenniang category image](/media/photos/drink-style-light-aroma-baijiu-1c3af236.jpg)

## Definition and position

Niulanshan Chenniang belongs to the strong-aroma route of [baijiu](ref:baijiu) and runs in parallel with the brand's light-aroma er-guotou products. It does not aim at high-end gifting; it covers everyday drinking and small-restaurant settings through stable supply, familiar flavour and an acceptable price.

The word "chenniang" is a naming choice, not a vintage grade; the standard, ingredient list and producer information on the label are the basis for judgment. Treating it as "aged baijiu", or treating "Bai Niuer" as an official product name, are common misreadings.

## Producer and brand

The product is made by [Beijing Shunxin Agriculture Niulanshan Distillery](ref:producer) and the brand is [Niulanshan](ref:brand). The distillery's predecessor was the state-run Niulanshan distillery founded in October 1952, and it is now a branch of Shunxin Agriculture.

Brand and producer are two layers: the brand answers "what is it called and who is it for", while the distillery answers "who organises production and who carries legal responsibility". The same brand also has er-guotou and Bainian Niulanshan lines, so Chenniang's style cannot describe every product.

## Origin and raw materials

The product comes from Niulanshan town in Shunyi district, Beijing, on the Chaobai River alluvial plain. Local brewing tradition is known for er-guotou craft, but Chenniang follows the strong-aroma route; within one production area the two form different product structures.

The public label lists water, sorghum and wheat as the main raw materials. [Sorghum](ref:sorghum) provides the frame and structure, wheat participates in qu making and brings qu aroma and fermentation power, and [water](ref:water) affects the cleanliness of the entry and the harmony of the body. Solid-liquid products may also use liquid-method baijiu or edible alcohol in blending; the label and standard govern.

The ingredient list and standard are the key to the product type. Treating "pure grain" as the only standard ignores the regulatory status and labelling duties of solid-liquid baijiu.

## Process

The solid-state strong-aroma portion uses mud-pit fermentation, continued mash and mixed steaming. [Qu making](ref:qu-making) determines the microbial community and aroma precursors, [fermentation](ref:fermentation) completes saccharification and alcohol production in the pit, and [distillation](ref:distillation) cuts the run by stage.

Solid-liquid baijiu is [blended](ref:blending) from solid-state baijiu and liquid-method baijiu in proportion. It is a permitted process category under the standard but must be marked with its standard and ingredients. It is not the same category as "solid-state baijiu", so the process type should be stated first when discussing quality.

Blending balances aroma, body and cost; it is not simple addition. Ratios and base-spirit sources differ between batches and affect the sweetness and bite on entry, and buyers should rely on the label and company disclosures.

## Specification and label

The public retail page gives the common specification as 42% ABV in 500 mL, with the aroma type marked as strong aroma. The product uses the solid-liquid baijiu standard GB/T 20822, and a specific label may also show the ingredient list and quality grade.

The producer, product name, aroma type, strength, net volume, raw materials, standard and anti-counterfeit information on the label are the basic identity checks. Buy through regular channels and verify the anti-counterfeit mark and batch rather than judging by bottle shape or price.

## Aroma and tasting

The nose is led by pit aroma and grain aroma. The entry is noticeably sweet, the body is relatively light and the finish is short. At 42% ABV the alcohol is moderate and suits larger sips with food; "easy to drink" does not mean "high quality", and a solid-liquid product usually has less flavour complexity than a solid-state product at the same price.

For Niulanshan Chenniang, taste in a small glass: smell the resting spirit first, then swirl gently and smell again. Let the liquid stay in the mouth and follow the order of sweet, sour, bitter and umami, along with body weight. Ice or water changes the structure markedly and is not suitable for judging the original style.

## History and market

The state-run Niulanshan distillery was founded in 1952 and gradually built a product system centred on er-guotou; Niulanshan Chenniang took on the strong-aroma, mass-price distribution task. The product has wide coverage in catering and supermarkets and is one of the representative open-bottle spirits in the market.

Prices move with channel, region and supply and demand, and this page gives no figures. Treating sales volume as proof of quality, or "chenniang" as age, are common misreadings. It is better assessed as an everyday spirit than measured against the standards of a high-end banquet liquor.

## Buying and storage

Buy through regular channels and check the anti-counterfeit mark, producer and batch. Distinguish the brand's different lines: er-guotou is light aroma, Chenniang is strong aroma, and their standards differ. For a full case, also inspect the seal and storage conditions.

Unopened baijiu should be kept upright, away from light, in a cool place without strong odours. Once opened, drink it reasonably soon; prolonged exposure to air costs aroma. Solid-liquid products are not suited to long collecting, and the year of purchase does not equal a quality gain.

## Common misconceptions

First, treating "chenniang" as age. It is a product name.
Second, treating "Bai Niuer" as an official product name. It is a market nickname.
Third, treating solid-liquid baijiu as solid-state baijiu. They are different process categories.
Fourth, treating Niulanshan only as an er-guotou brand. Chenniang follows the strong-aroma route.
Fifth, judging authenticity by price or packaging. Anti-counterfeit marks, channels and labels are more reliable.

[Niulanshan](ref:brand) explains style; [Baijiu](ref:baijiu) adds region and vintage. Put Niulanshan Chenniang between them before deciding what it is.
