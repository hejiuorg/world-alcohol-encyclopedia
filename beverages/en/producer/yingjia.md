# Anhui Yingjia Distillery Co., Ltd.

Anhui Yingjia Distillery Co., Ltd. is a strong-aroma baijiu producer in Huoshan, Anhui. The joint-stock company was founded on 28 November 2003 and listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange on 28 May 2015, the 16th listed company in the baijiu industry. Its core brand is Yingjia Gongjiu, publicly positioned around ecological brewing.![Anhui Yingjia Distillery Co., Ltd. category image](/media/photos/drink-style-light-aroma-baijiu-1c3af236.jpg)

## Site and region

Huoshan lies in western Anhui, at the northern foot of the Dabie Mountains, with a humid subtropical monsoon climate, high vegetation cover and relatively good water conditions. The company publicly positions itself around ecological brewing, stressing the effect of regional environment, water and raw materials on the liquid.

Regional conditions affect raw-material quality and the microbial environment but do not determine quality by themselves. Strong-aroma flavour also depends on the pits, qu, fermentation cycle, distillation and storage. The raw materials and origin of a specific product should be taken from the label.

A site in Huoshan does not mean every product uses the same workshop or the same batch of base liquor. Product lines differ in raw materials, storage time and blending, and public company material and the label are the basis for judging a specific product.

## Craft and style

The typical strong-aroma route is: multi-grain blending, qu making, solid-state fermentation in mud pits, continued use of the fermented grain, basket distillation, fractional collection, ceramic-jar storage and blending. The maturity of the pits, the quality of the qu, the fermentation cycle and storage time all affect aroma and mouthfeel.

"Ecological brewing" is the company's public positioning, stressing regional ecology, raw materials and the production environment. Ecological conditions describe the company's resource base but cannot replace specific process parameters and label information.

For Anhui Yingjia Distillery Co., Ltd., a process description states a style route only and cannot replace a judgement about a specific product. Products at different prices and in different series within the same company may differ in base-liquor grade, storage time and blending ratio.

Strong-aroma practice is often summarised as the five-pot (laowuzeng) method: mash is removed from the pits in layers and mixed with new grain and qu; it is distilled pot by pot, with spirit collected pot by pot, and grain steaming and spirit distillation alternate in the same set of pots; part of the spent mash returns to the pit as continued mash, forming a continuously used mash system. Some producers also use two-round bottom fermentation, letting bottom-pit mash ferment across two rounds to build esters and pit aroma. Yingjia's public material stresses its ecological production area, but pit age, qu recipe, fermentation cycle and cutting standards belong to the company's process scope, and product labels and company disclosures govern.

## History

On 28 November 2003 Anhui Yingjia Distillery Co., Ltd. was founded, registered in Foziling town, Huoshan county, Anhui. On 28 May 2015 the company listed on the main board of the Shanghai Stock Exchange, becoming the 16th listed company in the baijiu industry. After listing, it expanded its product line around Yingjia Gongjiu and Guobin cave-storage series.

These points describe the shift from founding to listing. A listing date is not a plant-founding date and not the origin year of the brand; when discussing brand history, keep corporate history, brand narrative and product iterations apart.
